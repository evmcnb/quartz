Where too much cortisol is present. Dynamic suppression test needed - dexamethasoneshould reduce ACTH and cortisol. Classic features - moon face central shoulders, purple striae

#### Disease

Pituitary tumour releasing ACTH causes increased cortisol release

#### Syndrome

* Any other cause - steroids, adrenal cortex tumour, ectopic tumour eg small cell lung carcinoma
