Use the standard calculated osmolality formula to estimate it from the lab values:  

$$
\text{Calculated\ Osmolality}=(2\times [\text{Na}^{+}])+[\text{Glucose}]+[\text{Urea}]
$$

Useful for [[Full/Diabetes Mellitus#Complications/red Flags#Hyperosmolar hyperglycaemic state|HHS]] diagnosis which has a raised serum osmolality (> 320 mosmol/kg)
