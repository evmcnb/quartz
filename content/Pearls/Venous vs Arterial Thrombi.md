| **Feature**                | **Venous Thrombus**                                                | **Arterial Thrombus**                                                             |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Common Name**            | "Red Clot"                                                         | "White Clot"                                                                      |
| **Primary Composition**    | Mostly red blood cells trapped in a heavy fibrin mesh.             | Mostly aggregated platelets held together by a lighter fibrin mesh.               |
| **Environment**            | Low blood flow and low shear stress.                               | High blood flow and high shear stress.                                            |
| **Primary Trigger**        | Blood stasis (slow flow) and hypercoagulability (Virchow's Triad). | Endothelial injury, typically from a ruptured atherosclerotic plaque.             |
| **Formation Site**         | Deep veins (often starting near intact valves in the legs).        | Arteries (often at sites of narrowing or plaque buildup).                         |
| **Common Clinical Events** | Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT), Pulmonary Embolism (PE).               | Myocardial Infarction (Heart Attack), Ischemic Stroke, Peripheral Artery Disease. |
| **Medications**            | DOACs, heparin, warfarin                                           | aspirin, clopidogrel, ticagrelor                                                  |
| **Emergency Medication**   | Heparins                                                           | Thrombolytics: Alteplase (tPA), Tenecteplase                                      |
