**All post-tonsillectomy haemorrhages should be assessed by ENT**

Secondary haemorrhage after a tonsillectomy is that which occurs over 24 hours post-surgery. It is potentially life-threatening. All patients should be managed by ENT in the hospital. Children may swallow the blood making bleeding less noticeable and causing difficulties when trying to quantify blood loss.
