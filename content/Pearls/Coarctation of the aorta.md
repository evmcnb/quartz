A congenital narrowing of the descending aorta.

- More common in males 
- DESPITE an association with [[Turner's syndrome]]

Also associated with: 
- Bicuspid aortic valve
- Berry aneurysms 
- Neurofibromatosis

Features: 
- Radio-femoral delay
- Mid systolic murmur - maximal over the back
- Infancy: heart failure
- Adult: hypertension, notching of the inferior border of the ribs (due to collateral vessels)
- Apical click from the aortic valve
