Can be relative, primary (polycythaemia rubra vera) or secondary


Relative cause:
- Dehydration
- Stress: Gaisbock syndrome

Primary  
- polycythaemia rubra vera (blood cancer). JAK2 mutation. Aspirin (reduce thrombosis), venesection

Secondary:
- [[Full/COPD|COPD]]
- Altitude
- [[Full/Obstructive Sleep Apnoea|Obstructive Sleep Apnoea]]
- Excessive erythropoietin e.g. [[Pearls/Fibroids]]
