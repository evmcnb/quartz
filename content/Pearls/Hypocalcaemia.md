* EDTA contamination of the tube
* Vitamin D deficiency
* Hypoparathyroidism
  + Primary
  + Hypomagnesaemia
  + Pseudo-
* Hyperphosphataemia

Signs and symptoms

* **C**onvulsions
* **R**eflexes hyperactive
* **A**rrythmias - isolated QTc elongation
* **M**uscle spasms
* **P**ositive signs - Chvostek/Trousseau
* **S**ensation changes

Management

* Mild-moderate: oral calcium supplementation
* Severe: intravenous calcium gluconate, 10ml of 10% solution over 10 minutes. Find cause of hypo
