***Always treat B12 deficiency first -***giving patients folic acid when they have a B12 deficiency can lead to **subacute combined degeneration of the cord**, (demyelination in the spinal cord with severe neurological complications).

B12 deficiency causes ***macrocytic [[Pearls/Anaemia|Anaemia]]*** - DNA synthesis interference so produces large immature RBCs.

* Pernicious [[Pearls/Anaemia|Anaemia]] (autoimmune condition - antibodies produced against intrinsic factor)
* Insufficient dietary B12 - particularly vegan diet
* Medications that reduce B12 absorption (e.g. PPIs, [[Pearls/Metformin|Metformin]])

### Neuropathy

* **[[Full/peripheral neuropathy]]**
* Loss of vibration
* Loss of proprioception
* Visual changes
* Mood changes
