A sudden unexplained infant under 1 year of age. Sometimes referred to as cot death. It most often occurs within the first 6 months of life

**Risk factors**
- Prematurity
- Low birth weight
- Young maternal age

Breastfeeding is protective 

### Minimising the risk

- Having the baby sleep in their own cot
- Sleeping on their back
- Keep their head uncovered
- Place feet at the foot of the bed to prevent them from sliding down and under the blanket 
- Keep the cot clear of toys and loose blankets
- Avoid smoking or handling the baby after smoking
- Avoid co-sleeping on a sofa or chair

Using a **_dummy_** **_during sleep_** may lower the risk. But breastfeeding should be established before this.
