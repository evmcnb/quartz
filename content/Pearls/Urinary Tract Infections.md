## Uncomplicated

* Non pregnant
* Female
* Normal urinary tract and kidney function
* 3 day course of antibiotics - nitrofurantoin (avoid with eGFR <45), [[Pearls/Trimethoprim|Trimethoprim]] (higher resistance rates)

## Complicated

* Pregnant
* Male
* 7 day course of antibiotics

Asymptomatic bacteriuria in catheterised patients do not require treatment
