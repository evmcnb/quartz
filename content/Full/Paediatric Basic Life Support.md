The following principles apply to **infants and children** (not neonates):  
- age definitions:
    - an **infant** is a child under 1 year of age
    - a **child** is aged 1 year until the onset of puberty

## Danger

- Ensure safety of the environment

## Response

- Check responsiveness of patient
- Look, listen, feel - no more than 10 seconds
- If unsure or absent signs of life commence CPR

## Shout for help

## CPR

- Compression:ventilation ratio 15:2 for healthcare professionals
- Chest compression rate: 100-120 bpm
- Compression depth: depress the lower half of the sternum by at least 1/3 of the AP diameter of the chest 

In children: compress the lower half of the sternum using one or two hands as appropriate for the size of the child

In infants: two thumb encircling technique. If unable, use two fingers on the lower half of the sternum 

Pulse check only if no delay in CPR. Femoral or brachial. Carotids are not ideal given anatomy 


Additional key points:  
- minimise interruptions to chest compressions
- ensure full chest recoil between compressions
- change the rescuer performing compressions every 2 minutes if possible
- continue CPR until the child shows signs of life, advanced help arrives, or the rescuer is exhausted
- attach and use an AED as soon as available, using paediatric pads/settings if appropriate

Pulse check out of the paeds algorithm - up to individual
