**_Oral antihistamines_** are taken prior to exposure to reduce allergic symptoms:
- **_Sedating_** antihistamines include **_chlorphenamine_** (Piriton) and **_promethazine_**. First generation H1 antagonists have anticholinergic properties. Can cause acute [[Full/Delirium|Delirium]] 
- **_Non-sedating_** antihistamines include **_cetirizine_**, **_loratadine_** and **_fexofenadine_**. They do not cross the blood brain barrier so do not have sedating effects
