#### Positive Symptoms
something there in the presentation that shouldn't be

- Delusions - thoughts which are incorrect
- Hallucinations - incorrect sensory information i.e. voices, visual disturbances
- Formal thought disorders - structure of thinking

#### Negative Symptoms
lack of something that should be there

- Apathy
- Anhedonia
- blunted affect
- Asocial 

> [!warning] Diagnostic Criteria
> At least 1 of:
> - Thought echo / insertion / withdrawal / broadcast
> - Delusions of control / passivity / delusional perception
> - Auditory hallucinations giving running commentary or 3rd person
> - Bizarre delusions (culturally inappropriate)
>   
> Or at least 2 of:
> - Persistent hallucinations with delusions
> - Neologisms, breaks in train of thought resulting in incoherence
> - Catatonic behaviour
> - Negative symptoms
>   
>   Present most of the time for at least a month
