Alcohol is a depressant, stimulating GABA receptors. Also inhibits glutamate receptors (NMDA) causing further relaxing effect.

Long term alcohol use results in the GABA system becoming down-regulated and the glutamate system becoming up-regulated. 


> [!info] How to calculate a unit of alcohol
> $$
> \frac{\text{Volume (mL)} \times \text{Alcohol Content (\%)}}{1000}
> $$
> 
> - Not more than 14 units per week
> - Spread evenly over 3 or more days
> - Not more than 5 units in a single day


**Binge drinking** is defined as a single session involving more than 6-8 units of alcohol (different for men and women)

### Blood test results

Chornic alcohol excess changes blood tests:
- Raised MCV
- Raised ALT and AST
- **AST:ALT ratio above 1.5** suggests alcohol related liver disease
- Raised gGT
