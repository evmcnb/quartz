Patients with [[Pearls/Cirrhosis|Cirrhosis]] are at risk of developing [[Portal hypertension]]. Oesophageal varices are a high risk complication due to rupture and uncontrolled bleeding

### Acute management

- **ABCDE** - Blood transfusion may be required
- Correct clotting (due to liver damage): FFP, vitamin K, platelet transfusion may be required
- terlipressin: acts on V1 receptors → splanchnic vasoconstriction → ↓ portal blood flow and pressure → ↓ variceal pressure
- prophylactic IV antibiotics - the only thing shown to reduce mortality 
- Endoscopy: band ligation superior to sclerotherapy
- Sengstaken-Blakemore tube if uncontrolled haemorrhage
- Transjugular Intrahepatic Portosystemic Shunt (TIPSS) if above measures fail - can be complicated by [[Hepatic encephalopathy]] later


### Prophylaxis

- Non-selective beta-blockers - carvedilol (compensated cirrhosis), propranolol (decompensated)
- Endoscopic variceal band ligation
