## Saliva

* pH 6.2-7.6, hypotonic

**Submandibular gland** - mixed serum + mucous, lysosomes, more background supply

**Parotid gland** - largest, main amylase, CN IX innervation

**Sublingual** - similar to submandibular, doesn't produce

Sympathetic nervous → mucoid saliva

Parasympathetic → watery solution

## Swallowing:

1. Voluntary phase - tongue up
2. Pharyngeal phase - pharynx wall contracts and epiglottis pushed closed
3. Upper oesophageal phase - upper oesophageal sphincter opens
4. Lower oesophageal phase - bolus descends by peristatic activity

## Taste buds:

Ion channel based:

* salt: \(Na^+\xrightarrow[]{\text{ENaC}} \text{taste cell}\)
* sour \(H^+\xrightarrow[]{\text{TRPP}} \text{taste cell}\)
* sweet, umami, bitter - GPCR based, more complex
