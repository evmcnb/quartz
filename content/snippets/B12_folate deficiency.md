***Always treat B12 deficiency first -***giving patients folic acid when they have a B12 deficiency can lead to **subacute combined degeneration of the cord**, (demyelination in the spinal cord with severe neurological complications).

B12 deficiency causes ***macrocytic anaemia*** - DNA synthesis interference so produces large immature RBCs.

* Pernicious anaemia (autoimmune condition - antibodies produced against intrinsic factor)
* Insufficient dietary B12 - particularly vegan diet
* Medications that reduce B12 absorption (e.g. PPIs, metformin)

### Neuropathy

* **Peripheral neuropathy**
* Loss of vibration
* Loss of proprioception
* Visual changes
* Mood changes
