Nephritis refers to inflammation in the nephrons causing [[Full/Nephritic syndrome|Nephritic syndrome]]

Two most common causes are:
- Post-streptococcal glomerulonephritis
- IgA nephropathy (Berger's disease) - commonest cause of glomerulonephritis 

### Post-Streptococcal Glomerulonephritis

Occurs **1-3 weeks** following a group A strep infection (e.g. tonsillitis caused by Strep pyogenes)
- Immune complexses made of antigens and antibodies get stuck in the glomeruli causing inflammation and an [[Full/Acute Kidney Injury|Acute Kidney Injury]]
- Management is supportive - most patients make a full recovery
- Complications such as hypertension and oedema can be treated with anti-hypertensives and diuretics 

> [!success] Typical presentation
> A 5-12 year old with a history of **_tonsillitis_** and presenting with **_oedema_**, **_haematuria_** and **_high blood pressure_**.****

### IgA Nephropathy
aka Berger's disease

Involves transient IgA deposits in the nephrons causing inflammation. 
- Usually presents in teenagers or young adults (15-35) with **macroscopic haematuria** following an **URTI*** (1-2 weeks ago)
- Associated with [[Full/Alcohol-related Liver Disease|Alcohol-related Liver Disease]], [[Full/Coeliac Disease|Coeliac Disease]], [[Pearls/Henoch-Schonlein purpura]]
- Supportive treatment with mild disease
- Persistent proteinuria treated with ACEis
- Active disease (falling eGFR treated with corticosteroids)
- 25% progress to end stage renal failure
