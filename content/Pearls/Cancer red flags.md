## Lung

* Symptomatic of SVC obstruction, stridor
* Haemoptysis
* Persistent/recurrent chest infection
* Finger [[Pearls/clubbing]]
* Supraclavicular/cervical lymphadenopathy
* Thrombocytosis
* Two of: cough, fatigue, SOB, chest pain, weight loss, ↓appetite, smoker, asbestos

## Upper GI

* Dysphagia with weight loss and upper abdo pain/reflux/dyspepsia
* [[Pearls/Jaundice|Jaundice]] or upper abdo mass
* Diarrhoea plus back pain, abdo pain, nausea, constipation, new-onset diabetes
* Haematemesis

## Lower GI

* Faecal occult blood
* Abdo pain plus weight loss
* Rectal bleeding
* Iron deficiency [[Pearls/Anaemia|Anaemia]]
* Change in bowel habit
* Rectal/abdo mass

## Gynaecological

* Asites
* Pelvic mass
* Post-menopausal bleeding

## Breast

* Unexplained breast lump
* Changes to one nipple
* Axillary lump

## Urology

* Irregular prostate on PR
* Deranged PSA
* Macroscopic haematuria
* Non-painful enlargement or change in shape/texture of testicle

## CNS

* Sub-acute loss of central neurological function
* Weight loss, ↓appetite
* DVT
