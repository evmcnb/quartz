More common in boys until 3 months of age.

> [!warning] NICE guidelines for checking urine sample in a child  
> - if there are any symptoms or signs suggestive or a UTI
> - with unexplained fever of 38°C or higher (test urine after 24 hours at the latest)
> - with an alternative site of infection but who remain unwell (consider urine test after 24 hours at the latest)

Clean catch method preferred.


## Management

- Infants <3 months should be seen by a pediatrician 
- Upper UTI $\rightarrow$ hospital admission for ? IV abx (cephalosporin or co-amoxiclav)
- Lower UTI $\rightarrow$ oral abx for 3 days according to local guidelines 

> [!warning] NICE definitions of atypical and recurrent UTIs
> **Atypical UTI includes:**
> 
> - Seriously ill 
> - Poor urine flow
> - Abdominal or bladder mass
> - Raised creatinine
> - Septicaemia
> - Failure to respond to treatment with suitable antibiotics within 48 hours
> - Infection with non-_E. coli_ organisms
> 
> **Recurrent UTI:**
> 
> - Two or more episodes of UTI with acute upper UTI (acute pyelonephritis), or
> - One episode of UTI with acute upper UTI plus 1 or more episodes of UTI with lower UTI (cystitis), or
> - Three or more episodes of UTI with lower UTI

### Imaging in UTI

- Send those with atypical first-time UTI for ultrasound
- Send those <6 months for ultrasound within 6 weeks of UTI
- Those <6 months with atypical or recurrent UTI get a MCUG (give prophylactic antibiotics orally for 3 days with the MCUG on the second day)
