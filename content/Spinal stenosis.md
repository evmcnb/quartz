Most common type: lumbar spinal stenosis due to degenerative changes. Can also be due to anything that causes constriction (disc herniation, tumour, congenital canal narrowing)

- Lower back pain
- Bilateral leg pain
- Symptoms mimicking claudication (neurogenic claudication)

The pain is position dependant
- Worse with standing and walking (lumbar extension)
- Relieved by sitting/lumbar flexion
- Easier to walk uphill than downhill

MRI for diagnosis

Can initially manage conservatively but **surgical decompression** (laminectomy) for severe/refractory cases
