Twisting of the spermatic cord with rotation around the testicle. **Urological emergency**

Typical patient is teenage boy - but can occur at any age

## Presentation

* Often triggered by activity - sport. Ask what patient was doing at time of pain
* Acute rapid onset unilateral testicular pain
* With abdo pain and vomiting
* Sometimes abdo pain is the only symptom
* Firm swollen testicle in abnormal position (elevated, horizontal, rotated)
* Absent cremasteric reflex


> [!warning] 
> The moment testicular torsion is suspected surgical exploration is warranted

## Management


A scrotal USS can confirm the diagnosis and can show the **whirlpool sign**- spiral appearance to the spermatic cord and blood vessels. Any delay in treatment is not recommended however.




* Nil by mouth in preparation for surgery
* Analgesia
* Senior urology assessment
* Surgical exploration of scrotum
* Orchiopexy or orchidectomy
