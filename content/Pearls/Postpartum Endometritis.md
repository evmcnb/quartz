Inflammation usually caused by an infection. Occurs more commonly after a [[Pearls/Caesarean Section|Caesarean Section]] than a vaginal delivery. 

- Foul smelling discharge or lochia
- Bleeding that gets heavier or does not improve with time
- Lower abdominal or pelvic pain
- Fever
- [[Full/Sepsis|Sepsis]]

Patients presenting with milder symptoms and no signs of sepsis can be treated in the community with oral broad-spectrum (e.g. co-amoxiclav) depending on risk of chlamydia and gonorrhoea. 

- Vaginal swabs 
- Urine MC&S
- Consider ultrasound to rule out retained products of conception
