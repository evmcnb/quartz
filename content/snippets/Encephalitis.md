Inflammation of the brain usually viral - HSV, West Nile, enterovirus

* Fever
* Headache
* AMS
* Seizures
* Focal neurological deficits

Do a neuro exam, CSF?, MRI

Antivirals - for viral

Corticosteroids - for immune-mediated
