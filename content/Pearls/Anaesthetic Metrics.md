#### Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP)

$$
MAP = DBP + \frac{1}{3}(SBP - DBP)
$$

Target of >60-65 mmHg
#### Minimum Alveolar Concentration (MAC)

The percentage concentration of an inhaled anaesthetic in the alveoli needed to prevent movement in 50% of patients in response to a surgical stimulus. Can be weight adjusted

1 MAC of:
- Nitrous oxide is 104% 
- Sevoflurane 2%
- Isoflurane 1.15%
- Desflurane 6%
