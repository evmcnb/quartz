Typically complication of bacterial [[Full/Tonsillitis & Pharyngitis|Tonsillitis & Pharyngitis]] (group A strep)
- Severe throat pain which lateralises
- **Deviation of the uvula to the unaffected side**
- Trismus (difficulty opening the mouth)
- Reduced neck mobility


![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260131164935.png|500]]


**Require an urgent ENT review**
- Needle aspiration/incision & drainage + IV antibiotics $\pm$ steroids
- Tonsillectomy should be considered to prevent recurrence
