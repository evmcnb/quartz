- Single cytopenia - peripheral process
- Bicytopenia - peripheral but could be bone marrow
- Pancytopenia - likely bone marrow


### Cause

Need to think what can kill cells or prevent new cells

**4S rule**
- Systemic ([[full/Systemic Lupus Erythematosus]], tickborne illness)
- Substances (chemo, heavy metals, antibiotics (linezolid), alcohol, LACK of substances folate, B12 etc)
- Space occupying disease - in the bone (granulomatous diseases, fungal infections, [[Tuberculosis]], [[Lymphoma]])
- Stem cell disorders ([[Differentiating features of leukaemia|leukaemia]], [[Myelodysplastic syndromes]], aplastic anaemia)
