[[MLA/Osteoarthritis]] **LOSS -**irregular [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] remodelling:

1. **L**oss of joint space
2. **O**steophyte formation (bony spurs protrude out)
3. **S**clerosis (underlying [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] becomes hard)
4. **S**ubchondrial cyst

[[Pearls/Rheumatoid Arthritis|Rheumatoid Arthritis]] **LESS**- immune reaction:

* **L**oss of joint space
* **E**rosions
* **S**oft tissue swelling
* **S**oft bones (osteopaenia)
