Inflammatory condition that typically affects the genitalia in elderly females. Increased risk of vulval cancer

Leads to atrophy of the epidermis with white plaques forming. 

Features:
- white patches that may scar
- Itch
- Pain during intercourse or urination

Usually clinical diagnosis but a biopsy may be performed if atypical features present.

Managed with very potent topical steroids (clobetasol propionate) and emollients
