Affects 1 in 7 couples

But 85% who have regular sex will conceive within 1 year, 92% in 2 years

Causes  
- male factor 30%
- unexplained 20%
- ovulation failure 20%
- tubal damage 15%
- other causes 15%

Basic investigations
- Semen analysis
- Serum progesterone on **day 21**
