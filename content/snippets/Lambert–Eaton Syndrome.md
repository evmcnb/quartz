Lambert–Eaton myasthenic syndrome is a rare autoimmune disorder characterised by muscle weakness of the limbs. Around 60% of those with LEMS have an underlying malignancy, most commonly **small-cell lung cancer**; it is therefore regarded as a **paraneoplastic syndrome**.
