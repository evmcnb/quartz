Classified by the site of the problem - pre-hepatic, hepatocellular or cholestatic/obstructive

Or by the type of circulating bilirubin - conjugated or unconjugated

![](https://nosos-static.s3.amazonaws.com/media/uploads/2024/03/09/image.png)

## Unconjugated

* Water insoluble - does not enter urine
* Overproduction: haemolysis (malaria/DIC)
* Impaired hepatic uptake - drugs ([[Snippets/Paracetamol|Paracetamol]], rifampicin), ischaemic [[Hepatitis]]

## Conjugated

* Water soluble - excreted in urine making it darker
* Failure of conjugated bilirubin to be excreted by the gall bladder into the intestines can cause pale stools
