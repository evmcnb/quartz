Upper limit is 24 weeks gestation - these limits do not apply in cases where it is necessary to save the life of the woman, there is evidence of extreme fetal abnormality, or there is risk of serious physical or mental injury to the woman.

- 2 registered medical practitioners must sign a legal document (in an emergency only 1 is needed)
- Only a registered medical practitioner can perform an abortion which must be in an NHS hosptal or licensed premise

[[Pearls/Rhesus negative pregnancy|Anti-D prophylaxis]] should be given to rhesus D negative women having an abortion **after 10 weeks gestation**



> [!warning] NICE Choice of Procedure
> NICE recommend that women are offered a choice between medical or surgical abortion up to 24 weeks gestation
> - After 9 weeks medical abortions become less common
> - Before 10 weeks medical abortions are usually done at home

#### Medical management

Often referred to mimicking a miscarriage - 
- Mifepristone & misoprostol 48 hours later - same management as [[Miscarriage#Medical Management|medical miscarriage]]
- Pregnancy test required in 3 weeks also - need to detect hCG level rather than just +ve or -ve 

#### Surgical management

Transcervical procedures - vacuum aspiration & dilation and evacuation
- cervical priming with misoprostol +/- mifepristone is used before procedures
- Offerred LA alone, conscious sedation with LA, deep sedation or GA
- Following a surgical abortion an intrauterine contraceptive can be inserted immediately
