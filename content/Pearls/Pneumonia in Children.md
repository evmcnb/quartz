*Strep. pneumoniae* is the most likely cause in children

**Management** (5 days)
- Amoxicillin is first-line for all children with pneumonia
- Macrolides may be added if no response to therapy or penicillin allergic (erythromycin or clarithromycin). Doxycycline is not recommended for children under 12 due to potential for permanent teeth staining
- Macrolides should be used if mycoplasma or chlamydia is suspected
- If associated with influenza - co-amoxiclav is recommended
