Pretty self explanatory for the location. Caused by coxsackie A16 and enterovirus 71. **Very contagious** and typically occurs in outbreaks at nursey

- Sore throat
- Fever
- Oral ulcers
- Followed by vesicles on the palms and soles of feet

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260204113335.png|500]]

Can impair oral intake due to painful ulcers, predisposing to dehydration and pre-renal acute kidney injury

Management:
- Symptomatic treatment only
- Reassurance 
- Children do not need to be excluded from school
