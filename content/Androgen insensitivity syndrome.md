X-linked recessive condition. Affected individuals are usually genotypically male (46XY) but present with a spectrum of phenotypes depending on the degree of receptor dysfunction

#### Complete AIS
- Normal female external genitalia 
- Short blind-ending vagina
- Absent uterus and fallopian tubes 
- Primary amenorrhoea
- Little or no axillary and pubic hair
- Breast development at puberty
- Inguinal/abdominal testes

#### Partial AIS
- Variable external genitalia
- Reduced but not absent pubic hair
- Variable breast development
- Testes often undescended or ectopic


Need a karyotype/chromosomal analysis to confirm the genotype. 
- Normal/high testosterone (male reference rage)


### Management
- MDT & psychological support
- CAIS usually raised as female, PAIS sex assignment requires specialist input
- Bilateral orchidectomy is recommended - risk of [[Full/Testicular Cancer|Testicular Cancer]] due to [[Full/Cryptorchidism|Cryptorchidism]]
- Oestrogen therapy afterwards
