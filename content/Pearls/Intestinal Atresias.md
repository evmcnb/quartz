When part of the intestinal track does not develop fully. Typically:
- Oesophageal atresia
- [[Pearls/Biliary Atresia|Biliary Atresia]]
- Duodenal atresia - more common in [[Pearls/Down's syndrome|Down's syndrome]]

All will probably present with vomiting to some extent. The features of the vomit + other symptoms will help work it out.
