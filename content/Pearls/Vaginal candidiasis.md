Very common condition. 
- 80% Candida albicans
- 20% other species

Most have no risk factors. However some can make it more likely:
- [[Full/Diabetes Mellitus|Diabetes Mellitus]]
- Anti-biotics/steroids
- Pregnancy
- Immunosuppresion

**Features:**
- 'Cottage cheese', non-offensive discharge. pH <4.5
- Vulvitis - superficial dyspareunia, dysuria
- Itch
- Vulval erythema, fissuring, satellite lesions may be seen 

Can be a **clinical diagnosis** - high vaginal swab is not routinely indicated.

**Management**:
- Avoid local irritants - tight fitting clothes, scented products etc
- Oral fluconazole 150mg single dose
- Clotrimazole 500mg intravaginal pessary as a single dose if cannot take orally
- If there are vulval symptoms, consider adding topical imidazole

If pregnant, then only local treatments may be used. 


#### Recurrent

BASHH define it as 4 or more episodes per year
- Consider a blood glucose test to exclude diabetes
- Exclude differential diagnoses such as [[Pearls/Lichen sclerosus]]
- Consider the use of induction-maintenance regime
	- Induction: oral fluconazole every 3 days for 3 doses
	- Maintenance: oral fluconazole weekly for 6 months
