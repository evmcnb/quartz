|**Concomitant**|**Paralytic**|
|---|---|
|Due to imbalance in extraocular muscles  <br>Convergent is more common than divergent|Due to paralysis of extraocular muscles|

**Cover test** is used to identify the nature of the squint: 
- Ask the child to focus on an object
- Cover one eye
- Observe movement
- Cover other eye and repeat test

**Management**

Refer to secondary care

The visual field are still developing up until the age of 8 so can be treated before then. 

Occlusive patch can be used to cover the good eye to force the weaker one to develop
