An infection of the chorioamniotic membranes and amniotic fluid


Leading causes of [[Full/Sepsis|Sepsis]] in [[Full/Pregnancy|Pregnancy]] along with [[Full/Urinary Tract Infection|Urinary Tract Infection]]

## Causes/Factors

A wide variety of bacteria including:
- Gram +ve
- Gram -ve
- Anaerobes

## Presentation

All patients admitted to maternity inpatient units will have monitoring on a MEOWS (Maternity Early Obstetric Warning System)

Specific symptoms:
- Abdominal pain
- Uterine tenderness
- Vaginal discharge

Non specific signs: 
- Fever
- Tachycardia
- Raised RR (often an early sign)
- Reduced SpO2
- Low BP
- Altered consciousness
- Reduced UO 
- Raised WBC on FBC
- Evidence of fetal compromise on [[Full/Cardiotocography|CTG]]

## Investigations

- FBC - WBC and neutrophils
- U&E - kidney function, check for [[Full/Acute Kidney Injury|Acute Kidney Injury]]
- LFT - assess liver function and possible source of infection ([[Full/Cholecystitis|Cholecystitis]])
- CRP
- Clotting - DIC
- Blood cultures
- Blood gas to assess lactate, pH, glucose

Additional investigations for source identification: 
- Urine dip/culture
- High vaginal swab

## Management

Senior involvement early - recognition and management is essential: 

![[Full/Sepsis#BUFALO|Sepsis]]

**Continuous** maternal and fetal monitoring is required. Early delivery may be indicated

Follow local guidelines when choosing antibiotics - very broad spectrum needed:
- Tazocin + gentamicin
- Amoxicillin + clindamycin + gentamicin
