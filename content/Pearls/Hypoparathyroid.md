Underproduction of parathyroid hormone despite low calcium. Much rarer than hyperparathyroidism

## Primary

Causes

* DiGeorge syndrome
* Autosomal dominant hypoparathyoidism
* Pseudohypoparathyroidism Type 1A - bones + kidney don't respond to parathyroid hormones - defective parathyroid hormone receptor
* **Surgical damage to the gland after thyroidectomy**

Symptoms of hypocalcemia and [[Pearls/Hyperphosphatemia|Hyperphosphatemia]] ([[Pearls/Electrolyte imbalance|table]])
## Secondary

[[Full/Depression]] due to chronic [[Pearls/Hypocalcaemia|Hypocalcaemia]], would likely accompanied by signs of [[Pearls/Hypocalcaemia|Hypocalcaemia]]
