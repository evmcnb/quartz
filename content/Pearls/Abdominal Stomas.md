### Complications

Early
- Bleeding
- Ischaemic
- Retraction
- Peristomal skin irritation
- High-output stoma $\rightarrow$ dehydration, electrolyte loss
- Infection

Late complications (weeks–years)  
- Parastomal hernia – most common long-term
- Stenosis/stricture – poor effluent flow, obstruction
- Prolapse – bowel telescopes through stoma
- Chronic retraction – persistent, hard to fit appliances
- Fistula formation
- Psychological issues – body image, odour, social impact

  
Key points of stoma complications  
- Colostomy: prone to prolapse and hernia
- Ileostomy: risk of high output, electrolyte imbalance
- Urostomy: skin irritation from alkaline urine
- Management: stoma nurse support, appliance fitting, skin care; surgery if severe


## High-output Stoma History and Management

**History**
- What's normal for them? How often changing bag
- Consistency of output?
- Symptomatic? Is it disturbing them
- Eaten anything out of the ordinary?
- Any infections
- Blood/mucous/anything abnormal in the output? 
- Have they been eating and drinking okay?
- Passing urine?
- Medication review


Exam 
- Check stoma and output
- Check fluid status
- General abdo exam

Management
- Rehydration - oral or IV depending on severity
- Output MC&S - treat if infective cause identified
- C diff toxins/antigens
- Loperamide
- PPIs? Can reduce volume of gastric acid
