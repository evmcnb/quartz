Focal (partial) seizure involving face, drooling, and one sided limb twitching **before or after bedtime**
- Ages 4-12 
- No loss of consciousness 
- Common if sleep deprived

The seizure is initiated in the rolandic fissure (central sulcus thus centrotemporal spikes)

Good prognosis - **might not need treatment**. Depends on frequency and severity
