Danger - ensure safety of yourself and patient

Response -  patient unresponsive and not breathing properly (without a central pulse)

**Shout for help**

## BLS

A - head tilt (if **no spinal injury**) and chin lift/jaw thrust  

B - looking listen and feel for breathing for maximum 10 seconds =  

C - begin chest complressions at 100-120 bpm with 5-6cm depth with rebound. After 30 compressions give 2 rescue breaths. Do not interrupt compressions until intructed to do so by defib.

## ALS

**Call resuscitation team**

### Reversible causes of cardiac arrest

**4Hs**

* Hypoxia
* Hypovolaemia
* Hypo/[[Pearls/Hyperkalaemia|Hyperkalaemia]]/metabolic
* Hypothermia

**4Ts**

* Thrombosis
* Tension [[MLA/pneumothorax]]
* Tamponade
* Toxins
