Diagnostic criteria:

* High BM - >11 mmol/L
* Ketones - >3 mmol/L
* Acidosis  pH <7.3

Presentation:

* Deep laboured breathing
* Fruity smell of breath
* Metabolic acidosis with increased anion gap

Fluid bolus (± potassium) + fixed rate insulin infusion, 0.1 units/kg/hr
