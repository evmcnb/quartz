* Most common pancreatic cancer - usually in head of pancreas
* ↑ risk with chronic pancreatitis, DM, alcohol
* Hereditary with Lynch syndrome, FAMMM (p16), Her-2, BRCA1/2, Von Hippel-Lindau, SPINK1, Peutz-Jeghers
