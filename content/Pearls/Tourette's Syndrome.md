title: "Tourette's Syndrome"
date: 2023-09-27
date modified: 2025-04-23
---

Development of tics that are persistent for over a year. Tics are involuntary movements or sounds that the individual performs repetitively throughout the day. They become more prominent when the person is under pressure or excited. 
- The urge to perform the tic increases the more they supress it. 
- Often presents around 5
- Associated with [[Pearls/Obsessive compulsive disorder]] and [[Pearls/Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder]]


Usually tics will improve with time. More severe or troublesome tics should be referred to specialist
- Habit reversal training
- Exposure with response prevention 
- Medications in severe cases 

Co-morbid conditions should be treated first
