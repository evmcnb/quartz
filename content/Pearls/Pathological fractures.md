A fracture due to an underlying disease of the [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] - usually a tumour, [[Full/osteoporosis]] or Paget's disease of the [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]]. Occur with disproportionately little trauma. Common sites are **vertebral bodies and the femur**

The main cancers that metastasise to the [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]]

* Prostate
* Renal
* Thyroid
* Breast
* Lung
