***Always treat B12 deficiency first -***giving patients folic acid when they have a B12 deficiency can lead to **Subacute combined degeneration of the cord**, (demyelination in the spinal cord with severe neurological complications).

B12 deficiency causes ***macrocytic [[Pearls/Anaemia|Anaemia]]*** - DNA synthesis interference so produces large immature RBCs.

* Pernicious [[Pearls/Anaemia|Anaemia]] (autoimmune condition - antibodies produced against intrinsic factor)
* Insufficient dietary B12 - particularly vegan diet
* Medications that reduce B12 absorption (e.g. PPIs, [[Pearls/Metformin|Metformin]])

#### Neuropathy

* **[[Full/peripheral neuropathy]]**
* Loss of vibration
* Loss of proprioception
* Visual changes
* Mood changes

### Sub-acute combined degeneration of the cord

Due to vitamin B12 deficiency resulting in impairment of the dorsal columns, lateral corticospinal tracts and spinocerebellar tracts. Causes both **UMN and LMN signs**


Causes can be anything that limits B12 absorption or intake: 
- nitrous oxide
- pernicious anemia
- malabsorption ([[Full/Crohn's Disease|Crohn's Disease]], [[Full/Coeliac Disease|Coeliac Disease]], gastric bypass surgery)

#### Vitamin E Deficiency

Though incredibly rare, a severe deficiency in Vitamin E can lead to a similar pattern of spinal degeneration. Because Vitamin E is a fat-soluble vitamin, this is almost exclusively seen in individuals with severe, chronic fat-malabsorption syndromes (like cystic fibrosis or severe cholestatic liver disease).
