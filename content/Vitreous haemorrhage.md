One of the most common causes for painless vision loss. Variable degree, from floaters to complete vision loss. 

Once the bleeding stops, the blood is typically cleared from the vitreous at an approximate rate of 1% per day.

- [[Full/Diabetic eye disease|Diabetic eye disease]]
- [[Full/Vitreous detachment|Posterior Vitreous detachment]]
- Ocular trauma

Management
- urgent referral to ophthalmology
- manage underlying cause (e.g. optimise diabetes control, laser therapy for proliferative changes)
- Observe with regular ophthalmic exams assess resolution or complications like retinal detachment, guiding treatment decisions if spontaneous recovery occurs
- consider vitreoretinal surgery (e.g. pars plana vitrectomy) if the haemorrhage does not clear spontaneously
