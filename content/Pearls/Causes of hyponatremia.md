PPI TACO:

* PPIs
* Thiazides
* ACEi/ARBs
* [[Pearls/Carbamazepine|Carbamazepine]]
* Opioids


Other common drug causes of low sodium include: chlorpropramide, selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI) [[Full/Antidepressants|Antidepressants]], tricyclic antidepressants, [[Pearls/Lithium|Lithium]], MDMA/ecstasy, tramadol, haloperidol, vincristine, desmopressin, fluphenazine
