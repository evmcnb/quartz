Reactive arthritis is defined as an arthritis that develops following an infection (within 4 weeks of initial infection) where the organism cannot be recovered from the joint.

Symptoms generally last 4-6 months

Typically after salmonella or GU infection. Post-STI form much more common in men

Joint aspiration for MC&S and crystal examination for [[Full/gout|gout]] and [[Pearls/Pseudogout|Pseudogout]]

## Can't see, can't pee, can't climb up a tree
- Asymmetrical oligoarthritis of the lower limbs
- Urethritis
- Conjunctivitis

Management  
- symptomatic: analgesia, NSAIDS, intra-articular steroids
- sulfasalazine and methotrexate are sometimes used for persistent disease
- symptoms rarely last more than 12 months
