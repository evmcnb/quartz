Protozoan spread through sexual intercourse. Can increase the risk of: 
- Contracting [[Full/HIV|HIV]]
- [[Pearls/Bacterial vaginosis|Bacterial vaginosis]]
- [[Full/Cervical Cancer|Cervical Cancer]]
- [[Pearls/Pelvic inflammatory disease]]
- Pregnancy related complications - [[Pearls/Premature Babies|Premature Babies]]


**Presentation**:
- **Strawberry cervix**
- **pH > 4.5**
- Vaginal discharge: offensive, yellow/green frothy
- Vulvovaginitis
- Usually asymptomatic in men but may cause urethritis

NAATs gold standard

**Management**:
- Refer to GUM for, diagnosis, treatment and contact tracing
- Oral metronidazole for 5-7 days
- Or STAT 2g metronidazole
