### Antenatal

Pregnant women are screened for anaemia at:  
- the booking visit (often done at 8-10 weeks), and at
- 28 weeks

| Gestation              | Cut-off   |
| ---------------------- | --------- |
| First trimester        | < 110 g/L |
| Second/third trimester | < 105 g/L |

oral ferrous sulfate or ferrous fumarate
- treatment should be continued for 3 months after iron deficiency is corrected to allow iron stores to be replenished


### Post-partum

Common after delivery. Essential to optimise treatment of anaemia during pregnancy before delivery

**FBC** is checked the day after deliver if: 
- Postpartum haemorrhage over 500ml
- [[Pearls/Caesarean Section|Caesarean Section]]
- Antenatal anaemia
- Symptoms of anaemia 

| Haemoglobin | Treatment                             |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------- |
| <100 g/l    | Start ferrous sulfate                 |
| < 90 g/l    | Consider an iron infusion + oral iron |
| < 70 g/l    | Blood transfusion + oral iron         |
An iron infusion is considered in those who don't response to oral iron 


> [!danger] Active infection contraindication
> An active infection is a contraindication to an iron infusion. Many pathogens gain nutrition from iron so the infection would worsen. Need to wait until the infection is treated first
