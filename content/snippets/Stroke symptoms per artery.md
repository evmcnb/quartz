**PICA**- vertigo, vomiting dysphagia, ipsilateral facial pain & temperature loss, contralateral limb pain & temperature loss

**AICA** - vertigo, vomiting, ipsilateral facial paralysis & deafness

**ACA** - unilateral motor weakness, visual field defects, sensory loss, dysphasia, dysarthria dysphagia

**MCA** - commonly affected hemiparesis

**PCA** - coordination difficulties, cerebellar symptoms

* Right sided facial pain - right sided lesion
* Past pointing  - cerebellar infarct
