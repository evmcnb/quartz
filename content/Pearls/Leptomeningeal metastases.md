title: Leptomeningeal metastases
date: 2023-09-27
date modified: 2025-04-23
---
Form of metastatic cancer that spreads to some of the layers around the brain and spinal cord. 

Tends to occurs with cancers that spread to the CNS:
- Breast
- Lung
- Melanoma
- GI 

Causes more **wide spread multifocal** neurology where as [[Pearls/Brain Tumours|Brain Tumours]] more focal. 

No treatment
