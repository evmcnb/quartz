## Gynaecological Symptoms


> [!info] Summary of key gynaecological symptoms
> - Abdominal and pelvic pain - [[Full/Ectopic Pregnancy|Ectopic Pregnancy]], ruptured ovarian cysts, [[Pearls/Endometriosis|Endometriosis]], [[Pearls/Pelvic inflammatory disease|Pelvic inflammatory disease]], [[Pearls/Ovarian torsion|Ovarian torsion]]
> - Post-coital bleeding - always pathological. Causes include [[Pearls/Cervical ectropion|Cervical ectropion]], [[Full/Cervical Cancer|Cervical Cancer]], [[Pearls/Gonorrhoea|Gonorrhoea]], [[Pearls/Chlamydia|Chlamydia]] and [[Pearls/Atrophic vaginitis|Atrophic vaginitis]]
> - Intramenstrual bleeding: contraception changes (e.g. Mirena coil), ovulation, [[Pearls/Miscarriage|Miscarriage]], [[Pearls/Gonorrhoea|Gonorrhoea]], [[Pearls/Chlamydia|Chlamydia]], [[Pearls/Fibroids|Fibroids]], peri-menopause and malignancy 
> - [[Full/Menorrhagia|Menorrhagia]]
> - [[Pearls/Dysmenorrhoea]]
> - Post-menopausal bleeding - 10% [[Pearls/Endometrial cancer|Endometrial cancer]], most others [[Pearls/Atrophic vaginitis|Atrophic vaginitis]]
> - Abnormal vaginal discharge - [[Pearls/Vaginal candidiasis|Vaginal candidiasis]], [[Pearls/Bacterial vaginosis|Bacterial vaginosis]], [[Pearls/Chlamydia|Chlamydia]] and [[Pearls/Gonorrhoea|Gonorrhoea]]
> - Dyspareunia
> - Vulval skin changes and itching
> - Systemic symptoms


#### Vaginal discharge

Should ask about any **changes** to the following:
- Volume: "Have you noticed any change in the amount of vaginal discharge"
- Colour: "Have you noticed any change in the colour?"
- Consistency: "Have you noticed that your discharge has become more watery or thickened recently"
- Smell: "Have you noticed anything unusual in the smell of the discharge?"

#### Dyspareunia

Pain that occurs during sexual intercourse. Need to differentiate between superficial and deep dyspareunia:
- Superficial - pain at the external surface of the genitalia ([[Pearls/Atrophic vaginitis|Atrophic vaginitis]], skin changes or fissures at the vaginal introitus)
- Deep - pain deep within the pelvis ([[Pearls/Endometriosis|Endometriosis]], [[Pearls/Fibroids|Fibroids]] or infection)

Need to clarify the **duration, location and nature** of the pain 


#### Other symptoms

**Urinary symptoms** - frequency, urgency, and dysuria can be relevant to gynaecological problems

**Bowel symptoms** - change in bowel habit or pain during defecation can be associated with [[Pearls/Endometriosis|Endometriosis]]

**Fever** - may be associated with [[Pearls/Pelvic inflammatory disease|Pelvic inflammatory disease]]

**Fatigue** & **Unintentional weight loss** 

Abdominal distension


### Menstrual history

##### Duration

*"How long do your periods typically last"*
Avg. 5 days, more than 7 days is considered prolonged

##### Frequency

- *"How often do your periods occur"*
- *"Are they regular and predictable?"*

Avg. 28 day cycle - but significant variation ([[Pearls/Menstrual Cycle]])

##### Flow 

Just need to get a general idea of volume - frequency of changing sanitary products and if this is more or less than usual

Textbook definition is more than 80mls or having bleeding lasting long than 7 days


##### Menstrual pain

- *Have your recent periods been more painful than usual?*
- *Do these interfere with your day to day life?*


##### Last menstrual period

*"What date was the first day of your last period?"*

If the period is late, essential to consider pregnancy


##### Menarche/Menopause

If relevant to assess risk of lifetime oestrogen exposure. Couple this with quick obstetric history


## Contraception

Clarify the type of contraception currently used: 
- [[Pearls/Combined oral contraceptive pill|Combined oral contraceptive pill]]/patch
- Progesterone only pill
- Depot injection (progesterone)
- [[Pearls/Intrauterine contraceptive device|Intrauterine contraceptive device]]
- Barrier methods

Explore if this has changed and why


## Reproductive plans

Need to ask if they have children/trying for children/considering having children

Important for evaluating treatment options. 


## Past gynaecological history

Need to ask about the following: 
- Gynaecological conditions
- Gynaecological surgeries - abdominal/pelvic surgery, loop excision of the transitional zone
- Cervical screening - check attendance/vaccination against HPV 

## PMH

As normal. Relevant to gynaecological presentations:
- **Migraine with aura**: oestrogen containing medications (e.g. combined oral contraceptive) would be contraindicated.
- **Previous venous thromboembolism (VTE)**: oestrogen containing medications would be contraindicated.
- **Breast cancer (current or previous)**: use of oestrogen containing medications would be usually be contraindicated or require specialist input before being commenced.
- **Bleeding disorders** (e.g. Von Willebrand's) would be relevant if a patient presented with heavy vaginal bleeding.

## Obstetric history

**Current pregnancy**
- Gestation
- Symptoms associated
- Complications
- Recent scan results

**Previous pregnancies**
- Age of children
- Pre-mature/birth weight
- Mode of delivery
- Complications

## Drug history

As usual including **allergies**

Specific drugs include
- St John's Wort increases the metabolism of the COCP reducing its effectiveness.
- Antibiotics may cause secondary vaginal thrush.


## Family history

- *Any history of cancer in the family?* - screening for BRCA gene
- *Is there any history of bleeding disorders in the family* - menorrhagia can be the first symptom of [[Pearls/Von Willebrand Disease|Von Willebrand Disease]]
- *"Have any of your close family members had blood clots in the past"*

## Social History

Really quite important for overall picture of health

#### Social context

- Type of accommodation they live in
- Who else they live with and their personal support network
- What tasks they can do independently and what they need help with

#### Smoking

History including type and amount. [[Pearls/Combined oral contraceptive pill|Combined oral contraceptive pill]] contraindicated with smoking too much: 
- $>$ 40 a day, contraindicated
- If over 35 y/o and smoking more than 15 a day, contraindicated


#### Alcohol & recreational drugs

Ask frequency, type and volume


#### Diet and weight

Current weight most important risk factor-wise

#### Occupation 

What they currently do for work and if symptoms are preventing them from working
