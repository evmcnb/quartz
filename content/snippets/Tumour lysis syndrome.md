---
title: "Tumour lysis syndrome"
date created: 2024-02-26 16:12:05.171271+00
date modified: 2024-02-26 16:12:05.177061+00
tags: ['Oncology']
---



Chemotherapy for rapidly proliferating tumours (leukaemia, lymphoma, myeloma) leads to cell death add ↑urate, K+, phosphate and ↓calcium.

Risk of arrhythmia and renal failure

Prevent with hydration and uricolytics eg rasburicase, allpurinol
