1. Significantly below average intelligence - ($\geq 2 \sigma$ below the mean, typically IQ <70)
2. Significantly below average adaptive behavior
3. Originating during the developmental period (usually 0-5 period but cut-off 18. Otherwise an acquired brain injury )


![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260422101316.png]]

- Leading genetic cause: [[Pearls/Down's syndrome|Down's syndrome]]
- Leading non-genetic cause: [[Pearls/Fetal alcohol syndrome|Fetal alcohol syndrome]]
- Leading inherited cause: [[Pearls/Fragile X Syndrome|Fragile X Syndrome]]
