Commonest cause of respiratory distress within 2 hours of birth. It is caused by delayed resorption of fluid in the lung. 
- Most common in those with [[Pearls/Caesarean Section|Caesarean Section]]
- CXR may show hyperinflation of the lungs and **fluid in the horizontal fissure**
- Supportive care + oxygen to maintain sats
- Usually settles within 1-2 days
