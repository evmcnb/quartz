The inflammatory bowel diseases (IBD), [[Full/Ulcerative Colitis]] and Crohn's disease are chronic autoimmune relapsing and remitting inflammation of the intestine.

Can effect any part of GI tract from mouth to anus
Crohn’s is also associated with **_strictures_** and **_fistulas_**.
More difficult to manage due to this


> [!success] Differentiating Features of Crohn's (Crows **NEST**)
> - **N**o blood or mucus (PR bleeding is less common)
> - **E**ntire GI tract can be affected (mouth to anus)
> - **S**kip lesions on endoscopy
> - **T**erminal ileum most affected + **T**ransmural (full thickness) inflammation
> - **S**moking is a risk factor

## Causes/Factors

Inappropriate immune response against (?abnormal) colonic flora in genetically susceptible individuals - failure to maintain tolerance

- Smoking $\uparrow$ risk 3-4 times
- NSAIDs - onset & flare ups
- Hygiene - cleaner environment doesn't expose intestinal immune system to pathogens

## Symptoms

- Diarrhoea
- Abdominal pain
- Weight loss/failure to thrive
- Systemic symptoms: pyrexia, malaise, anorexia

## Signs

- bowel ulceration
- abdominal tenderness
- [[Pearls/Perianal abscess|Perianal abscess]]/fistulae/skin tags
- anal strictures
- patchy inflammation

Extraintestinal signs:

- [[Pearls/clubbing]]
- Eyes - [[Full/Uveitis|Uveitis]], [[Full/Scleritis|episcleritis]], [[Full/Conjunctivitis|Conjunctivitis]]
- Enteropathic arthritis
- [[Full/erythema nodosum|erythema nodosum]]
- [[Full/pyoderma gangrenosum|pyoderma gangrenosum]]

## Diagnostic Tests

- Calprotectin - protein released from neutrophils into stool -> inflammatory marker, highly sensitive. Used as initial test before endoscopy
- Blood tests for inflammatory markers/[[Pearls/Anaemia|Anaemia]]:
	- FBC - Hb + platelet
	- CRP
	- U&E indicate electrolyte imbalances and kidney function
	- LFT - low albumin in severe disease
	- TFT for hyperthyroidism as a cause of diarrhoea
	- Anti-TTG  to rule out coeliac as a differential 
- Full colonoscopy or sigmoidoscopy $\rightarrow$ biopsy
- Crypt abcesses on histology
- AXR - no feacal shadows, mucosal thickening/islands

## Management

**Optimise nutrition**
![|650](z_attachments/650.png)



### Inducing Remission

- **Steroids** - oral prednisolone or IV hydrocortisone
- **Exclusive enteral nutrition** - very simple food, treating nutritional deficiencies (B12, folate, calcium). First-line for children 

where steroids are not enough may add:
- azathioprine
- mercaptopurine
- methotrexate
- infliximab
- adalimumab

**Hospital admission** if the patient is systematically unwell with bloody diarrhoea, fever, tachycardia, hypotension

### Maintaining remission

First line for maintaining remission: 
- azathioprine
- mercaptopurine

Methotrexate is an alternative when first line options are unsuitable
