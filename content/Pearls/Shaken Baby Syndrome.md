> [!danger] Classic Triad
> Retinal haemorrhages + subdural haematoma + encephalopathy


Babies have a big head and an unstable neck leading to big forces on the brain when shaken. This causes yearing of bridging veins giving [[Full/Subdural Haematoma|Subdural Haematoma]]
