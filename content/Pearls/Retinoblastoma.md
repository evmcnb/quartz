Most common ocular malignancy. Average age of diagnosis at 18 months.
- Autosomal dominant - 10% of cases hereditary 
- absent red reflex (white pupil instead)
- strabismus/visual problems

Depending on how advanced the tumour is can try external beam radiation, chemotherapy and photocoagulation. Ultimately may need to remove the eye

Good prognosis of >90%
