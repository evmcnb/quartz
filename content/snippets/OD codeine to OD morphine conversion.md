The conversion factor between oral codeine and oral morphine is to **divide by 10**. This is based on the general consensus that the potency of oral morphine is approximately 10 times greater than that of oral codeine. Therefore, when converting a patient's dose from codeine to morphine, you should divide the total daily dose of codeine by 10 to obtain the equivalent daily dose of oral morphine.
