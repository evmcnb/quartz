Kidneys are primarily responsible for the excretion of potassium from the body. Potassium secretion is enhanced by sodium absorption through Na/K pump in distal renal tubules. Therefore if in an injury this exchange cannot occur - [[snippets/Hyperkalaemia|Hyperkalaemia]]

Sodium excretion and reabsorption is dependant on fluid and acid-base balance and more facts may influence if the kidneys need to excrete it
