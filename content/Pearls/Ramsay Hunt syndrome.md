Ramsay Hunt syndrome (herpes zoster oticus) is caused by the reactivation of the [[Pearls/Varicella zoster virus|Varicella zoster virus]] in the geniculate ganglion of the seventh cranial nerve.  

  

Features

* auricular pain is often the first feature
* facial nerve palsy
* vesicular rash around the ear
* other features include vertigo and tinnitus

  

Management

* oral aciclovir and corticosteroids are usually given
