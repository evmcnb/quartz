Testes develop in the abdomen and migrate down through the inguinal canal. In about 5% the testes have now made it out of the abdomen by birth. The longer the testes take to descend the less likely it is to happen spontaneously.

![](z_attachments/TEOIepH.png)

Many babies can be born within the 25-35 week window without testis decent

***Cryptorchidism*** refers to failure of one or both testes to descend fully into the scrotum by around **3 months of age.**
## Causes/Factors

- Family history
- Low birth weight
- Small gestational age
- Permaturity
- Maternal smoking during [[Full/Pregnancy]]

For **bilaterial non-palpable testes consider disorders of sex development**

## Management

1. **Orchidopexy:** Refer for surgical correction of the undescended testes after 3 months of age. Carried out between 6 and 18 months to salvage fertility.

> [!info]
> It is normal in boys that have not reached puberty for the testes to move out of the scrotum and into the inguinal canal when its cold or the **cremasteric reflex** is activated

## Complications/red Flags

- **Subfertility:** Undescended testicles can impact sperm production and fertility.
- [[Full/Seminoma]]/[[Full/Testicular Cancer|Testicular Cancer]] - 40x increased risk **regardless of whether it was surgically corrected**  
- **Inguinal [[Full/Hernias]]**
- **[[Pearls/Testicular Torsion|Testicular Torsion]]**
