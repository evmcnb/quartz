Endocrine disorder characterised by hyperandrogenism + [[Pearls/Insulin|Insulin]] resistance. Both a high insulin and LH levels are seen in PCOS - some overlap with metabolic syndrome 

Insulin resistance $\rightarrow$ Increased GnRH stimulation from the hypothalamus $\rightarrow$ elevated LH levels & decreased FSH

- $\uparrow$ LH causes androgen production (from the theca interna cells) $\rightarrow$ hirutism (& $\downarrow$ sex hormone binding globulin (SHBG))
- $\downarrow$ FSH causes poor follicular development $\rightarrow$ follicles retained as cysts 

Investigated with:
- Pelvic USS
- FSH, LH, prolactin (normal/high), TSH, testosterone (normal/slightly elevated, should not be really high), SHBG (normal/low)
- CBG/check for impaired glucose tolerance

* Treatment with weight loss and oral contraceptive (cycle regulation)

> [!success] Rotterdam criteria
> Diagnosis 2/3 of:
>* infrequent/no ovulation
>* clinical/biochemical signs of hyperandrogenism (hirutism, [[Full/Acne vulgaris]])
>* polycystic ovaries on USS (>12 follicles bigger than 2-9mm, or increased ovarian volume >10 cm$^3$ )

If weight loss does not help with conception, can add in **metformin** as an adjunct. Metformin has been shown to have a beneficial effect on ovulation and conception rates in patients with PCOS.

Clomiphene citrate can be given to stimulate ovulation in those trying to conceive. Increases endogenous FSH production 

Increased risk of [[Pearls/Endometrial cancer|Endometrial cancer]]. Due to high levels of unopposed oestrogen. Functioning follicles (corpus luteam) produce progesterone so as these are lacking, high exposure to unopposed oestrogen
