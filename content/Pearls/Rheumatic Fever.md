Complication of Group A Strep pharyngitis (*Strep pyogenes*) 2-4 weeks ago. 

* Children in developing countries
* Untreated Strep pyogenes + **immune cross reactivity**
* Antibodies also recognise proteins on surface of cells in heart, skin, joints, CNS

### Diagnostic criteria

Need a recent streptococcal infection plus 2 major or 1 major + 2 minor criteria:

**Major**
- Erythema marginatum
- Polyarthritis
- Sydenham's chorea
- Carditis and valvulitis
- Subcutaneous nodules

**Minor**
- raised ESR or CRP
- pyrexia
- prolonged PR 


#### Management

- NSAIDs first-line
- Antibiotics: oral pen V
- Treat any complication
