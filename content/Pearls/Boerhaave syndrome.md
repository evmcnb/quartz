Full thickness rupture of the oesophagus caused by intense vomiting/coughing/retching. It is distinct from Mallory-Weiss syndrome (a non-transmural, partial-thickness tear)

> [!success] Mackler triad
> - Vomiting
> - Lower chest pain
> - Subcutaneous chest pain

Treatment requires urgent surgical repair or, in some cases, conservative/endoscopic management, often followed by ICU care for septic management. Without treatment, mortality exceeds 90%. Even with treatment, it is associated with a mortality rate of 40%.
