Extrapyramidal side-effects (EPSEs)  
- [[Pearls/Parkinsonism features|Parkinsonism]]
- acute dystonia
    - sustained muscle contraction (e.g. torticollis, oculogyric crisis)
    - may be managed with procyclidine
- akathisia (severe restlessness)
- tardive dyskinesia (late onset of choreoathetoid movements, abnormal, involuntary, may occur in 40% of patients, may be irreversible, most common is chewing and pouting of jaw)



> [!info] Choreoathetoid movements
> A combination of involuntary, jerky, rapid movements (chorea) and slow, writhing, twisting movements (athetosis)
> - Caused by basal ganglia dysfunction
>
