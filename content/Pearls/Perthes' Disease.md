Degenerative condition affecting joints of children aged 4-8. Due to **avascular necrosis of the femoral head** (epiphysis). 
- 5x more common in boys
- 10% bilateral
- gradually developing hip pain (weeks)
- limp
- stiffness and reduced range of movement

Diagnosis via plain XR (widening of joint space then decreased femoral head size)

**Catterall staging**

|**Stage**|**Features**|
|---|---|
|Stage 1|Clinical and histological features only|
|Stage 2|Sclerosis with or without cystic changes and preservation of the articular surface|
|Stage 3|Loss of structural integrity of the femoral head|
|Stage 4|Loss of acetabular integrity|

**Management**
- Keep the femoral head within the acetabulum with cast, braces
- <6 years old obseration
- Older: surgical management 

Most cases resolve with conservative management
