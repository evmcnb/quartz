**Last menstrual period (LMP)** - the date of the most recent period

**Gestational age (GA)** - the duration of the pregnancy starting from the date of the LMP
- 5+0 refers to 5 weeks GA
- 13+6 refers to 13 weeks and 6 days

**Estimated date of delivery (EDD)** - the estimated date of delivery (date at 40 weeks gestation)

The timeline for each pregnancy depends on the start date of the LMP. This determine sthe GA and the EDD of the pregnancy. After the booking scan, the GA is more accurately assessed and the EDD is updated accordingly.


--- 

**Gravida (G)** - total number of pregnancies a woman has had

**Primigravida** - patient who is pregnancy for the first time

**Multigravida** - patient who is pregnant for at least the second time

**Para (P)** - refers to the number of times the woman has given birth after 24 weeks regardless of fetal outcome

**Nullparous ("nullip")** - a patient that has never given birth after 24 weeks gestation

**Primiparous** - technically refers to a woman that has given birth once before, however it is often used on the labour ward to refer to a woman that is due to give birth for the first time (and has never given birth before) 

**Multiparous ("multip")** - a patient that has given birth after 24 weeks gestation 2+ times

###### Examples

- A pregnant woman with 3 previous deliveries at term: G4 P3 
- A non-pregnant woman with a previous birth of healthy twins: G1 P1
- A non-pregnant woman with a previous *miscarriage*: G1 P0 + 1
- A non-pregnant woman with a previous stillbirth (after 24 weeks gestation): G1 P1

--- 

##### Trimesters

1. **First trimester** is from the start of pregnancy until 12 weeks gestation
2. **Second trimester** is from 13 weeks until 26 weeks
3. **Third trimester** is from 27 weeks until birth
