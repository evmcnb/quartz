**Viral labyrinthitis** stereotypical history: recently developed an upper respiratory tract infection presents with vertigo and vomiting. Hearing is also affected. The symptoms came on suddenly

Prochlorperazine may be useful in the acute phase of vestibular neuronitis

**Vestibular neuritis** is inflammation of the vestibular nerve. It presents very similarly to labyrinthitis. However, hearing loss is **not** a feature of vestibular neuritis
