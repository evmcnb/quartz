A sudden collapse of a patient either antenatally or in the first 6 weeks post-partum 
- A-E assessment as normal 
- Add in F for fetus
- Call for help early 
- Resuscitate the woman before considering the baby
- Most women are fit and well before pregnancy so can compensate well

**Common causes of an emergency:**
- [[Pearls/Postpartum haemorrhage|Post/antepartum haemorrhage]]
- [[Full/pulmonary embolism|pulmonary embolism]]
- [[Pearls/Eclampsia|Eclampsia]]
- [[Full/myocardial infarction|myocardial infarction]] (uncommon)
- [[Pearls/Uterine rupture|Uterine rupture]]
- Uterine inversion (rare)
- [[Full/Sepsis|Sepsis]]
- [[Pearls/Amniotic fluid embolism|Amniotic fluid embolism]]
