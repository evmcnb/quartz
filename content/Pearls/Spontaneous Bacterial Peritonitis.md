A form of peritonitis, usually seen in patients with **[[Pearls/Ascites|Ascites]]** secondary to liver [[Pearls/Cirrhosis|Cirrhosis]]

* Abdominal pain and fever in patients with [[Pearls/Ascites|Ascites]]
* Diagnosis is made from **paracentesis**, with a raised neutrophil count of over 250 cells
* Most common causative organism is Escherichia coli then *Klebselia pneumoniae*
* Give broad-spec antibiotics (Co-trimoxazole/piptaz/cipro) to those with a ascitic protein of <15g/L for 5-7days
