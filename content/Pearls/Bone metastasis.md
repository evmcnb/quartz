Most common tumour causing bone metastases (in descending order)  
- prostate
- breast
- lung

Most common site (in descending order)  
- spine
- pelvis
- ribs
- skull
- long bones

Other than bone pain, features may include:  
- [[Pearls/Pathological fractures|Pathological fractures]]
- [[Pearls/Hypercalcaemia|Hypercalcaemia]]
- raised ALP
