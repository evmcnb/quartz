Excessive pain during the menstrual period.
- **Primary**: no underlying pelvic pathology. Managed with mefenamic acid or ibuprofen. If unsuccessful [[Pearls/Combined oral contraceptive pill|Combined oral contraceptive pill]]
- **Secondary**: underlying pathology, pain usually starts 3-4 days before the onset of the period


##### Secondary

Causes include: 
- [[Pearls/Endometriosis|Endometriosis]]
- [[Pearls/Adenomyosis|Adenomyosis]]
- [[Pearls/Pelvic inflammatory disease|Pelvic inflammatory disease]]
- [[Pearls/Intrauterine contraceptive device|Intrauterine contraceptive device]]
- [[Pearls/Fibroids|Fibroids]]

Need to refer all patients to secondary care for investigation - STILL PROVIDE TREATMENT e.g. analgesia]
