Disorders that present with muscle inflammation (myositis). Both present with proximal muscle weakness. Difficulty in standing from a chair, climbing stairs or lifting anything overhead.

Dermatomyositis is a variant of the disease where skin manifestations are prominent, for example a purple (heliotrope) rash on the cheeks and eyelids
## Causes/Factors

- Polymyositis or dermatomyositis can be paraneoplastic syndromes. 25% association with underlying visceral cancer
- Virus (e.g., Coxsackie virus or HIV)
- Certain HLA genes are risk factors

## Features

- Elevated CK. Other muscle enzymes (lactate dehydrogenase (LDH), aldolase, AST and ALT) are also elevated in 85-95% of patients
- Dermatomyositis involves characteristic skin changes - **Gottron lesions**

## Investigations

- ANA (80% positive)
- myositis-specific antibodies (MSAs):
	- anti-Mi-2 - dermatomyostitis
	- anti-Jo-1 - polymyositis

## Management

High dose cortiosteroids. Other immunosuppresants an option.
