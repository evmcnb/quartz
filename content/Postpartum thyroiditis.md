Three stages in order usually 3 months apart: 
1. [[Full/Hyperthyroidism|Thyrotoxicosis]] - managed by propanolol alone
2. [[Full/Hypothyroidism|Hypothyroidism]] - managed with thyroxine 
3. Normal thyroid function - but high recurrence rate in future pregnancies 

Thyroid peroxidase antibodies are found in 90% of patients
