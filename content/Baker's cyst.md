Are not true cysts. Distensions of the gastrocnemius-semimembranosus bursa

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20261016115828.png|332]]

- Primary: no underlying pathology, typically seen in children
- Secondary: underlying condition such as osteoarthritis, typically seen in adults

Rupture may occur resulting in similar symptoms to a [[Full/Deep Vein Thrombosis|Deep Vein Thrombosis]], i.e. pain, redness and swelling in the calf. **USS** to confirm/exclude DVT


However, the majority of ruptures are asymptomatic.  
  
Baker's cysts in children typically resolve and do not require treatment.  
  
In adults, the underlying cause should be treated where appropriate
