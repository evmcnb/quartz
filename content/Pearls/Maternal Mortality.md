Maternal death - during pregnancy or within 42 days of the end of pregnancy (includes all types of pregnancy)

Direct - resulting from complications of the pregnant state

Indirect - resulting from previous existing disease

Late - from 42 to 1 years

Incidental - not as the result of any complication
