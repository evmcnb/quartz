HSP is an **IgA mediated small vessel vasculitis**. Degree of overlap with [[Pearls/Nephritis in Children#IgA Nephropathy|IgA nephropathy]]. Usually seen in children following an infection


![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260202203154.png]]

Features:
- palpable purpuric rash with localised oedema over buttocks and extensor surfaces
- abdominal pain
- polyarthritis
- haematuria/renal failure

Usually a self-limiting condition (especially in kids without renal involvement). BP and urinanalysis should be monitored. Treat with analgesia. Use of steroids/immunosuppressants not consistently supported in literature
