Autosomal dominant connective tissue disorder that mostly affects type III collagen. 

Features:
- Elastic, fragile skin
- Joint hypermobility
- Easy bruising
- [[Full/Aortic regurgitation|Aortic regurgitation]]
- [[Full/Subarachnoid haemorrhage|Subarachnoid haemorrhage]]
- Angioid retinal streaks


Clinical diagnosis 
- check echo for valvular/aortic disease
- genetic testing to confirm specific subtypes, especially when vascular EDS is suspected
- ophthalmological assessment


> [!success] Beighton Score
> Used to assess for hypermobility and support the diagnosis. One point is scored for each side of the body - max score of 9 if the patient can:
> - Place their palms flat on the floor with their straight legs (1)
> - Hyperextend their elbows (beyond 190 degrees)
> - Hyperextend their knees
> - Bend their thumb to touch their forearm
> - Hyperextend their little finger past 90$\degree$

**Management**  
- management is supportive, focusing on physiotherapy to strengthen joints and reduce dislocation risk
- regular cardiovascular monitoring is recommended due to the risk of aortic pathology
- avoid high-impact activities and contact sports to limit joint injury and bruising
- genetic counselling should be offered to affected individuals and their families
