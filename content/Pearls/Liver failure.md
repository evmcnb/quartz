| Hyper Acute | <7 day onset |
| --- | --- |
| Acute | 8-21 days |
| Subacute | 4-26 weeks |

Anything that harms the liver can cause liver failure and associated symptoms - [[Pearls/Jaundice|Jaundice]], hepatic encephalopathy, asterixis etc

Commonest causes:

* Acute [[Pearls/Viral hepatitis|Viral hepatitis]]
* Acute [[Pearls/Paracetamol|Paracetamol]] overdose
