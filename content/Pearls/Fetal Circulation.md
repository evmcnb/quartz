Fetal blood travels to the placenta via the two **_umbilical arteries_**. The umbilical arteries originate from the **_internal iliac arteries_**.

Fetal blood travels back from the placenta to the fetus via the **_umbilical vein_**.

#### Fetal Shunts

- Ductus venosus - connecting umbilical vein to the inferior vena cava bypassing the liver
- Foramen ovale - right atrium to the left atrium bypassing the RV and lungs
- Ductus arteriosus - connecting the pulmonary artery to the aorta bypassing the lungs

The first breaths after birth expand the alveoli, decreasing pulmonary vascular resistance. This causes a fall in pressure in the right atrium and ventricle. 

At this point, left atrial pressure is greater than right which squashes the atrial septum causing a *functional* closure of the foramen ovale. Its sealed within a few weeks becoming the fossa ovalis

The oxygenation causes a drop in circulating prostaglandins, closing the ductus arteriosus becoming ligamentum arteriosum

When blood stops circulating through the umbilical vein, the ductus venosus stops functioning. It closes structurally a few days later to become the ligamentum venosum.
