**Calcium antagonist** - smooth muscle relaxation, decreased neurotransmitter release.

NDMA antagonist - blocks neuronal excitation (neuroprotection). Can act as a adjunct analgesic (prevent's wind-up & chronic pain)

Cofactor - required for many enzymatic reactions

### Clinical Indications

1. Torades de Pointes
	- First-line for polymorphic VT even if serum Mg is normal
	- Stabilises cardiac membrane and shortens the QT interval
2. Severe pre-eclampsia/eclampsia
	- Seizure prophylaxis - better than benzos/phenytoin
	- Increases seizure threshold and causes cerebral vasodilation
3. Severe acute asthma
	- Adjunct when SABA/anticholinergics fail 
	- Smooth muscle relaxation $\rightarrow$ bronchodilation


### Other factors

- PPIs and diuretics cause chronic hypomagesemia 
- Do not give magnesium to patients on aminoglycosides (gentamicin) or with [[Pearls/Myasthenia gravis|Myasthenia gravis]]
- Reduce the induction and maintenance dose of propofol
- Prolongs the effect of non-depolarising muscle relaxants (rocuronium)
