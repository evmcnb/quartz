Just as it states, antibodies against phospholipids. Causes a **pre-disposition to both venous and arterial thromboses**. May occur as a primary disorder or secondary to other conditions.

Causes a paradoxical raise in the APTT  

**Associated conditions**
- [[Pearls/Systemic Lupus Erythematosus|Systemic Lupus Erythematosus]] - 30% of patients with SLE have APL antibodies
- Other autoimmune disorders

**Features**
- Venous/arterial thrombosis
- Recurrent miscarriages
- Other: [[Full/Pre-eclampsia|Pre-eclampsia]], [[Full/cor pulmonale|cor pulmonale]]

**Investigations**
- anticardilolipin antibody positive
- FBC - thrombocytopenic
- Clotting - pro-longed APTT


> [!warning] NICE Diagnostic criteria
> Clinical criteria (at least one must be met):
    > - ≥1 clinical episode of arterial, venous, or small-vessel thrombosis (confirmed by >imaging or histopathology)
    > - Pregnancy morbidity: three or more consecutive, unexplained spontaneous abortions <10 weeks, or ≥1 unexplained fetal loss ≥10 weeks, or ≥1 preterm birth <34 weeks due to placental insufficiency
>
> Laboratory criteria (at least one must be met on ≥2 occasions at least 12 weeks apart):
    > - Lupus anticoagulant (LA)
    > - Anticardiolipin (aCL) antibodies
    > - Anti-beta2 glycoprotein I (anti-β2GP1) antibodies

#### Management

Based on the EULAR guidelines - high risk group should receive the below:  

Primary thromboprophylaxis: 
- Low-dose aspirin
- Consider LMWH in some situations (see pregnancy)

Secondary thromboprophylaxis
- Lifelong [[Pearls/Warfarin|Warfarin]] with target INR of 2-3
- If recurrent events increase INR target to 3-4 and add aspirin

**Pregnancy**
- Low-dose aspirin should be commenced once the pregnancy is confirmed on urine test
- LMWH once a fetal heart is seen on ultrasound. Discontinued at 34 weeks
