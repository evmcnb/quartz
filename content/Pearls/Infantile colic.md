Starts in the first few weeks of life and usually resolves 3-4 months of life. 
- Most often in late afternoon/evening
- Arching of back
- Lack of any more sinister signs

No treatment shown to be of substantial benefit - except antimuscarinics but have serious adverse side effects so not recommended. 

Advise simple measures such as holding, gentle motion and white noise to help
