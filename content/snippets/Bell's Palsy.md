Facial paralysis resulting from inflammation or compression of facial nerve (CN VII)

* **Sudden onset**
* Unilateral
* Drooping of the eyelid and corner of mouth
* Difficulty in closing one eye
* Altered taste

Associated with viral infections

Most resolve spontaneously within weeks to months but **corticosteroids** may be prescribed

Eye protection is essential to prevent corneal damage due to blinking
