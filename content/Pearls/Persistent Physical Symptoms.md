1. Psychologically based symptoms (unconscious)
2. Abnormal illness behavior (unconscious)
3. Malingering (conscious)
4. Factitious or feigned symptoms (conscious)

**Not** a diagnosis of exclusion. Make the positive diagnosis from the following features 

- Quantity
- Quality
- Consistency
- Plausibility - head shaking side to side in NESD. 
- Multiple drugs/drugs with unclear indications 
- Multiple allergies
- Syndrome shift - symptoms moves around systems
- History of early chronic physical illness from family/parent
- Lack of somatic vocabulary 

Moving away from the cause of the symptom and try and understand what function the symptom serves. 
- Acknowledge the symptom is real 
- Avoid reassurance
