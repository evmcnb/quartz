## Introduction

- **Wash hands** (and don PPE if needed)
- **Introduce yourself** (name and role)
- Confirm patient's **name** and **DOB**
- Explain what the examination involves
- Explain the **need** for a chaperone
- Gain consent to continue
- Ask if patient in any **pain** before continuing
- Ask the patient to expose themselves in privacy and check when to re-enter

#### Gather equipment

- Non-sterile gloves
- Apron
- Lubricant
- Paper towels

#### Preparation

- Don an apron and pair of gloves
- With the couch flat, ask the patient to lie down on their left with their knees bent up towards their chest
## Inspection

#### Clinical Signs

- <span style="color:#ff0000">Skin excoriation</span> - caused be <span style="color:#00b0f0">haemorrhoids</span> , <span style="color:#00b0f0">faecal incontinence</span> or <span style="color:#00b0f0">constipation</span>
- <span style="color:#ff0000">Skin tags</span> - can be associated with <span style="color:#00b0f0">Crohn's </span>
- <span style="color:#ff0000">External haemorrhoids</span> 
- <span style="color:#ff0000">Anal fissure</span>
- <span style="color:#ff0000">External bleeding</span> - <span style="color:#00b0f0">external haemorrhoids</span>, <span style="color:#00b0f0">anal cancer</span> or brisk <span style="color:#00b0f0">GI bleeding</span>
- <span style="color:#ff0000">Anal fistula</span> - may discharge pus with surrounding inflammation. <span style="color:#00b0f0">Crohn's disease, chronic anal abscess and diverticulitis</span>
- <span style="color:#ff0000">Irregular growths</span> - <span style="color:#00b0f0">anal warts or anal cancer</span>

##### Ask patient to cough & inspect

- <span style="color:#ff0000">Rectal prolapse</span>
- <span style="color:#ff0000">Internal haemorrhoids</span> - above the dentate line

## Palpation

1. Lubricate the digit
2. Warn the patient
3. Insert the digit
4. In males **palpate the prostate gland anteriorly** - size symmetry and texture
5. Rotate finger 360$\degree$ and feel for abnormalities and location of any tenderness
6. Withdraw the finger and examine for blood mucus or pus
7. Clean the patient using paper towels
8. Cover the patient and say that the exam is complete
9. Dispose of used equipment 

## To Complete the Exam

- **Explain** to the patient that the examination is now **finished**.
- **Thank the patient** for their time.
- **Dispose of PPE** appropriately and **wash your hands**.
- **Summarise** your findings.
