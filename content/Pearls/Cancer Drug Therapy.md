Types of Systemic Anti-Cancer Treatment (SACT)
 - [[Pearls/Cytotoxic agents|Cytotoxic agents]] (e.g. docetaxel, cisplatin)
 - Immunotherapy (e.g. immune checkpoint inhibitors such as anti-PD1 - pembrolizumab)
 - Hormone treatment (tamoxifen, LHRH analogue leuprorelin)
 - Biological or targeted therapy (monoclonal antibodies, tyrosine kinase inhibitors)
