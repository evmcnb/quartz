#### Hepatitis B serology

- HBsAg/HbeAg - **ongoing infection**, either acute or chronic if present >6 months
- Anti-HBs implies immunity either exposure or immunisation
- Anti-HBc implies previous or current infection.

Some examples: 
- Previous immunisation: anti-HBs positive, all others negative
- previous hepatitis B, not a carrier: anti-HBc positive, HBsAg negative
- Previous hepatitis B now a carrier: anti-HBc positive, HBsAg positive

> [!warning] Remebering serology
> - HBsAg - ongoing infection
> - Anti-HB**c** - **c**aught by the body, previous infection 


### Hepatitis in Pregnancy

#### Hep B

- all pregnant women are offered screening for hepatitis B
- babies born to mothers who are chronically infected with hepatitis B or to mothers who've had acute hepatitis B during pregnancy should receive a complete course of vaccination (doses at birth, 1-2m & 6m) + hepatitis B immunoglobulin
- studies are currently evaluating the role of oral antiviral treatment (e.g. Lamivudine) in the latter part of pregnancy
- there is little evidence to suggest caesarean section reduces vertical transmission rates
- hepatitis B cannot be transmitted via breastfeeding (in contrast to HIV)
