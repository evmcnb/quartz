Subdermal implant in the non-dominant arm. Slowly releases progestogen

Advantages:
- **Most effective form of contraception**
- Long-acting - lasts 3 years
- Doesn't contain oestrogen (safe if past history of VTE, migraine)
- Can be inserted immediately following termination of pregnancy

Disadvantages:
- **Irregular/heavy bleeding** - sometimes managed with a co-prescription of the [[Pearls/Combined oral contraceptive pill|Combined oral contraceptive pill]]
- Need to a trained professional to remove the device
- Additional contraception needed for first 7 days if not inserted on day 1-5 of menstrual cycle 
- Enzyme inducing drugs may reduce efficacy
- Contra-indicated in current breast cancer
