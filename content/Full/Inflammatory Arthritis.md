Usually the lining of the joint that gets affected
Anyone at any age can get inflammatory arthritis

#### Features

- Inflammation with swelling, tenderness, warmth and decreased ROM.
- Worse in the morning - improved with movement
- Good response to NSAIDs
- Strong family history
- Presense of systemic features - lethary, poor appetite, weight gain

## Rheumatoid Arthritis

^435d6c

^ab6a7d
A systemic autoimmune disorder affecting synovial joint membrane with extra-articular manifestations 

- Autoimmune
- Most common presents at 40-60s y/o
- 3:1 F:M
- Other organs can be effected
- 80% positive Rheumatoid Factor blood test
- Positive anti-CCP antibody
- Accelerated risk of [[Full/Coronary Artery Disease|Coronary Artery Disease]] (RA is a feature on Q-risk)

#### Signs
- **Symmetrical polyarthritis** - most common joints: MCP, wrist, PIP, knee, MTP, shoulder, ankle, C-spine, hip, elbow, TMJ
- Rheumatoid nodules (elbow and lungs)
- Positive squeeze test
  ![|500](z_attachments/500.png)
- **_Z-shaped deformity_** to the thumb
- **_Swan neck deformity_** (hyperextended PIP and flexed DIP)
- **_Boutonniere deformity_** (hyperextended DIP and flexed PIP)
- **_Ulnar deviation_** of the fingers at the MCP joints

> [!important] 
> Rheumatoid arthritis very rarely affects the distal interphalangeal joints. Enlarged and painful distal interphalangeal joints are more likely to represent Heberden’s nodes due to [[Full/Osteoarthritis|Osteoarthritis]].



Extra-articular:
- Generalised malaise, fatigue
- Pericardtis or percardial effusion
- Vasculitis
- [[Full/Pulmonary Fibrosis]]
- Subluxation of atlas joint

**LESS**
- **L**oss of joint space  
- **E**rosions  
- **S**oft tissue swelling  
- **S**oft bones (osteopaenia)

#### Diagnostic Tests

- **Bloods** - $\uparrow$ ESR, CRP - FBC: [[Pearls/Anaemia|Anaemia]] of chronic disease, thrombocytosis, leukocytosis
- **Anti-CCP antibody** - more specific and sensitive than RF 
- **XR**/MRI of the area looking for erosions and damage
- **Joint aspirations** - synovial fluid with increased WCC and protein

> [!tldr] 2010 ACR/EULAR Classification Criteria for Rheumatoid Arthritis
>
> **Who to test:** Patients with ≥1 joint with definite clinical synovitis (swelling) not better explained by another disease.
> **Classification:** A score of **≥6/10** = definite RA.
>
> | Domain | Criterion | Score |
> |---|---|:---:|
> | **A. Joint involvement** | 1 large joint | 0 |
> | | 2–10 large joints | 1 |
> | | 1–3 small joints (± large joints) | 2 |
> | | 4–10 small joints (± large joints) | 3 |
> | | >10 joints (≥1 small joint) | 5 |
> | **B. Serology** (≥1 test needed) | Negative RF *and* negative ACPA | 0 |
> | | Low-positive RF *or* low-positive ACPA | 2 |
> | | High-positive RF *or* high-positive ACPA | 3 |
> | **C. Acute-phase reactants** (≥1 test needed) | Normal CRP *and* normal ESR | 0 |
> | | Abnormal CRP *or* abnormal ESR | 1 |
> | **D. Duration of symptoms** | <6 weeks | 0 |
> | | ≥6 weeks | 1 |
>
> **Definitions:**
> - *Large joints:* shoulders, elbows, hips, knees, ankles.
> - *Small joints:* MCPs, PIPs, 2nd–5th MTPs, thumb IPs, wrists.
> - *Low-positive:* >ULN but ≤3× ULN. *High-positive:* >3× ULN.
> - Score the highest applicable category in each domain; sum all four.

#### Management

1. DMARD monotherapy ([[Pearls/Methotrexate]]) ± steroids
2. Can add - sulfasalazine or leflunomide
3. If not managed by these switch to TNF-A blocker

Monitor with combination of CRP and DAS28 (disease activity measure)

> [!Info] Biological therapy
> TNF inhibitors eg adalimimab and immunomodulators such as rituximab are used in adults with highly active disease who have failed to respond to 2 DMARDs

##### In Pregnancy

RA typically develops in women of reproductive age. Should really be consultant lead care for both and no established guidelines. Principle-wise: 
- Patients with early or poorly controlled RA should be advised to defer conception until their disease is more stable 
- RA tends to improve in pregnancy (due to modification/slight immunosuppression)
- Methotrexate: not safe in pregnancy and needs to be stopped at least 6 months before conception. ? Stop in men to at ~3 months before conception 
- low-dose corticosteroids may be used in pregnancy to control symptoms
- NSAIDs may be used until 32 weeks but after this time should be withdrawn due to the risk of early close of the ductus arteriosus

### Psoriatic Arthritis

- About 15% of people with [[Full/Psoriasis]] will develop psoriatic arthritis. Precedes the development of skin lesions 
- Dactylitis & nail changes (onycholysis) 
- Increased CV risk
- Asymmetrical oligoarthritic typically affects hands and feet. 
- Tends to affect the DIP joints and axial skeleton. RA doesn't not to help distinguish

#### Investigations

Bloods: 
- CRP/ESR elevated
- RF, anti-CCP **NEGATIVE** - seronegative spondyloarthropathy

XR changes: 
- Combination of erosions and new bone formation
- Pencil-in-cup appearance in advanced disease

#### Management 

By a rheumatologist. Similar to RA. 
- Mild disease can be managed by NSAIDs alone (+PPI cover)
- Moderate-to-severe $\rightarrow$ methotrexate
- Prognosis generally better than RA 
### Axial Spondylarthritis

- Inflammatory back pain
- Young people
- Can cause red inflamed eyes ([[Full/Uveitis]])
- Untreated bones can fuse and cause an immobile spine
