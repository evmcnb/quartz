Whats required for reproduction? Egg + endometrium 

Proliferation phase - caused by **oestrogen**
Secreatory phase - caused by **progesterone** from corpus luteum. 

Follicular phase - Usually also 14 days but can vary
Luteal phase - 'always' 14 days

**1. Menstruation (Days 1–5):**

- **What happens:** The uterus lining sheds because no pregnancy occurred.    
- **The result:** You get your period.
    
**2. Follicular Phase (Days 6–14):**

- **What happens:** The brain tells the ovaries to prepare an egg.
- **Hormones:** Estrogen rises to rebuild the uterus lining (making it thick and soft again).


**3. Ovulation (Around Day 14):**

- **What happens:** A mature egg is released from the ovary into the fallopian tube.
- **Hormones:** A sudden spike in **LH (Luteinizing Hormone)** triggers the release. This is the main window for pregnancy.
    

**4. Luteal Phase (Days 15–28):**

- **What happens:** The body waits to see if the egg gets fertilised (hCG)
- **Hormones:** **Progesterone** stays high to keep the lining healthy.
- **The End:** If the egg isn't fertilized, hormone levels drop, triggering step 1 (Menstruation) again.
