aka intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy (ICP)

Affects around 1% of pregnancies. Associated with increased risk of premature birth

- Pruritus - may be intense, typical worse palms, soles and abdomen
- No rash
- Raised bile acids (diagnostic >19 $\mu$mol/L)
- Mild elevation of ALT/AST
- Normal or mildly raised bilirubin 

**Management**

**Still birth risk only rises significantly when bile acids >100**
- Mild ICP (19-39): risk similar to background. Conisder planned birth by 40 weeks or routine care
- Moderate ICP (40-99): risk remains low until 38-39 weeks - consider a delivery then 
- Severe ICP (>100): increased stillbirth risk. Consider delivery at 35-36 weeks

No treatment improves fetal outcomes - do not routinely use ursodeoxycholic acid

Recurrence is 45-90% in subsequent pregnancies
