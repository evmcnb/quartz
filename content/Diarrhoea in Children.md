### Causes:

- Functional/non-specific - excessive intake of juice/osmotic agents
- Enteric infection
- Malabsorption - [[Full/Coeliac Disease|Coeliac Disease]]/pancreatic insufficiency
- [[Full/inflammatory bowel disease|inflammatory bowel disease]]
- Food poisoning
- Immune deficiency
- Malnutrition 
- Protein losing gastroenteropathy - Lymphangiectasia
- Neuroendocrine tumours


#### Investigations

- Stool microscopy 
- Stool calprotectin
- Faecal elastase
- Bloods - U&E, LFTs, TFTs, FBC, CRP, ESR, anti-TTG
- Faecal alpha 1 antitrypsin
- USS
- ?Endoscopy
