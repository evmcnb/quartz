**_Inflammatory_** condition that causes pain and stiffness in the shoulders, pelvic girdle and neck.
## Causes/Factors

- Strong associated with [[Full/Giant cell arteritis|Giant cell arteritis]]
- Older white patients 
## Symptoms

The characteristic features of **_pain and stiffness_** in the shoulders, pelvic girdle and neck are:
- Worse in the morning
- Worse after rest or inactivity
- Interfere with sleep
- Take at least 45 minutes to ease in the morning
- Somewhat improve with activity


Associated features include:
- Systemic symptoms (e.g., weight loss, fatigue and low-grade fever)
- Muscle tenderness
- Carpel tunnel syndrome
- Peripheral oedema

## Investigations


Diagnosis is based on **_clinical presentation_**, **_response to steroids_** and **_excluding differentials_**.


Differentials:
- [[Full/Osteoarthritis|Osteoarthritis]]
- [[Pearls/Rheumatoid Arthritis|Rheumatoid Arthritis]]
- [[Full/Systemic Lupus Erythematosus|Systemic Lupus Erythematosus]]
- [[Pearls/Statins|Statin]]-induced myopathy
- Myositis (e.g., polymyositis)
- Cervical spondylosis
- Adhesive capsulitis (frozen shoulder)
- [[Full/Hyperthyroidism|Hyperthyroidism]] or [[Full/Hypothyroidism|Hypothyroidism]]
- [[Pearls/Osteomalacia|Osteomalacia]]
- [[Pearls/Fibromyalgia|Fibromyalgia]]
- [[Pearls/Lymphoma|Lymphoma]] or [[Pearls/Memorising the leukaemias|leukaemia]]
- [[Pearls/Multiple myeloma|Multiple myeloma]]


## Management

Treatment of PMR is with steroids:
- **_15mg prednisolone_** daily initially
- **_Follow up after 1 week_**

Patients with PMR usually have a dramatic improvement in symptoms within one week. Inflammatory markers normal within one month. Poor response to steroids suggests another diagnosis

Treatment with steroids typically lasts 1-2 years:
- 15mg until the symptoms are fully controlled, then
- 12.5mg for 3 weeks, then
- 10mg for 4-6 weeks, then
- Reducing by 1mg every 4-8 weeks

#### Cover & complications

**Don't STOP**: 
- **Don't** - steroid dependence occurs after 3 weeks of treatment - abruptly stopping risks adrenal crisis
- **S**ick day rules - doubled in periods of illness
- **T**reatment card - so can tell
- **O**steoporosis - side effect of steroids, bisphosphonates, calcium and vit D may be required
- **P**PIs for gastro protection
