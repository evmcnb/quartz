Congenital condition where the ganglion cells of the enteric nervous system are absent for a portion of the bowel - usually sigmoid of rectum causing it not to relax. 

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20251206135141.png|475]]


## Causes/Factors

- 3x more common in males
- [[Pearls/Down's syndrome]]

## Presentation

Usually right after birth with acute intestinal obstruction:
- Absent meconium
- Abdo distension 
- Vomiting

Or can be slower:
- Delay in passing meconium
- Chronic constipation 
- Abdo pain and distension
- Vomiting
- Poor weight gain and failure to thrive 

## Investigations

- Abdo XR 
- Rectal biopsy gold standard for diagnosis


## Management

- Initially: rectal washouts/bowel irrigation
- Definitive: surgery to remove the affected area of the colon
