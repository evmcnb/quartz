Incompetent tricuspid valve

**Pan-systolic murmur** wit ha split second heart sound as the pulmonary value is closing earlier than the aortic. 

## Causes

- Pressure - left sided [[Full/Heart Failure]] or pulmonary [[Full/Essential hypertension]]
- [[Full/Endocarditis]]
- Rheumatic heart disease
- Connective tissue disorders - [[Pearls/Marfan's syndrome]]

## Signs

- Thirll
- Raised JVP
- Pulsatile liver
- Peripheral oedema
- [[Pearls/Ascites|Ascites]]
