The emotional and physical symptoms that women may experience in the luteal phase of the normal menstrual cycle

PMS only occurs in the presence of ovulatory menstrual cycles

- Anxiety
- Stress
- Fatigue
- Mood swings
- Bloating
- Breast pain

Management options depend on severity
- Mild symptoms can be manged with lifestyle advice - sleep, exercise, smoking, alcohol, etc. Specific advice includes **regular frequent (2-3 hourly), small, balanced meals rich in complex carbohydrates**
- Moderate symptoms may benefit from new generation [[Pearls/Combined oral contraceptive pill|Combined oral contraceptive pill]]
- Severe symptoms may benefit from GnHR analogues or SSRIs
