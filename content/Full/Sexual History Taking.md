## Introduction

- **Wash hands** (and don PPE if needed)
- **Introduce yourself** (name and role)
- Confirm patient's **name** and **DOB**
- Explain **confidential nature** and **aim** of history
- Gain consent to continue

## Presenting Complaint

Same as usual

## History of Presenting Complaint 


#### Vaginal/urethral discharge

Important to establish between normal and abnormal - ask about **change** in vaginal discharge
- Volume 
- Colour
- Consistency
- Smell

#### Vaginal bleeding

Important to clarify the following: 
- Nature and pattern - post-coital, intermenstrual
- Volume - in relation to a typical period
- Colour - bright red vs dark
- Impact on quality of life 

#### Dyspareunia

Pain during sexual intercourse. Need to localise the pain
- Superficial - external surface of the genitalia/as the penis enters
- Deep - deep in the pelvis [[Pearls/Sexually Transmitted Infection Treatments|Sexually Transmitted Infection Treatments]]

Need to clarify **duration, location and nature** of the pain

#### Skin changes/itching/lesions

- Have you noticed any lumps/bumps or rashes?
- Any skin changes?
- Is it episodic? Worse at night? 

#### Testicular pain/swelling

Normal SOCRATES history

#### Rectal Symptoms

Patients may not routinely disclose these symptoms
- Discharge - [[Pearls/Sexually Transmitted Infection Treatments|Sexually Transmitted Infection Treatments]] in MSM
- Rectal pain - anal fissure, proctitis
- Rectal lump - [[Pearls/Genital warts]], malignancy
- Anal skin changes - [[Pearls/Genital warts]], [[Pearls/Genital herpes]], [[Pearls/Lichen sclerosus|Lichen sclerosus]], [[Full/Syphilis|Syphilis]] and cancer

## Sexual history 

I need to ask you these sensitive questions so that I know what tests are needed and make sure they are accurate.

1. *When was the last time you had sexual contact with someone?*
2. *Who?* - clarify the gender, someone you know/might see again? 
3. *What?* - what kind of sexual contact did you have? (oral, vagina, anal giving or receiving)
4. *When?* - when did you last have sexual contact with someone different?
5. *What protection is/was used?* 

Need to ask when their last sexual health screen was. 
## Risk assessment

Often done due to the population seen. Risk of HIV and other BBVs. Risk factors for BBVs include:
- Partners from countries where HIV and hepatitis viruses have a high prevalence
- Sex between cisgender men or transgender woman
- Current or past use of injecting drugs
- Recreational drug use during sex
- Sex with someone living with HIV (if they don't have an undetectable viral load) or hepatitis?
- Sex work
- Sex with more than >10 partners in 12 months
- Post-sexual assault

*These are questions we ask everyone about the risk of viruses in the blood*

- Have you been tested for HIV before? - if previous test gather risk information since that test
- Have you had any sexual contact with people outside of the UK?
- Have you been paid or have you paid someone else for sex? 
- Have you had sexual contact with anyone who is living with HIV?
