Reduced amniotic fluid.

Causes  
- premature rupture of membranes
- [[Pearls/Potter sequence|Potter sequence]]
    - bilateral renal agenesis + pulmonary hypoplasia
- intrauterine growth restriction
- post-term gestation
- pre-eclampsia

Can lead to pulmonary hypoplasia
