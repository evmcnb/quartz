**Primary** - failure to establish menstruation by 15 years of age in girls with normal secondary sexual characteristics, or by 13 in girls with no secondary sexual characteristics

**Secondary** - cessation of menstruation for 3-6 months in women with previously normal regular menses or 6-12 months in women with previous oligomenorrhoea 

Not enough oestrogen and testosterone being produced

| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| - gonadal dysgenesis (e.g. [[Pearls/Turner's syndrome\|Turner's syndrome]]) - the most common causes<br>- testicular feminisation<br>- congenital malformations of the genital tract<br>- functional hypothalamic amenorrhoea (e.g. secondary to anorexia)<br>- [[Pearls/Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia\|Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia]]<br>- imperforate hymen | **Pregnancy is the most common reason**<br>- hypothalamic amenorrhoea (e.g. secondary stress, excessive exercise)<br>- [[Pearls/Polycystic ovary syndrome\|Polycystic ovary syndrome]]<br>- hyperprolactinaemia<br>- premature ovarian failure<br>- [[Full/Hyperthyroidism\|Thyrotoxicosis]]<br>- [[Full/Hypothyroidism\|Hypothyroidism]]<br>- Sheehan's syndrome<br>- Asherman's syndrome (intrauterine adhesions) |

- Need to exclude pregnancy
- Test gonadotrophins
	- Low levels indicate a hypothalamic cause (LH and FSH not produced)
	- Raised if gonadal dysgenesis (gonads fail to respond to LS/FSH)
- FBC, U&E, coeliac screen, TFTs
- Prolactin - prevents the release of GnHR
- Androgen levels
- Oestrodiol

Treat underlying cause 

##### [[Pearls/Osteoporosis|Osteoporosis]]

Patients with amenorrhoea associated with low oestrogen levels are at increased risk. When the amenorrhoea lasts more than 12 months, the following is indicated:
- Ensure adequate vitamin D and calcium intake
- HRT or the [[Pearls/Combined oral contraceptive pill|Combined oral contraceptive pill]]
