Basically fetal heart failure causing abnormal oedema in at least 2 body compartments. Life-threatening/not compatible with life.

Can be caused by: 
- alpha [[Pearls/Thalassaemia|Thalassaemia]]
- parvovirus B19 in pregnancy 
- Rh negative pregnancy
