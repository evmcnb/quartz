Loss of the maternal copy of the UBE3A gene on chromosome 15

This can be the result of one of the following mechanisms:
- Maternal deletion of the gene
- Mutation in the maternal copy of the gene 
- Both copies of the gene inherited from the father

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20251220165456.png|475]]


Features:
- Unusual fascination with water
- Happy demeanour/inappropriate laughter
- Severe sspeech impairment
- Ataxia
- Abnormal sleep patterns
- [[Pearls/Epilepsy|Epilepsy]]

Management:
- Speech and language therapy
- Melatonin may help with sleep
- Antiepileptics may help with [[Pearls/Epilepsy|Epilepsy]]
