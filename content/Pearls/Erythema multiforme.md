A hypersensitivity reaction that is commonly triggered by infections. Previously thought that [[Pearls/Steven-Johnson Syndrome|Steven-Johnson Syndrome]] was a severe form of erythema multiforme, but now considered separate.
- Target lesions
- Initially seen on the back of the hands/feet before spreading to the torso.
- Upper limbs more common
- Major variant has mucosal involvement
