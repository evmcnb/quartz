A term that encompasses a variety of specific diseases that affect both the upper and lower motor neurones. Its a progressive and eventually fatal condition.

**There is no effect on the sensory neurones**. Sensory symptoms suggest an alternate diagnosis.

*Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis* (ALS) is the most common and well-known type. *Progressive bulbar palsy* is the second most common (affects muscles of swallowing).
Other types are *progressive muscular atrophy*, and *primary lateral sclerosis*

## Causes/Factors

- Family history 5-10%
- Smoking?
- Heavy metals
- Pesticides

## Symptoms

- Progressive muscle weakness. First affects limbs $\rightarrow$ trunk $\rightarrow$ face $\rightarrow$ speech
- Dysarthria
- Dropping things
- Increased fatigue while exercising

## Signs

Signs of **_lower motor neurone disease_**:
- Muscle wasting
- Reduced tone
- Fasciculations (twitches in the muscles)
- Reduced reflexes

Signs of **_upper motor neurone disease_**:
- Increased tone or spasticity
- Brisk reflexes
- Upgoing plantar reflex

## Investigations

The diagnosis needs to be made very carefully. It is based on the clinical presentation after excluding other conditions by a neurologist. 

## Management

- No halting or reversing progression
- Riluzole can slow progression in ALS
- Non-invasive ventilation can be used to support breathing when respiratory muscles start to fail.
