**Schistocytes** (fragmented red blood cells) - [[Haemolytic uraemic syndrome]] caused by E. Coli _0157:H7_

**Elliptocytes** (elliptical cells) - Hereditary elliptocytosis but also in iron deficiency anaemia, thalassaemia, megaloblastic anaemia, and myelodysplastic syndromes.

**Rouleaux formation** (red blood stacked together in long chains) - [[Pearls/Multiple myeloma|Multiple myeloma]]

**Auer Rods** - [[Pearls/Acute myeloid leukaemia|Acute myeloid leukaemia]]

**Target Cells** - post-splenectomy states, hepatic disease, and thalassaemia

**Howell-Jolly bodies** - hyposplenism

**Tear drop cells** - myelofibrosis and megaloblastic anaemia

**Haemolysis with blister cells** - GP6D deficiency
