Caused by exotoxin released from _Clostridium tetani_ preventing the release of GABA. Spores are present in soil and may be introduced to the body via a wound. May be seen in IVDU.

**Features** - GABA is a relaxant, inhibition of this causes prolonged contraction
- prodrome fever, lethargy, headache
- trismus (lockjaw)
- risus sardonicus: facial spasms
- opisthotonus (arched back, hyperextended neck)
- spasms (e.g. dysphagia)

Management  
- 5 doses of vaccine provides life-long protection
- supportive therapy including ventilatory support and muscle relaxants
- IM human tetanus Ig for high-risk wounds (e.g. compound fractures, delayed surgical intervention, significant degree of devitalised tissue)
- metronidazole the antibiotic of choice
