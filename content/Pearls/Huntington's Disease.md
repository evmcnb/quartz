Autosomal dominant inherited neurodegenerative disorder. Caused by a **repeat expansion** repeat of CAG in Huntingtin gene (chromosome 4).
- Results in degeneration of cholinergic and GABAergic neurons in the striatum of the basal ganglia 
- In successive generations the number of repeat expansions increases $\rightarrow$ earlier onset of disease - **anticipation**

Features typically develop after the age of 35:
- chorea
- personality changes
- dystonia
- saccadic eye movements 

Diagnosis via genetic testing. 

Treat symptoms ([[Pearls/Antipsychotics|Antipsychotics]] and [[Full/Antidepressants|Antidepressants]]). Advanced care planning essential.
