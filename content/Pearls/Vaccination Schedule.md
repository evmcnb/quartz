BCG is offered from birth to babies who are at a higher risk of TB (relatives from countries of high TB prevalence, or live in an urban area with high rate)

Live vaccines: 
- MMR
- MCG
- Varicella
- Rotavirus
- Yellow fever
- Intranasal flu

### Contraindications

- Previous anaphylaxis (not local reactions)
- Live vaccines if immunosuppressed - in babies with HIV mothers if a baby has 2 negative HIV tests, can give BCG
- Egg allergy (anaphylactic) should not receive influenza or yellow fever. MMR with caution


#### Delay immunisations

- Child acutely unwell (more than a minor viral illness)
- Evolving neurological condition
- Immunoglobulins recently given
