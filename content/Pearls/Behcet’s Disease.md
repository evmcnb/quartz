> [!success] Classic Triad
> A complex inflammatory condition affecting blood vessels and tissues. Classically: 
> 1. Oral ulcers
> 2. Genital ulcers
> 3. [[Full/Uveitis|Anterior uveitis]]

Other features include:
- thrombophlebitis and deep vein thrombosis
- arthritis
- neurological involvement (e.g. aseptic meningitis)
- GI: abdo pain, diarrhoea, colitis
- erythema nodosum


#### Epidemiology 

- more common in the eastern Mediterranean (e.g. Turkey)
- more common and more severe in men
- affects young adults (20-40 years)

Associated with HLA B51 gene.


#### Diagnosis   

- no definitive test
- diagnosis based on clinical findings
- positive pathergy test is suggestive (puncture site following needle prick becomes inflamed with small pustule forming)
