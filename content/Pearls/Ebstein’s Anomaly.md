Congenital heart defect characterised by low insertion of the tricuspid valve - large atrium, small ventricle

May be caused by exposure to lithium **in-utero**

Associations: 
- Patent foramen ovale or ASD seen in 80% of patients
- [[Pearls/Wolff-Parkinson White]] syndrome
- Hepatomegaly
- Tricuspid regurgitation - pansystolic murmur worse on inspiration

Features:
- Cyanosis
- Prominent a wave in the distended jugular venous pulse
- RBBB widely split S1 and S2
