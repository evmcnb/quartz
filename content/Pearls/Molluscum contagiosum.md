Caused by molluscum contagiosum virus. Transmission bu close person contact or contaminated surface. The majority of cases occur in children 1-4 years old.

Pinkish/pearly white papules with central umbilication up to 5mm in diameter. Lesions appear in clusters.

**Management**

Treatment is not usually recommended - usually a self-limiting condition. 
- Spontaneous resolution within 18 months
- Encourage not to scratch the lesions
- Exclusion from school/gym/swimming is not necessary
- Cryotherapy may be used
- HIV-positive individuals with extensive lesions should have urgent referral to HIV specialist
