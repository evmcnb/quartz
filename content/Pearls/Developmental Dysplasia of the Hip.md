Structural abnormality caused by abnormal development leading to instability and the tendency for subluxation or dislocation

Risk factors:
- 6x more common in girls
- Breech presentation - all babies at or after **36** weeks gestation require USS regardless of delivery
- Family history
- First born child
- Birth weight >5 kg

Screened for in the [[Full/Newborn infant physical examination|Newborn infant physical examination]] (Barlow and Ortolani test)

**Imaging**
- USS generally first line if suspected
- If the infant is >4.5 months then XR

Most will spontaneously stabilise by 3-6 weeks. If not Pavlik harness in children younger than 4-5 months
