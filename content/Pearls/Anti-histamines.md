**_Oral antihistamines_** are taken prior to exposure to reduce allergic symptoms:
- **_Non-sedating_** antihistamines include **_cetirizine_**, **_loratadine_** and **_fexofenadine_**
- **_Sedating_** antihistamines include **_chlorphenamine_** (Piriton) and **_promethazine_**
