Inhibit HMG-CoA reductase - hepatic cholesterol synthesis

## Indication

- All those with established cardiovascular disease ([[Full/Stroke|Stroke]], [[Full/Transient Ischemic Attack|Transient Ischemic Attack]], [[Full/Coronary Artery Disease|Ischemic Heart Disease]], [[Full/peripheral vascular disease|peripheral arterial disease]])
- Anyone with a Q-risk >10% (general cardio risk, AF, BP, other coagulopathic conditions)
- Patients with [[Full/Diabetes Mellitus|T1DM]] who were diagnosed more than 10 years ago
- CKD with eGFR < 60 

Primary prevention (not had CV event)
- Atorvastatin 20mg OD

Secondary prevention 
- Atorvastatin 80mg OD 

## Adverse effects

- Interacts with marcolides (clarithromycin) need to consider alternative or stop stain
- Myopathy - with associated CK increase
- Liver impairment - check LFTs at baseline, 3 months and 12 months. **Treatment should stop if transaminases rise to over 3x normal limit**
- Possible increase risk of bleed in stroke patients - avoid in those with history of **intracerebral haemorrhage**
