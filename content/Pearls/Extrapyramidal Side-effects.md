#### Acute EPSE

- [[Pearls/Parkinsonism features|Parkinsonism]] - adjust dose + manage with 
- acute dystonia
    - sustained muscle contraction (e.g. torticollis, oculogyric crisis)
    - may be managed with **procyclidine**
- akathisia (severe restlessness) - managed with **propanolol**

#### Chronic EPSE 

- tardive dyskinesia (late onset of choreoathetoid movements, abnormal, involuntary, may occur in 40% of patients, may be irreversible, most common is chewing and pouting of jaw) - managed with **tetrabenazine**


> [!info] Choreoathetoid movements
> A combination of involuntary, jerky, rapid movements (chorea) and slow, writhing, twisting movements (athetosis)
> - Caused by basal ganglia dysfunction
>
