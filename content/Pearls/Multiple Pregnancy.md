Different types: 
- Dichorionic/diamniotic - 37-38 weeks
- Monochorionic/diamniotic - 36 weeks 
- Monochroionic/monoamniotic - 32-34 weeks by [[Pearls/Caesarean Section|Caesarean Section]]
- Conjoined twins

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260106115849.png]]
The **_lambda sign_**, or **_twin peak sign_**, refers to a triangular appearance where the **_membrane_** between the twins meets the **_chorion_**. Indicates a dichorionic twin pregnancy

The **_T sign_** refers to where the membrane between the twins abruptly meets the chorion, giving a T appearance. This indicates a monochorionic twin pregnancy (single placenta).


Higher risk of everything 
- Monochorionic pregnancies - twin-to-twin transfusions
- Miscarriage
- Stillbirth
- Fetal growth restriction
- Prematurity

- Anaemia
- Polyhydramnios
- Hypertension
- Malpresentation

If the presenting twin is cephalic can try vaginal birth. Otherwise [[Pearls/Caesarean Section|Caesarean Section]]
