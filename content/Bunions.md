Bony deformity at the MTP at the base of the big toe. Joint becomes inflamed and enlarged. Overtime the additional stress can result in osteoarthritis.

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20261016121941.png|369]]

They develop slowly, unclear cause. 

- Weight-bearing XRs to assess extent 
- Conservative management with wide shoes and analgesia. Bunion pads to protect from friction
- Surgery for definitive treatment.
