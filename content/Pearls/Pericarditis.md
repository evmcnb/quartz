Inflammation of pericardial sac - reduces effectiveness of contractions

* **Acute** - serofibrinous, caseous, haemorrhagic & purulent
* **Chronic** - constrictive

Infections - Coxackie B, bacteria, TB, fungi etc Autoimmune - [[Pearls/Rheumatic Fever|Rheumatic Fever]], SLE, scleroderma, post-MI Other- uraemia, neoplasia, trauma

**Signs:**

* Saddle shaped ST segment with PR [[Full/Depression]]
* PR depression
* Worse on inspiration lying down - betting leaning forwards
* Kussmaul's sign (the raised JVP that doesn't fall with inspiration).
* Low-grade fever
* Friction rub on auscultation/knock


Management with high dose **NSAIDs** - usually aspirin as only one which is not cardiotoxic - 300-600mg QDS and **colchicine**
