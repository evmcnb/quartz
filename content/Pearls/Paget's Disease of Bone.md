Increased, uncontrolled bone turnover. Disorder of osteoclasts.

The skull, spine/pelvis, and long bones of the lower extremities are most commonly affected.

* Rapid [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] turnover and formation - $\uparrow$ ALP
* Abnormal [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] remodelling mainly in over 50 year old
* [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] pain, deformity, fractures, arthritis
* Cranial nerve defects if skull affected

#### Investigations

- Raised ALP, normal/raised calcium & phosphate
- Plain film XR standard for diagnosis
- Bone scintigraphy

#### Management

- Treat if bone pain, skull or lung bone deformity, fracture periarticular Paget's
- Bisphosphonate (either oral risedronate or IV zoledronate)
