Complications: 
- Prematurity, infection, pulmonary hypoplasia
- [[Full/Chorioamnionitis]] in the mother


**_Rupture of membranes_** (**_ROM_**): The amniotic sac has ruptured.

**_Spontaneous rupture of membranes_** (**_SROM_**): The amniotic sac has ruptured spontaneously.

**_Prelabour rupture of membranes_** (**_PROM_**): The amniotic sac has ruptured before the onset of labour.

**_Preterm prelabour rupture of membranes_** (**_P‑PROM_**): The amniotic sac has ruptured before the onset of labour and before 37 weeks gestation (preterm).


### Diagnosis

By speculum revealing pooling of amniotic fluid in the vagina. 

When there is doubt about the diagnosis, can test the fluid for: 
- Insulin-like growth factor binding protein 1
- Placental alpha microglobin-1
- Ultrasound may also be useful to show oligohydramnios

### Management

- Admit for observation and monitor for signs of infection - 48 hours to see if labour will start
- Oral erythromycin 250 mg for 10 days or until labour is established
- Antenatal corticosteroids (usually dexamethasone/betamethasone) 12mg IM x 2 
- Consider IV mag sulphate for fetal neuroprotection if <30 weeks and birth is imminent (no blood brain barrier in babies so risk of meningitis)
- **Delivery is recommended at 37 weeks** if there are no other indications for early delivery (chorioamnionitis, fetal compromise)
