People with a genetic predisposition to develop allergic conditions.

Triad of:
* [[Full/Atopic dermatitis (eczema)|Eczema]]
* [[Full/Rhinosinusitis|Allergic rhinitis]]
* Food allergies

Many patients with [[Full/asthma|asthma]] will be atopic.
