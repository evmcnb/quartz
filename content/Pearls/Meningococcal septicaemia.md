**_Meningococcal septicaemia_** is when the meningococcus bacterial infection is in the bloodstream. Meningococcal septicaemia can cause the classic **_non-blanching rash_**

Fluids + IV antibiotics are mainstay of treatment

While dexamethasone is often given for bacterial meningitis to reduce neurological complications, it is contraindicated in meningococcal septicaemia
