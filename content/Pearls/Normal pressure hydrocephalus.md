Reversible cause of dementia seen in elderly patients. Thought to be due to reduced CSF absorption (at the arachnoid villi). 


> [!success] Classic Triad
> - Urinary incontinence
> - Dementia/bradyphrenia 
> - Gait abnormality
>   
>   60% of patients will have all 3 features at the time of diagnosis.

CT - hydrocephalus with ventriculomegaly without/out of proportion to sulcal enlargement 

Need ventriculoperitoneal shunt - 10% have significant complications (seizures, infection, cerebral haemorrhage)
