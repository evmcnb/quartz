---
title: Central Pontine Myelinolysis
tags: [Neurology]
date: 2023-09-27
date modified: 2025-04-23
aliases: [Demyelination of the Pons, Osmotic demyelination syndrome]
---
Destruction of the myelin sheath in the central part of the brainstem. Triggered by the rapid correction of hyponatraemia. The rapid fluid shift out of the brain causes cells to shrink and the destruction of the myelin sheath

Symptoms typically appear 2 to 6 days **after the rapid correction**
 - Speech and swallowing difficulties
 - Motor weakness $\rightarrow$ quadriplegia $\rightarrow$ locked-in syndrome
 - Altered mental status
