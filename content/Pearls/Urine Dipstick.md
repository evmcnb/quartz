#### Nitrites

Suggest **Gram negative** bacteria in the urine (E. coli). These bacteria break down nitrates into nitrites

#### Leukocytes

White blood cells. Normal to have a small number. A significant rise can be a result of infection or other inflammatory cause

#### Red Blood Cells

**Microscopic haematuria** - where bleed is seen on a urine dipstick but not seem when looking at the sample.

**Macroscopic haematuria** - blood is visible in the urine

Common sign of infection but can be present with other causes such as nephritis and [[Pearls/Haemolytic uraemic syndrome]]
