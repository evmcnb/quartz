Intermittent/constant pain the the lower abdo/pelvix going on for 6+ months with no diagonsis.

Often symptoms of IBS or [[Painful bladder syndrome]] (interstitial cystitis)

Allow enough time for history - address psychological/social issues

**Management**
- TV scan
- Trial of [[Pearls/Combined oral contraceptive pill|Combined oral contraceptive pill]]/GnRH analogues
- Antispasmodics
