Inflammation of blood vessels in the temple
## Causes/Factors

- Older age
- Caucasian
- Smoking
- [[Full/Polymyalgia rheumatica]] 
## Presentation

- Severe headache
- Jaw pain
- Scalp tenderness
- Loss of/double vision - can be transient but large risk of permanent loss
- Prominent temporal vessels but no pulse
- Pale optic disc

## Investigations

- Painful temporal arteries of palpation/eating
- CRP, ESR
- Biopsy for definitive diagnosis
- USS halo sign

## Management

- High dose corticosteroids - [[Pearls/Prednisolone|Prednisolone]] (+ PPI and bisphosphonates for protection of stomach and bones)
- Rheumatology referral
