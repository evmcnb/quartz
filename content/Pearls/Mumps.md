Tends to occur in winter/spring. Spread by droplets. Infective 7 days before and 9 days after parotid swelling. Incubation period of 14-21 days. 
- fever
- malaise
- parotitis - pain on eating/earache sensation. Unilateral initially then bilateral in 70% of cases 


**MMR vaccine** prevents 

Management
- Rest
- Paracetamol
- Notifiable disease 

Complications  
- orchitis - uncommon in pre-pubertal males but occurs in around 25-35% of post-pubertal males. Typically occurs four or five days after the start of parotitis
- hearing loss - usually unilateral and transient
- meningoencephalitis
- pancreatitis
