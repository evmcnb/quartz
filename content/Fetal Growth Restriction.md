Fetal weight < 10th percentile for gestational age or **full term** infant < 2500g
*i.e. baby doesn't growth to their genetic potential*

Fundal height should $\approx$ gestation weeks $\pm$ 2 after 20 weeks
## Causes

#### Maternal

- Malnutrition
- smoking
- substance abuse (alcohol)
- diabetes (esp T1DM)
- previous FGR 

#### Placenta

- Gestational hypertension
- [[Full/Chronic Kidney Disease|Chronic Kidney Disease]]
- [[Pearls/Placenta praevia|Placenta praevia]]
- Abnormal cord insertion
- [[Full/Pre-eclampsia|Pre-eclampsia]]

#### Fetal

- multiple gestation
- [[Pearls/Identifying Chromosomal Disorders|Identifying Chromosomal Disorders]]
- [[Pearls/Congenital Conditions|TORCH Conditions]]
- [[Pearls/Oligohydramnios]]

**Type 1** - occurs early in gestation (1st trimester) from a global insult to the fetus. Head and abdomen are both equally small.

**Type 2** - occurs later in gestation (late 2nd-3rd trimester). Caused from placental insufficiency ([[Full/Pre-eclampsia|Pre-eclampsia]]). Abdomen circumference is smaller, as brain is spared. 

## Risks

- Fetal mortality
- Labour/delivery issues (Meconium aspiration syndrome)
- Hypoglycaemia
- [[Pearls/Necrotising enterocolitis|Necrotising enterocolitis]]
- [[Pearls/Developmental Milestones|Developmental Delay]]

## Investigations

Diagnosed via ultrasound. Look at 
- Head circumference
- Abdomen circumference
- Placental assessment: doppler (uterine artery and umbilical artery). Resistance index & pulsatility index
- Fetal movements/tone
- Amniotic fluid volume

> [!success] Treatment
> No real treatment for FGR. The goal is the **maximise gestational age**. 
> - <34 weeks: antenatal steroids
> - <32 weeks: neuroprotection with magnesium sulfate
