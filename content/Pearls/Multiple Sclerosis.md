Relapsing remitting autoimmune condition - ***demyelination***in the ***central nervous system***

Episodes of neurological dysfunction including sensory disturbances, and coordinations difficulties

**Causes**

* Multiple genes
* Epstein–Barr virus (EBV)
* Low vitamin D
* Smoking
* [[MLA/Obesity]]

[[MLA/Optic neuritis]] is the most common presentation of multiple sclerosis. Other eye issues/movements may be present

Based on clinical symptoms supported by MRI and CSF

* Disease modifying drugs - lots of immunomodulators
* High dose steroids for relapses
