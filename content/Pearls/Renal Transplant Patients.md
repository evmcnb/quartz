Renal transplant patients have an increased risk of developing skin cancers due to long-term immunosuppression.

There is a three to five times increase in the risk of developing skin malignancy. It is usually more aggressive than skin cancers occurring in the general population:
- cutaneous [[Full/Squamous cell carcinoma|Squamous cell carcinoma]]
- [[Full/Basal Cell Carcinoma|Basal Cell Carcinoma]]
- Kaposi sarcoma
- malignant [[Full/Melanoma|Melanoma]]. 


The main risk factors are long-term immunosuppression, age, sun exposure, and smoking.
