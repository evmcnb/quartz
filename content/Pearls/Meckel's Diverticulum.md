A congenital diverticulum of the small intestine. A remnant of a duct (omphalomesenteric) that contains ectopic **ileal, gastric or pancreatic** mucosa. 


![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260211115149.png|450]]


> [!success] Rule of 2s
> - Occurs in 2% of the population
> - Is 2 feet from the ileocaecal valve
> - Is 2 inches long

Usually asymptomatic but can present with: 
- Abdominal pain mimicking [[Full/Appendicitis|Appendicitis]]
- Rectal bleeding - **most common cause of painless massive GI bleeding in children aged 1-2**. Often requires a transfusion
- Intestinal obstruction 

**Investigations**
- Can use USS to consider diagosis
- If stable to confirm can consider a Meckel's scan: uses 99m technetium pertechnetate (affinity for gastric mucosa)
- Mesenteric arteriography if unstable. 

Surgical removal if narrow neck or symptomatic.
