A spectrum of disease is recognised:  

- subacromial pain syndrome, previously called impingement syndrome
- calcific tendonitis
- rotator cuff tear
- rotator cuff arthropathy

**Risk factors:**
- age-related tendon degeneration
- repetitive overhead activity
- trauma
- calcific tendonitis

**Features:**
- painful arc of abduction typically 60-120 degrees
- tenderness over the anterior acromion or greater tuberosity
- reduced active movement with preserved passive movement
- Acute weakness of abduction or external rotation may suggest a tear

**Investigations**
- Usually clinical diagnosis
- USS or MRI if suspected tear

**Management**
- Rest in acute phase
- Analgesia ladder
- Physio 
- Consider subacromial corticosteroid injection if pain severe of limiting physio. Usually avoid repetitive injections for risk of tendon damage
- Refer to secondary care if symptoms persist despite 6 weeks of non-surgical treatment
