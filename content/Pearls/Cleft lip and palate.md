title: Cleft lip and palate

date modified: 2025-04-23
---
1:1,000 babies. Most common congenital deformity.
- Isolated cleft lip (15%)
- Isolated cleft palate (40%)
- Combined cleft lip and palate (45%)

Increased risk from maternal use of antiepileptics

Babies may need special orthodontic devices to feed properly. Difficulty with speech - 75% develop normal speech with therapy. Increased risk of [[Full/Otitis media|Otitis media]]

- Cleft lip - repaired at 0-3 months
- Cleft palates - repaired 6-12 months
