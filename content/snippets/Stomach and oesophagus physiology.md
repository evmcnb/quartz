## Stomach

**Secretions**:

| Region | Secretes | Motility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lower oesophageal sphincter + cardia | mucus, HCO3- | * prevention of reflux * entry of food * regulation of belching |
| Fundus + body | H+, intrinstic factors, mucus, HCO3-, pepsinogens, lipase | * reservoir * tonic force during emptying |
| Antrum + pylorus | mucus, HCO3- | * mixing and grinding * regulation of emptying |

**Gastric gland**

|  | * G cell - gastrin secretion **inhibited by**luminal H+, **causes**acid secretion + mucosal growth * M cell - motalin * D cell - somatostatin (paracrine) **inhibits** *gastrin, CCK\*, secretin* |
| --- | --- |

\*CCK, cholecystokinin, inhibits gastric emptying
