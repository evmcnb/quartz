##### Progesterone
- Corpus luteum
- Absolutely necessary in early pregnancy - any damage could terminate the pregnancy

##### Ultrasound

Transvaginal or transabdominal
- 4-5 weeks: gestation sac
- 5-6 weeks: yolk sac
- 6 weeks: fetal pole
Foetus doubles in size every week until 12 weeks
- 6-7 weeks: fetal heart activity - can now determine if the fetus is viable
- 8 weeks: limb buds, fetal movements
- 12 weeks: risk of miscarriage goes down

#### Fetal Growth

##### Small for gestational age

Fetus that has failed to achieve a specific biometric or estimated weight threshold by a specific gestational age
- Constitutionally small - baby is supposed to be small
- Intra-uterine growth restriction - placenta not functioning as it should. Measured on ultrasound:
	- biparietal diameter
	- head circumference
	- abdominal circumference
	- femur length 

**Head sparing** - if the placental is not functioning the body growth will slow but the head will continue to grow

To help determine which need serial measurements - e.g. if the baby is in the 10th percentile and remains there for multiple measurements its likely just a small baby. If the baby is in the 10th percentile and then drops to the 4th percentile that indicates IUGR

In placental insufficiency, less blood goes to the fetal abdomen. So less blood to the kidneys, so less amniotic fluid 

#### Doppler scans

Measures the blood flow in the placental. Helps determine if the fetus is growing appropriately. Uses pulsatility index.
