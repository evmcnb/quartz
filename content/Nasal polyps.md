1% prevalance. 2-4 times more common in men (usually middle-aged). 

Associations  
- [[Full/asthma|asthma]] (particularly late-onset asthma)
- aspirin sensitivity
- infective sinusitis
- [[Full/Cystic Fibrosis|Cystic Fibrosis]]
    - nasal polyps are uncommon in children, and their presence in a child should raise suspicion for cystic fibrosis
- [[Pearls/Kartagener's Syndrome|Kartagener's Syndrome]]
- Churg-Strauss syndrome


> [!success] Samter's triad
> Association of asthma, aspirin sensitivity and nasal polyposis is known as Samter's triad

Features  
- nasal obstruction
- rhinorrhoea, sneezing
- poor sense of taste and smell


> [!danger] Urgent referral criteria
> - Unilateral polyps
> - Bleeding


Management  
- all patients with suspected nasal polyps should be referred to ENT for a full examination
- topical corticosteroids shrink polyp size in around 80% of patients
