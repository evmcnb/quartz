- The LIKELIHOOD of something happening  
- The TIME FRAME in which it’s expected to happen  
- The IMPACT if it did happen

#### To Self

- Self-harm/suicide
- Self-neglect 
- Substance use
- Putting themselves in vulnerable positions

##### Self harm

Taking action, or inaction, to lead to an adverse outcome for themselves  
- Not done with the intent of ending their life  
- It can sometimes lead to accidental death by misadventure
- To convert emotional pain to physical pain
- Stopping people's only coping strategy isn't the best idea. Build up other areas like the underlying cause and alternative coping strategies 

##### Suicidality

Self-harm and suicide attempts can often appear very similar clinically, but  
the intent is different

Dynamic risk factors often include a trigger. 

> [!danger] High Risk Features of an Attempt
> - Lethality of method & their belief around it
> - Degree of planning
> - Final acts - note, wills, letters
> - Preparations to avoid discovery
> - Actions after the act - did they seek treatment? 
> - On-going intent

**Static Risk Factors** 
- Male (lethality of methods, societal expectation)  
- Middle-aged  
- Adverse childhood experiences (ACEs)  
- Family history of completed suicide

**Psychological Risk**
- Hopeless
- Low-self esteem
- Impulsive
- Feeling trapped 

#### To Others

- Neglecting care responsibilities 
- Physical 
- Driving

#### From Others 

- Power dynamic
- Exploitation
