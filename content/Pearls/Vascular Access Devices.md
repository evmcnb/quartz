| Device           | Placement                     | Duration        | Best Used For                                          | Notes                       |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| **PICC**         | Upper Arm (Tip: Heart)        | Weeks to Months | Long-term IV meds, chemo, blood draws                  | Must stay dry               |
| **Central Line** | Neck/Chest (Tip: Heart)       | Weeks to Months | ICU care, frequent, long-term infusions                | Quickest for central access |
| **Port**         | Under Skin/Chest (Tip: Heart) | Months to Years | Long-term, intermittent treatment (e.g., chemo)        | Can get wet                 |
| **Midline**      | Upper Arm (Tip: Shoulder)     | Up to 1 Month   | Short-term antibiotics, hydration, non-irritating meds | Less invasive               |
