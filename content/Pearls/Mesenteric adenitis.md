title: Mesenteric adenitis
date: 2023-09-27
date modified: 2025-04-23
---

Inflamed lymph nodes within the mesentery. Can cause similar symptoms to an [[Full/Appendicitis|Appendicitis]] and can be difficult to distinguish between the two
- Often follows a viral infection
- Pain starts in right iliac fossa
- No anorexia or vomiting
- Higher ferver
- More normal CRP and WBC

Self limiting within 24-48 hours
