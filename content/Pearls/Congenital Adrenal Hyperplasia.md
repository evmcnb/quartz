Autosomal recessive condition that prevents the conversion of progesterone to aldosterone and cortisol. Thus from birth:
- Underproduction of cortisol and aldosterone
- Overproduction of androgens


Presentation varies depending on the specific enzyme deficiency and the severity of the disorder
- Female infants may present with ambiguous genitalia. Males may appear normal at birth
- Salt-wasting crisis (same electrolyte triad as [[Pearls/Addisonian crisis]])
- Precocious puberty - early development of secondary sexual characteristics in both males and females
- Height and growth abnormalities - initial accelerated growth but may have shorter adult stature 


**Diagnosed with ACTH stimulation** - evaluates the adrenal gland's response to ACTH 

Treat with glucocorticoid replacement - in cases of mineralocorticoid deficiency fludrocortisone is also prescribed
