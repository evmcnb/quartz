Was very rare, but more outbreaks are occurring due to the lack of uptake with the vaccine

Overview  
- RNA paramyxovirus
- one of the most infectious known viruses
- spread by aerosol transmission
- infective from **prodrome** until 4 days after rash starts
- incubation period = 10-14 days


##### Features 

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260309202724.png|400]]

**Prodrome**:
- irritable
- conjunctivitis
- fever

- Koplik spots, typically develop before the rash. White spots ('grain of salt') on the buccal mucosa

- Maculopapular rash starts behind ears then to the whole body becoming blotchy & confluent
- Desquamation that typically spares the palms and soles may occur after a week
- Diarrhoea occurs in around 10% of patients

IgM detactable within days of rash onset. 

Management mainly **supportive** - notifiable disease → inform public health

#### Complications 

- [[Full/Otitis media|Otitis media]]: the most common complication
- [[Full/pneumonia|pneumonia]]: the most common cause of death
- [[Pearls/Encephalitis|Encephalitis]]: typically occurs 1-2 weeks following the onset of the illness)
- [[Subacute sclerosing panencephalitis]]: very rare, may present 5-10 years following the illness
- [[Pearls/Febrile convulsions|Febrile convulsions]]
- keratoconjunctivitis, corneal ulceration
- diarrhoea
- increased incidence of [[Full/Appendicitis|Appendicitis]]
- [[Pearls/Myocarditis|Myocarditis]]
