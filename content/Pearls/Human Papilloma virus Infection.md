Most important risk factor for developing [[Full/Cervical Cancer]]. 
- Subtypes 16,18 & 33 are particularly carcinogenic.
- The other most common subtypes (6 & 11) are non-carcinogenic and associated with [[Pearls/Genital warts]].

Infected endocervical cells may undergo changes resulting in the development of **koilocytes**.
