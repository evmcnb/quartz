Defined as more than 80ml of blood loss. Rarely actually measured in practice. Diagnosis based on symptoms, *needing to change pads every 1-2 hours, bleeding more than 7 days and passing large clots*

A diagnosis can be made based on self-report of very heavy periods

## Causes/Factors

- Dysfunctional uterine bleeding (no identifiable cause)
- Extremes of reproductive age
- [[Pearls/Fibroids]]
- [[Pearls/Endometriosis|Endometriosis]] and [[Pearls/Adenomyosis]]
- [[Pearls/Pelvic inflammatory disease|Pelvic inflammatory disease]]

## Investigations

- FBC - performed in all women checking for anaemia
- Pelvic exam with speculum and bimanual, unless there is a straightforward history or young and not sexually active. Assessing for fibroids/ascites and cancers
- Transvaginal ultrasound if symptoms suggest a structural/histological abnormality

## Management

Split into if the patient wants contraception or not

**Does not want contraception**
- Tranexamic acid when there is no pain (Antifibrinolytic)
- Mefenamic acid when there is pain (NSAID)

**Wants contraception**
1. [[Pearls/Intrauterine contraceptive device|Mirena coil]]
2. [[Pearls/Combined oral contraceptive pill|Combined oral contraceptive pill]]
3. Long acting progestogens

Refer to secondary care for further investigations if the treatment is unsuccessful, symptoms are severe or there are large [[Pearls/Fibroids]] (>3cm)
