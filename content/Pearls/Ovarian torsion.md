Partial or complete torsion of the ovary on its supporting ligaments that may compromise the blood supply. If the fallopian tube is also involved its called adnexal torsion 

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260104153310.png|400]]

**Risk factors**
- [[Pearls/Ovarian Tumours|Ovarian Tumours]] - present in 90% of cases as the ovary is heavier than usual 
- Reproductive age
- Pregnancy
- Ovarian hyperstimulation

**Features**
- Sudden onset deep-seated colicky abdominal pain 
- Often history of vigorous activity or sudden increase in abdominal pressure
- N&V
- Fever may be seen in a minority, severe cases
- Vaginal exam may reveal adnexial tenderness

Ultrasound shows **whirlpool sign**

Laparoscopy for diagnosis and treatment
