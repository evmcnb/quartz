> [!success] Classic Triad
> Seen in young children following a diarrhoeal illness
> - [[Full/Acute Kidney Injury|Acute Kidney Injury]]
> - Normocytic [[Full/Anaemia|Anaemia]]
> - Thrombocytopenia

Classically caused by **Shiga toxin-producing E. Coli** 0157:H7 - 90% of cases. **Ask about foreign travel**.
Can also be caused by: 
- Pneumococcal infection
- HIV
- Rare: [[Pearls/Systemic Lupus Erythematosus|Systemic Lupus Erythematosus]], drugs, cancer

Investigations  
- a blood film is the most useful initial diagnostic test
    - remember that in HUS there is a ΜΑНΑ (microangiopathic haemolytic anaemia, i.e. Coombs-negative haemolysis) resulting from intravascular red blood cell fragmentation → formation of ѕϲhiѕtοϲytеs and burr cells
    - anaemia: a haemoglobin level < than 8 g/dL
    - thrombocytopenia
- FBC: anaemia 
- U&E: acute kidney injury, urea and creatinine elevated. 
- stool culture
    - looking for evidence of STEC infection
    - PCR for Shiga toxins

Management: 
- Supportive - fluids, blood transfusion and dialysis if required
- Manage AKI - [[Pearls/Sick Day Rules|Sick Day Rules]]
- No role for antibiotics
- Plasma exchange is reserved for severe cases not associated with diarrhoea
- Eculizumab (C5 inhibitor monoclonal Ab) better for adult atypical HUS
