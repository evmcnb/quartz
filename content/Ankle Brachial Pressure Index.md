$$
ABPI = \frac{\text{Ankle Systolic Pressure}}{\text{Brachial Systolic Pressure}}
$$

| Resting ABPI | Severity of disease                                             |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| >1.4         | Calcification may be present                                    |
| >1.0         | Probably no arterial disease                                    |
| 0.81-1.00    | No signification arterial disease or mild/insignificant disease |
| 0.5-0.80     | Moderate disease                                                |
| <0.5         | Severe disease                                                  |
| <0.3         | Critical ischemia                                               |
