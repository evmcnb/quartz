Common caused by local spread of STIs. 
- Chlamydia trachomatis
- Neisseria gonorrhoeae
- E.col or Kleb pneumoniae in older adults with low-risk sexual history


**Features**:
- Unilateral testicular pain and swelling
- Urethral discharge may be present 

**Most important differential is [[Pearls/Testicular Torsion|Testicular Torsion]]**. This must be urgently excluded 
- Assess for STIs
- Send MSU for MC&S

**Management**
- If STI is the most likely cause, advise urgent referral to local specialist sexual health clinic
	- If organism is unknown: ceftriaxone 500mg IM STAT + doxycycline 100mg BD for 10-14 days
- If enteric organism is the most likely cause: oral quinolone for 2 weeks
