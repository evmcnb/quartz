Needed in around 20% of pregnancies. Indicated in: 
- Prolonged pregnancy, 1-2 weeks after EDD
- Prelabour premature rupture of membranes - membranes rupture but no labour
- Maternal medical problems
	- Diabetic mother >38 weeks
	- [[Full/Pre-eclampsia]]
	- [[Pearls/Obstetric Cholestasis]]
- Intrauterine fetal death

### Bishop Score

Used to assess whether induction of [[Full/Labour]] will be required:

| |**0**|**1**|**2**|**3**|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Cervical position**|Posterior|Intermediate|Anterior|-|
|**Cervical consistency**|Firm|Intermediate|Soft|-|
|**Cervical effacement**|0-30%|40-50%|60-70%|80%|
|**Cervical dilation**|<1 cm|1-2 cm|3-4 cm|>5 cm|
|**Fetal station**|-3|-2|-1, 0|+1,+2|

- A score of < 5 indicates that labour is unlikely to start without induction
- A score of $\geq$ 8 indicates a high chance of spontaneous labour, or response to interventions made to induce it 

### Management

> [!warning] NICE Guidelines
> If the Bishop score is $\leq$ 6
> - vaginal postaglandins or oral misoprostol
>  If the Bishop score is >6
>  - Amniotomy
>  - Intravenous oxytocin infusion 


**Membrane Sweep**
- Involves the examining finger passing through the cervix to rotate against the wall of the uterus to separate the chorionic membrane from the decidua
- Can be done by a midwife at the antenatal clinic. Woman are typically offered this at the 40- and 41- week antenatal visit
- **Regarded as an adjunct** prior to formal labour induction.

**Prostaglandins** - misoprostol (oral), dinoprostone (vaginal)
- Stimulate contraction of the uterine muscles
- Also have a role in ripening the cervix

**Oxytocin infusion**
- Stimulate contraction of the uterine muscles
- Also have a role in ripening the cervix

**Amniotomy**

### Complications

**Uterine hyperstimulation** - the prolonged and frequent uterine contractions - tachysystole
- Intermittent interruption of the blood flow to the intervillous space over time could result in fetal hypoxemia and acidemia
- rarely [[Uterine ]]
- remove the vaginal prostaglandins and stop the oxytocin infusion
- consider tocolysis (drugs that stop contractions)
