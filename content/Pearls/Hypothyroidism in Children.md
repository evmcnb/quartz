### Congenital

Occurs in 1 in 3000 births. Can be the result of: 
- ***Thyroid dysgenesis*** 
- **_Dyshormonogenesis_** (a fully developed gland that does not produce enough hormone)
- **_Pituitary_** or **_hypothalamus pathology_** (rare)

Screened for on the [[Pearls/Newborn Blood Spot Screening Test]]

Where it is not picked up a birth, patients can present with:
- Prolonged neonatal jaundice
- Poor feeding
- Constipation
- Reduced activity
- Slow growth
- Delayed development

### Acquired

[[Full/Hashimoto's thyroiditis|Hashimoto's thyroiditis]] is still the most common



### Management

Investigate with TFTs, ultrasound and thyroid antibodies. 

Levothyroxine taken orally OD. Dose titrated
