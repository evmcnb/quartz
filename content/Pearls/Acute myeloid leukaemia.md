Accumulation and proliferation of immature myeloid cells

* Median presenting age 69
* Features of [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] marrow failure ([[Pearls/Anaemia|Anaemia]], severe opportunistic infections, easy bruising and haemorrhage (thrombocytopenia)), fatigue, gum hypertrophy
* NPM-1 good FLT3-ITD bad
* Treatment with intensive chemo, then [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] marrow transplant in vulnerable, then FLT3 receptor inhibitors

Complications include:

* Bleeding
* [[Full/Neutropenic sepsis|Neutropenic sepsis]] - from chemo, broad spec antibiotics

Poor overall prognosis and worsens with age. Most common type in adults
