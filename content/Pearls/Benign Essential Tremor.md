Common condition associated with older age. It is characterised by a **_fine tremor_** affecting all the **_voluntary_** muscles

Most noticeable in the hands but also in the head, jaw and vocal cords. 


#### Features

- Fine tremor
- Symmetrical
- More prominent with voluntary movement
- Worse when tired, stressed or post caffeine
- Improved by alcohol
- Absent during sleep

### Differentials

Usually diagnosed after excluding other causes:
- [[Full/Parkinson's disease|Parkinson's disease]]
- [[Full/Multiple Sclerosis|Multiple Sclerosis]]
- [[Huntington's Disease]]
- [[Full/Hyperthyroidism|Hyperthyroidism]]
- Fever
- [[Dopamine antagonists]]

### Management

No definitive treatment, not harmful. Doesn't require medication unless affecting a patient's life in some way.

- Propanolol (non-selective bea blocker)
- Primidone (barbiturate anti-epileptic)
