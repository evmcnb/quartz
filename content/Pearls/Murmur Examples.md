### Misc

##### Split S2
(delayed pulmonary valve closure)

![[z_attachments/splits21.mp3]]

Can be:
- Fixed (with regard to breathing) - ASD
- Wide (changes through the respiratory cycle) - aortic stenosis

##### S3

![[z_attachments/s31.mp3]]

- Normal in: young, healthy individuals, well-trained athletes, and during pregnancy, S3 reflects rapid ventricular filling handling increased blood volume.
- Abnormal in: older adults, it often signals systolic heart failure, where a weak, dilated left ventricle can't handle the incoming blood, causing it to vibrate.
