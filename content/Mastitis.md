Inflammation of the breast tissue typically associated with breastfeeding - occurs in 10% of women
- Painful, tender, red hot breast
- Fever and general malaise may be present

#### Management
- **Continue breastfeeding**
- Conservative management: analgesia and warm  compresses

Criteria for medical management if: 
- systemically unwell
- nipple fissure present
- symptoms do not improve after 12-24 hours of effective milk removal
- culture indicates infection

Oral flucloxacillin for 10-14 days - staph aureus most common organism
- Continue breastfeeding at this time
- If left untreated may develop into a [[Full/Breast abscess|Breast abscess]]
