Occurs in 3-6% of all children - presents in the first 3 months of life in formula-fed infants (rarely seen in breastfed infants)

Both IgE mediated and delayed non-IgE mediated reactions are seen 
- Regurgitation and vomiting
- Diarrhoea
- Urticaria, atopic eczema
- Colic symptoms
- Rarely angioedema & [[Pearls/Anaphylactic shock|Anaphylactic shock]]

Diagnosis often clinical, can do a skin prick/patch test

### Management

If child is failing to thrive - **refer to paeds**

**If formula-fed:**
- Extensive hydrolysed formula first line
- Amino acid-based formula with severe CMPI or if no response to above

**If breastfed:**
- Continue breast feeding
- Eliminate cow's milk protein from the maternal diet. Consider calcium supplements after diary is excluded from diet
- Use eHF when breastfeeding stops 

Usually resolves spontaneously
