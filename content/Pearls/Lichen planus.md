Itchy papular rash most common on the palms soles, genitalia and flexor surfaces 

Lichenoid drug eruptions - causes:  
- gold
- quinine
- thiazides

A rash that can affect parts of the body including mouth and hands

* F >> M
* skin involvement 10-15%
* Autoinflammatory disease - T cell immune response
* Potential to transform into oral [[Full/Squamous cell carcinoma]]
* Treat with potent topical steroids
