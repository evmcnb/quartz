## Indications and Dose

Immediate release: 50 mg 4 times a day for 3 days (7 days in males and pregnant women).

## Contra-indications

- eGFR  <45mmol/l
- 3rd trimester of [[Full/Pregnancy|Pregnancy]]

## Side effects

Acute hepatitis and hepatic failure
