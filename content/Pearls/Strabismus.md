**Hirschberg’s Test** used to identify the presence of a squint
- Shine a pen torch at the patient from 1 metre away. 
- When they look at it, observe the reflection of the light source on their cornea
- The reflection should be central and symmetrical
- A deviation from the centre indicates a squint. Make a note of the affected eye and the direction the eye deviates.

**Cover test** is used to identify the nature of the squint: 
- Ask the child to focus on an object
- Cover one eye
- Observe movement
- Cover other eye and repeat test



**Management**

Refer to secondary care

The visual field are still developing up until the age of 8 so can be treated before then. 

Occlusive patch can be used to cover the good eye to force the weaker one to develop
