Thickening and tightening of the palmar fascia. It is more common in older male patients and around 60-70% have a positive family history.

Specific causes include:  
- manual labour
- [[Pearls/Phenytoin|Phenytoin]] treatment
- [[Full/Alcohol-related Liver Disease|Alcohol-related Liver Disease]]
- [[Full/Diabetes Mellitus|Diabetes Mellitus]] (more with type 1)
- trauma to the hand

Ring and little fingers most commonly affected

Consider surgical treatment of Dupuytren's contracture when the metacarpophalangeal joints cannot be straightened and thus the hand cannot be placed flat on the table
