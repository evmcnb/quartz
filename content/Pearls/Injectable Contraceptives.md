Depo Provera is the the main one used in the UK - medroxyprogesterone acetate 150mg.
- Every 12 weeks
- But can be up to 14 weeks after the last dose without any extra pre-cautions
- Is not reversible once given
- Potential to reduce fertility for up to 12 months

Adverse effects: 
- Weight gain
- Irregular bleeding 
- Increased risk of [[Pearls/Osteoporosis|Osteoporosis]]

Contraindicated in [[Full/Breast carcinoma|breast cancer]]
