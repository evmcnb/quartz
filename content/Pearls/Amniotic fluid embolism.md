When fetal cells/amniotic fluid enters the mothers bloodstream and stimulates a reaction.

Very rare - 2 / 100,000

**Presentation**
- Chills, shivering, sweating, anxiety and coughing
- Cyanosis, hypotension, bronchospasms, tachycardia

Diagnosis of exclusion 

Needs critical care outreach - management is predominately supportive
