### Campylobacter Jejuni
Gram -ve spiral shaped bacteria 

- Common cause of travellers diarrhoea
- Most common cause of bacterial GE worldwide
- Spread by raw poultry, untreated water, unpasteurised milk

Incubation period 2-5 days. Symptoms resolve 3-6 days:
- Abdominal cramps
- Diarrhoea
- Vomiting
- Fever 

Consider antibiotics (azithromycin or ciprofloxacin) after isolating the organism, severe symptoms or other risk factors. 

### Shigella

- Spread by faeces contaminated water
- 1-2 days incubation 
- Symptoms resolve within 1 week without treatment
- Camps, fever, bloody diarrhoea
- Can produce Shiga toxin causing [[Pearls/Haemolytic uraemic syndrome|Haemolytic uraemic syndrome]]
- Treat severe cases with azithromycin or ciprofloxacin

### Salmonella 

- Raw eggs or poultry
- 12 hours - 3 days incubation
- Symptoms resolve within 1 week
- Watery diarrhoea +/- blood or mucus, abdo pain and vomiting
- Antibiotics only necessary in severe cases

### Bacillus Cereus
Gram +ve rod

- Grows on food not immediately refrigerated, typically rice
- Toxin cereulide - abdo cramping and vomiting within **5 hours**
- When arrives in the intestines produces a different toxin that causes watery diarrhoea - 8 hours after ingestion.
- All symptoms usually resolve within 24 hours
