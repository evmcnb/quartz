Maturity-onset diabetes of the young refers to any of several **hereditary forms of diabetes** **mellitus** caused by mutations in an **autosomal dominant** gene disrupting [[Pearls/Insulin|Insulin]] production.

* Usually strong family history as autosomal dominant unless de novo mutation
* Typically non-obese and have normal or low body mass index (BMI)
* Glimepiride, a sulfonylurea, stimulates [[Pearls/Insulin|Insulin]] secretion from pancreatic beta cells and can be effective in some types of MODY
* Individuals with MODY generally have some endogenous [[Pearls/Insulin|Insulin]] production preventing DKA under most circumstances
