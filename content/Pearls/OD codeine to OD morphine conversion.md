The conversion factor between oral [[Pearls/Codeine|Codeine]] and oral [[Pearls/Morphine|Morphine]] is to **divide by 10**. This is based on the general consensus that the potency of oral [[Pearls/Morphine|Morphine]] is approximately 10 times greater than that of oral [[Pearls/Codeine|Codeine]]. Therefore, when converting a patient's dose from [[Pearls/Codeine|Codeine]] to [[Pearls/Morphine|Morphine]], you should divide the total daily dose of [[Pearls/Codeine|Codeine]] by 10 to obtain the equivalent daily dose of oral [[Pearls/Morphine|Morphine]].
