Infective-inflammatory collection within the perianal region - often associated with perianal fistulae

Infection and anal gland drainage obstruction from the perianal fistula may lead to an acute perianal abscess

a/w

* Diabetes
* Crohn's disease
* Pelvic infection
* Trauma
* Cancer ± radiation

May resolve spontaneously or require surgical drainage
