[[full/asthma]]-[[COPD]] overlap syndrome (ACOS) is diagnosed when you have symptoms of both [[full/asthma]] and [[COPD]]. The sum of both components if often worse than expected.

ACOS is persistent airflow limitation with several features usually associated with [[full/asthma]] and other with [[COPD]]

Treatment recommendation is to treat the [[full/asthma]] until further investigations are done.- inhaled corticosteroid and add-on LABA and LAMAs.

[[COPD]] - still breathless despite using SABA/SAMA and **[[full/asthma]]/steroid responsive features** → add a LABA + ICS

**Diagnosis**

* Previous diagnosis of [[full/asthma]] in context of [[COPD]]
* Variation if FEV1 of more than 400ml
* Diurnal variability in peak flow more than 20%
* Raised blood eosinophil count

Relatively new concept - not much on it
