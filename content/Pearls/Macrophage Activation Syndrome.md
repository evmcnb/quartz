A life-threatening form of secondary HLH, most associated with **systemic JIA**, adult Still's disease, and SLE. Driven by uncontrolled T-cell/macrophage activation and a cytokine storm.

**Key features (the "falling" picture):**
- **Fever** - persistent, non-remitting (a change from sJIA's usual quotidian spikes)
- **Cytopenias** - falling cell lines; a *dropping* platelet count and *falling* ESR are classic red flags
- **Hyperferritinaemia** - often very high (>10,000 highly suggestive); best single screening test
- **Hepatosplenomegaly** + deranged LFTs (transaminitis)
- **Coagulopathy** - high triglycerides, **low fibrinogen**, raised D-dimer → risk of DIC/bleeding
- **CNS** - irritability, seizures, reduced consciousness

**The paradox to remember:** In active sJIA everything is *high* (ESR, platelets, WBC, fibrinogen). In MAS these **fall** — a "normalising" or dropping ESR/platelet/fibrinogen in a sick sJIA patient is ominous, not reassuring.

Treat as emergency
- IV methylprednisolone
- ciclosporin
- IL-1
