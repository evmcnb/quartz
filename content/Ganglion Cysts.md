Cyst arising from the tendon sheaths or joints. Commonly seen around the dorsal aspect of the wrist. 3x more common in women

- Firm
- Well-circumscribed mass
- Transilluminates 

Management  
- conservative: ganglions often disappear spontaneously after several months
- needle aspiration - but also has a high recurrence rate
- surgical excision is indicated for cysts associated with severe symptoms or neurovascular manifestations. 


> [!warning] Bible therapy
> Used to treat by hitting with a heavy book. 
>- High recurrence rate - the cyst often refills because the underlying stalk/connection to the joint capsule or tendon sheath remains. You rupture the sac but don't fix the source.
>- Collateral damage - a blunt blow heavy enough to burst the cyst can bruise or injure surrounding structures (tendons, nerves, bone), and it's painful.
>- It's just not necessary - safer, more controlled options exist.
