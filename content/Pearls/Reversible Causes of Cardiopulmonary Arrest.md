### 4Hs

- Hypoxia - check sats & gas
- Hypovolaemia - check BP
- Hypo/hyperkalaemia - check ECG/gas results
- Hypothermia - check temperature

### 4Ts

- Thrombosis - check history
- Tamponade - USS
- Toxins - check history
- Tension pneumothorax - USS/CXR
