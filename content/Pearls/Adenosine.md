Used for cardioversion of an SVT (narrow complex tachycardia) (acts on A1 receptors)
- Contra-indicated in asthmatics (use verapamil instead)
- Half life of 10 seconds
- Impending sense of doom
- Facial flushing 

Adenosine also acts on **A2B and A3 receptors on airway mast cells**, triggering degranulation and release of histamine, leukotrienes, and other bronchoconstrictor mediators.
