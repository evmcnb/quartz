Endocrine disorder characterised by hyperandrogenism + [[Pearls/Insulin|Insulin]] resistance

* $\uparrow$ LH $\downarrow$ FSH $\rightarrow$ hyperandrogenism
* Treatment with weight loss and oral contraceptive (cycle regulatio)

Diganosis 2/3 of:

* infrequent/no ovulation
* clinical/biochemical signs of hypoaldosterism (hirutism, [[Full/Acne vulgaris]])
* polycystic ovaries on USS
