Tumour in the **_pulmonary apex_** of the lung 


**Most common symptoms:**
- Pain in the shoulder/neck/chest wall
- [[Full/Horner's Syndrome|Horner's Syndrome]]
- Atrophy in the hand
- Lymphedema in the arm
- Hoarseness 

Unlike other lung cancers, a persistent cough, shortness of breath, or coughing up blood are less common initial symptoms of a Pancoast tumour.
