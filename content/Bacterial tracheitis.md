Bacterial tracheitis is a rare, life-threatening, and severe bacterial infection of the trachea, often affecting children under six, frequently following a viral upper respiratory infection.

It manifests with rapid deterioration, high fever, severe croup-like cough, and thick, purulent secretions that can obstruct the airway
