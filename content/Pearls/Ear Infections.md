**[[Full/Otitis Externa]]** - 90% bacterial, S. aurerus, becomes maligant/life threatening when spreads to skull base

**[[Full/Otitis media]]** - middle ear infection, [[Pearls/Viruses|Viruses]], Strep. pneumoniae, H. influenzae. Can lead to mastoiditis

**[[Full/Pinna Cellulitis]]** - trauma & infection by Pseudomonas aeruginosa & S. aureus
