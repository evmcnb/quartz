| **Disorder / Syndrome**                      | **Alternative Name(s)**       | **Core Feature & Symptoms**                                                                                                  | **Common Causes / Associations**                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Depersonalisation-derealisation disorder** | N/A                           | Feeling separated/outside one's body (depersonalisation) and feeling the world is not real (derealisation).                  | Stress, trauma.                                                                        |
| **Dissociative amnesia**                     | N/A                           | Forgetting autobiographical information, leading to memory gaps.                                                             | Traumatic experiences.                                                                 |
| **Dissociative identity disorder**           | Multiple personality disorder | Lack of clear individual identity; presence of multiple separate identities with unique memories.                            | Severe stress and trauma in childhood.                                                 |
| **Catatonia**                                | N/A                           | Abnormal movement/behaviour (unusual postures, repeating words, or remaining blank/unresponsive).                            | Severe depression, bipolar, schizophrenia; rarely strokes or brain tumours.            |
| **Reactive Attachment Disorder**             | N/A                           | Emotional withdrawal, fearfulness, and inability to form close relationships or respond to affection.                        | Severe neglect and trauma in early childhood (absence of secure attachments).          |
| **Factitious Disorder**                      | Munchausen Syndrome           | Consciously faking or inducing illness to seek medical attention, care, and the "sick role."                                 | N/A (Psychological need for attention/identity).                                       |
| **Hoarding Disorder**                        | N/A                           | Excessive accumulation of possessions and emotional difficulty discarding items, causing severe clutter/hazards.             | Often associated with self-neglect, depression, and anxiety.                           |
| **Alien Hand Syndrome**                      | N/A                           | One hand acts independently with a "mind of its own" (spontaneous touching/grabbing).                                        | Brain lesions (tumours, injuries, aneurysms, post-surgery).                            |
| **Cotard Delusion**                          | Walking corpse syndrome       | False belief (delusion) that the patient is dead or actively dying.                                                          | Depression, schizophrenia, brain tumours, migraines.                                   |
| **Capgras Syndrome**                         | N/A                           | False belief that an identical duplicate/imposter has replaced someone close to them.                                        | Schizophrenia, dementia, other neurological conditions.                                |
| **De Clérambault’s Syndrome**                | Erotomania                    | False belief that a famous or high-status individual is secretly in love with the patient.                                   | Frequently occurs without other underlying psych/neuro diseases.                       |
| **Alice in Wonderland Syndrome**             | Todd syndrome                 | Incorrect perception of the size of body parts/objects, and altered perception of time passing.                              | Migraines (aura), epilepsy, brain tumours.                                             |
| **Koro Syndrome**                            | N/A                           | False belief that sex organs are retracting/shrinking and will disappear, causing panic.                                     | Culturally linked (primarily reported in Asia).                                        |
| **Body Integrity Dysphoria**                 | Apotemnophilia                | Strong feeling that a healthy body part does not belong to them, often with a desire to amputate it or be disabled.          | Not typically associated with other psych/neuro conditions.                            |
| **Foreign Accent Syndrome**                  | N/A                           | Sudden, unexplainable change in voice/accent not connected to previous language experience.                                  | Stroke in the left hemisphere.                                                         |
| **Functional Neurological Disorder**         | Conversion disorder           | Sensory/motor symptoms (weakness, seizures, vision loss) not explained by neurological disease. Not under conscious control. | Underlying psychosocial factors, history of severe stress or trauma.                   |
| **Ekbom Syndrome**                           |                               | Patient believes they have been invested with parasites. Crawling sensation on the skin                                      | Psychological or organic reason such as B12 deficiency                                 |
| **Charles Bonnet Syndrome**                  |                               | Organic syndrome of the elderly brain associated with visual field defect, producing hallucinations                          | Cortical input from other areas fill in the missing information from the visual defect |
| **Fregoli delusion**                         |                               | The patient believes that everyone they meet is the same person but with different disguises                                 | Injury to the right frontal area, left temporoparietal and fusiform gyrus              |
| **Othello syndrome**                         | Delusion jealousy             | Delusional disorder in which an individual believes their partner is unfaithful, despite the absence of proof                | It usually affects males and can result in stalking behaviour or even homicide         |
