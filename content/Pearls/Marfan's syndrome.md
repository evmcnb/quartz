Autosomal dominant connective tissue dissorder - FBN1 gene on chromosome 15

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20251220163834.png|425]]


Features:
- Tall statue with ape index > 1
- Dilation of the aortic sinuses (seen in 90%) $\rightarrow$ [[Full/Aortic aneurysm|Aortic aneurysm]], [[Full/Aortic Dissection|Aortic Dissection]], [[Full/Aortic regurgitation|Aortic regurgitation]] - leading cause of death 
- [[Pearls/Mitral valve disease|Mitral valve prolapse]]
- Repeated [[Full/pneumothorax|pneumothoraces]]
- Upwards lens dislocation 
- Dural ectasia 

- High arched palate
- Pectus excavatum 
- Pes planus (see picture)
