## Introduction

- **Wash hands** (and don PPE if needed)
- **Introduce yourself** (name and role)
- Confirm patient's **name** and **DOB**
- Explain what the examination involves - *"The exam will involve me inserting a small plastic device into the vagina. This will allow me to visualise the neck of the womb and the vagina. It shouldn't be painful but it will feel uncomfortable. You can ask me to stop at any point"*
- Explain the need for a **chaperone**
- Gain consent to continue
- Ask if patient in any **pain** or might be **pregnant before** continuing

**Provide the opportunity to pass urine before the exam**

#### Gather equipment

- Gloves
- Lubricant
- Speculum
- Source of light
- Paper towels
- (swabs)

Wash hands and prepare the speculum with lubricant

Ask the patient to undress and place a blanket over themselves. 

--- 

> [!danger] A gynaecological exam starts with an abdominal exam
> Need to read the OSCE station/ask the examiner for time. Doesn't need to be exhaustive - feel the 9 quadrants well. 


### Positioning

Regain consent to remove the blanket and expose the patient

Need to position the patient correctly before anything else:
> Could you move your feet towards your bottom and then let your legs fall open?



## Vulval exam

#### Inspect the vulva

- Ulcers
- Abnormal vaginal discharge
- Scarring
- Leukoplakia
- Masses ([[Pearls/Bartholin's cyst|Bartholin's cyst]] or [[Pearls/Vulval Cancer|Vulval Cancer]])
- FGM

#### Palpate the inguinal lymph nodes

Identify any lymphadenopathy, can be associated with:
- acute infections
- malignancy


## Speculum

#### Insertion

Warn the patient that you are going to insert the speculum. Remind them they are in control and if they need be to stop at any point tell me. 

1. Carefully **separate the labia**
2. With the blades closed, insert the speculum at 90$\degree$, so the blades are parallel to the labia.
3. Insert the speculum aiming slightly posteriorly - be careful not to open too soon
4. Open the speculum blades until and optimal view of the cervix is achieved
5. Tighten the locking nut if present

### Inspection of the cervix and vaginal walls

- Cervical os - is it open and the patient is pregnant? May indicate [[Pearls/Miscarriage|Miscarriage]]
- Erosions around the os - most often with an [[Pearls/Cervical ectropion|Cervical ectropion]]. But cervical cancer can have a similar appearance 
- Scarring
- Ulceration
- Atrophy
- Foreign body
- Abnormal discharge 

Can take a **high vaginal swab** or **smear** at this point

#### Removing the speculum

1. Hold the blades open whilst the locking nut is loosened
2. Gently remove the speculum whilst slowly closing the blades - do not catch on anything 
3. Cover the patient and explain the procedure is complete

## To Complete the Exam

- **Explain** to the patient that the examination is now **finished**.
- **Thank the patient** for their time.
- **Dispose of PPE** appropriately and **wash your hands**.
- **Summarise** your findings.
