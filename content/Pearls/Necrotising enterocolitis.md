Inflammation of the intestine leading to bacterial invasion causing cellular damage and death which causes necrosis of the colon and intestine. What causes the initial inflammation is unknown.

* 10-15% incidence in low weight babies (<1500g)
* Premature babies (less than 32 weeks) - 80% of cases
* Most common site is ileocolic region

One of the leading causes of death in **premature infants**

- Feeding intolerance 
- Abdominal distension
- Bilious vomiting
- Abdominal distension 
- Bloody stools

Can progress quickly to discolouration, perforation and peritonitis

**Abdominal XR** is the diagnostic investigation - pneumatosis intestinalis (intramural gas) is the hallmark finding. Portal venous gas and possibly free air could also be found

First medical management then if condition worsens surgical laparotomy to remove affected bowel
