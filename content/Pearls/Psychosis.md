---
When a person is experiencing a reality that is fundamentally disconnected from those around them:
- hallucinations
- delusions
- thought disorganisation
	- alogia: little information conveyed by speech
	- tangentiality: answers diverge from topic
	- clanging
	- word salad: linking real words incoherently
- agitation/aggression
- neurocognitive impairment
- depression
- thoughts of self harm 

Peak age around 15-30 years old.

May occur in a number of conditions:
- [[Full/Schizophrenia|Schizophrenia]]
- [[Full/Depression|Depression]]
- [[Full/Bipolar disorder|Bipolar disorder]]
- [[Pearls/Postpartum mental health problems|Postpartum depression]]
- [[Full/Parkinson's disease|Parkinson's disease]]
- [[Pearls/Huntington's Disease]]
- [[Pearls/Corticosteroids|Corticosteroids]]
- Cannabis, phencyclidine
