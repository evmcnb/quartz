Infection of [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] - acute pain swelling, pathological fracture

Acute a/w inflammatory reaction, fulminant, [[Full/Sepsis]]

Chronic - present for >1 month, smouldering acute flares

* Children - S. aureus, S. pyogenes, Kingella, Haemophilus
* Contiguous - Skin (Staph, Strep), enteric (GNB, anaerobes)
* Penetrating - skin flora/ environment
* Sickle cell - Salmonella sp.

Treat with long-term antibiotics and debridement of infected [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] in severe cases

IV flucloxacillin and rifampicin to cover for MRSA and gram -ve.

Early detection and aggressive management are crucial to prevent complications such as [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] necrosis and systemic spread of infection.
