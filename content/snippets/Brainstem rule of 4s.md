**4 structures in the midline beginning with M**

* motor pathway (corticospinal)
* medial lemniscus
* motor nuclei for cranial nerves
* medial longitudinal fasciculus

**4 lateral structures beginning with S**

* Spinocerebellar
* Spinothalamic
* Sensory nucleus of CN V
* Sympathetic pathway

**4 cranial nerves in medulla, pons + above the pons (2 in midbrain)**

* I, II (above), III, IV in midbrain
* V, VI, VII, VIII in pons
* IX, X, XI, XII in medulla

**4 motor cranial nerve nuclei in midline divide into 12 (except I + II)**

* so III, IV, VI, XII
* therefore V, VII, IX, XI are in lateral brainstem
