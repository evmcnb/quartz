Rather than changing intrinsic thoughts (psychological management), change the consequences of their behaviour.

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260225101218.png|500]]

Needs:
- Calm environment
- Consistent approach - between parents, over time
- Boundaries and rules

Warn parents that it may get worse before it gets better when introducing a new management plan. 

- Star charts (positive reinforcement) - decide value, put stars on promptly to associate with behaviour
- Time out (negative punishment) - somewhere neutral (not the child's bedroom), 1 min / year of age.
