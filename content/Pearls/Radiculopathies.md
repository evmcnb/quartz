Spinal roots are compressed or inflamed

* Pain
* Weakness
* Numbness
* Tingling

Commonly due to:

* Disc herniation
* [[Spinal stenosis]]
* Degenerative changes in spine

Symptoms radiate from the spine to the limbs in a specific pattern corresponding to the affected nerve root

Diagnose through exam, MRI/CT and EMG

Treatment may include pain management, physical therapy, epidural steroid [[Full/Injections]], and in some cases, surgical intervention to relieve nerve compression.
