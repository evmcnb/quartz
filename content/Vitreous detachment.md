With age the vitreous gets less jelly-like and more firm - loss of fluid, pulls the vitreous off the posterior wall 
![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20240416103155.png]]
## Causes/Factors

- Male
- Older age
- Humour less firm 
- Myopia (larger vitreal chamber)
- FHx
- Trauma

## Symptoms

- Lots of flashes and floaters
- Painless

## Signs

## Investigations

- Need to rule out retinal involement

## Management

- Quite conservative - brain will compensate to floaters and filter them out
- Surgical treatment can replace the vitreous with an artifical analouge
