Epithelial defect/scratch to the eye. Risk of transforming into [[Infective Keratitis]]
## Causes/Factors

- Trauma

## Symptoms

- Painful watery eye
- Foreign body sensation
- Light sensitivity

## Investigations

- Fluorescein staining

## Management

- Chloramphenicol and lubricating drops
- Safety netting

## Complications/red Flags

- [[Infective Keratitis]]
