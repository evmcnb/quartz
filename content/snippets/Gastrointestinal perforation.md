Four mechanisms that lead to perforation:

1. Ischemia (bowel obstruction, necrosis)
2. Infection (appendicitis, diverticulitis)
3. Erosion (malignancy, ulcerative colitis)
4. Physical disruption (trauma, iatrogenic)
