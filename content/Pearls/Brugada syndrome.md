Autosomal dominant cardiovascular disease which may present with sudden cardiac death (issue with sodium ion channel protein in the heart). More common in Asian populations

- convex ST segment elevation (>2 mm) in 1+ of V1-V3 followed by a negative T wave
- partial RBBB
- changes becomes more apparent after giving flecainide or ajmaline

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260330150739.png]]

**Management**

Avoid things that can precipitate ventricular arrhythmias: 
- **fevers**
- alcohol
- flecanide
- [[Pearls/Lithium|Lithium]]
- Tricyclic [[Full/Antidepressants|Antidepressants]]

ICD implantation
