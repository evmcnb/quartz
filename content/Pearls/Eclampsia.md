Eclampsia refers to the seizures associated with pre-eclampsia

**Magnesium sulphate** is used to both prevent seizures and treat them once they develop.
- Should be given once a decision to deliver has been made
- In eclampsia an IV bolus of 4g over 5-10 minutes should be given followed by an infusion of 1g/hour
- Urine output, reflexes, RR and SpO2 should be monitored during treatment.
	- **Calcium gluconate** is first-line for magnesium sulphate induced respiratory depression
- Treatment should continue for 24 hours after last seizure or delivery (around 40% of seizures occur post-partum)
