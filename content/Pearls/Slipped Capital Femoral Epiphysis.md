aka slipped capital femoral epiphysis

It is where the **_head of the femur_** is displaced (“slips”) along the **_growth plate_**.

The typical presentation is an obese boy aged 8 – 15 years undergoing a growth spurt. There may be some history of trauma 

Presenting symptoms can be vague. These can be:
- Hip, groin, thigh or knee pain
- Restricted range of movement in the hip - **reduced internal rotation of the leg in flexion**
- Painful limp
