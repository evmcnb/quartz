## Birthmarks
Some are permanent some aren't. 

Erythema toxicum - aka baby acne, benign.

| ![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260317163503.png\|425]] | ![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260317163526.png\|175]] |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |

---

Milia - "milk spots". Benign, white or yellowish cysts (1–2 mm) containing trapped keratin

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260317165125.png|550]]


Salmon patches/stork marks (nevus simplex) - common benign. Typically fade within 1-2 years 

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260317165217.png]]

---

Haemangiomas - usually shrink themselves after ~1 year

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260317165256.png]]

---

Port wine stains - common, permanent. Early pulse dye laser treatment 

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260317165353.png|525]]

---

Cafe-au-lait spots - pigmented birthmarks: "coffe with milk". Usually benign, but more than 6 could be a sign of [[Pearls/Neurofibromatosis]] ^f81550

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260317165432.png|475]]

---

Congenital dermal melanocytosis (blue-grey spots) - usually fade by age of 6. 

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260317165542.png|375]]

---

Congenital melanocytic naevi - benign, permanent
