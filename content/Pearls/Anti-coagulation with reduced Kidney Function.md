Guidelines state LMWH may be prescribed with a creatinine clearance of <30mL/min at reduced dose, but they are generally less favourable than **unfractionated heparin** due to the bleeding risk with LMWH.

DOACs at % of kidney function:
- dabigatran 80%
- edoxaban 50%
- rivaroxaban 35%
- apixaban 27%
