May be considered for: 
- myelofibrosis with massive splenomegaly 


Need vaccination against:
- Pneumococcus
- HiB
- Meningococcus

High risk of pneumococcal [[Full/Sepsis|Sepsis]]


Prophylactic antibiotics with **Phenoxymethylpenicillin** (erytho/clarithromycin 2nd line)
