Deposition of calcium pyrophosphate dihydrate crystals (no chance of me remembering that )

Strongly associated with increasing age. People who develop pseudogout at a younger age usually have an underlying risk factor: 
- [[Pearls/Haemochromatosis|Haemochromatosis]]
- [[Pearls/Hyperparathyroid|Hyperparathyroid]]
- low magnesium or phosphate
- acromegaly
- [[Pearls/Wilson's disease|Wilson's disease]]

**Features**
- knee, wrist and shoulders most commonly affected
- **weakly-positively birefringent rhomboid-shaped crystals** on joint aspiration
- Chondrocalcinosis on XR 

NSAIDs or intra-articular, intra-muscular or oral steroids as for [[Full/gout|gout]]
