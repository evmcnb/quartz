Fibromyalgia is a syndrome characterised by widespread pain throughout the body with tender points at specific anatomical sites. The cause of fibromyalgia is unknown.

## Causes/Factors

- 5:1 women:male ratio
- 30-50 years old

## Symptoms

- Chronic pain at multiple sites - sometimes "pain all over"
- Cognitive impairment
- Sleep disturbances
- Other non-specific symptoms - headaches, dizziness etc

## Investigations

> [!warning] American College of Rheumatologists Diagnostic Criteria
> The following are required:
> 1. Generalised pain in at least 4 of 5 regions
> 2. Symptoms have been present at a similar level for at least 3 months
> 3. Widespread pain index (WPI) **≥ 7** and symptom severity scale (SSS) score **≥ 5** **OR** WPI of **4-6** and SSS score **≥ 9**

## Management

The management of fibromyalgia is often difficult and needs to be tailored to the individual patient. A psychosocial and multidisciplinary approach is helpful.   
- explanation
- aerobic exercise: has the strongest evidence base
- cognitive behavioural therapy
- In adults aged 18 and over, medication: pregabalin, duloxetine, amitriptyline
