A syncopal episodes that occurs in response to pain or emotional stimuli. Neurally mediated transient asystole in children with sensitive vagal reflexes
- 6 months - 3 years

Child goes very pale $\rightarrow$ falls to the floor $\rightarrow$ secondary anoxic seizure common $\rightarrow$ rapid recovery

No specific treatment and prognosis is excellent
