Reaction to toxins from Group A Strep (usually *Strep. pyogenes*).
- Spread via respiratory route
- Children aged 2-6
- Incubation of 2-4 days, typically presenting with:
	- fever
	- malaise, headache, N&V
	- sore throat
	- strawberry tongue 
	- rash - sandpaper texture

Antibiotics started empirically not waiting for swab to return

**Management:**
- Oral penicillin V for 10 days - azythromycin for penicillin allergic
- Children can return to school after 24 hours of antibiotics 
- Notifiable disease


**Complications:**
- otitis media: the most common complication
- rheumatic fever: typically occurs 20 days after infection
- acute glomerulonephritis: typically occurs 10 days after infection
- invasive complications (e.g. bacteraemia, meningitis, necrotizing fasciitis) are rare but may present acutely with life-threatening illness
