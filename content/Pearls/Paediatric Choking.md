## Infants (<12 months)

- 1 chance for a finger sweep if object touches bottom lip
- 5 backslaps facing the floor
- 5 **chest** thrusts, place in neutral position
- Repeat until either object removed or becomes unresponsive 
- A&E regardless 

## Child/junior

- Ask to cough if they can
- 1 chance to look in mouth, if anything touches bottom lip can try and remove 
- 5 backslaps over knee 
- 5 abdominal thrusts, hand between umbilicus and xiphisternum 
- Repeat until either object removed or becomes unresponsive 
- A&E regardless
