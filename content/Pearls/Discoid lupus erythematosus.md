Benign disorder generally seen in younger females particularly those with darker skin pigmentation. Very rare for it to progress to [[Full/Systemic Lupus Erythematosus|Systemic Lupus Erythematosus]]

- Erythematous, raised rash sometimes scaly on the face neck ears and scalp.
- May be photosensitive
- Lesions heal with atrophy and scarring (may cause alopecia)
- Serology: negative or low ANA and anti-dsDNA

Manage with topical steroid cream. Hydroxychloroquine second-line. Avoid sun and smoking (smoking increases disease severity and reduces response to antimalarials)
