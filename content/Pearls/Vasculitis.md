| **Vasculitis**                                    | **Vessels** | **Lab Findings**           | **Key Features**                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Henoch-Schonlein Purpura**                      | Small       | –                          | Purpura (non-blanching rash) on extensor surfaces  IgA nephritis  Abdominal pain, arthritis, haematuria                         |
| **Microscopic Polyangiitis**                      | Small       | p-ANCA                     | [[Pearls/Glomerulonephritis\|Glomerulonephritis]], diffuse alveolar haemorrage                                                  |
| **Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis**              | Small       | c-ANCA                     | Nasal symptoms  Respiratory symptoms  [[Pearls/Glomerulonephritis\|Glomerulonephritis]]                                         |
| **Eosinophilic Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis** | Small       | p-ANCA  Raised eosinophils | Late-onset [[Full/asthma]]  [[Full/Rhinosinusitis]] and rhinitis                                                                |
| **Polyarteritis Nodosa**                          | Medium      | –                          | Renal impairment  [[Full/Essential hypertension]]  Cardiovascular events  Tender skin nodules                                   |
| [[Pearls/Kawasaki disease\|Kawasaki disease]]     | Medium      |                            | High fever (more than 5 days)  Widespread rash  Bilateral [[Full/Conjunctivitis]]  Strawberry tongue  Coronary artery aneurysms |
| **[[Full/Giant cell arteritis]]**                 | Large       | Raised ESR                 | Unilateral headache  Scalp tenderness  Vision loss                                                                              |
| **Takayasu’s Arteritis**                          | Large       | –                          | Aortic arch affected  “Pulseless” disease                                                                                       |
