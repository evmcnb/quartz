Shooting stuff down pH goes down, bringing stuff up pH goes up

Prolonged diarrhoea may result in a **metabolic acidosis** associated with **hypokalaemia**

Prolonged vomiting may result in a **metabolic alkalosis** with **hypokalaemia**
