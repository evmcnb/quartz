### Working out Urea:Creatinine easily

Divide the creatinine by 10, if the **urea** is **higher** than creatinine then think **pre-renal** cause.  

  

Eg if creatinine was 123 umol/L and urea was 16.1mmol/L.  

16.1 > 12.3 so consider pre-renal disease.

**Pre-renal** - something causes ↓ BP

* Vomiting
* Diarrhoea
* Diuretics
* Haemorrhage
* Burns
* Renal Artery Stenosis

**Intrinsic** - something within the kidney

* NSAIDS
* Nephrotoxins
* [Glomerulonephritis](https://notes.nosos.co.uk/Glomerulonephritis)
* Vascular

**Post-renal**

* [[MLA/Urolithiasis]]
* Tumours
* Retroperitoneal fibrosis
