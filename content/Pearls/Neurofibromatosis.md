Genetic condition that causes nerve tumors to develop. Benign but can cause structural problems

Two types: NF1 and NF2. Both are inherited via autosomal dominant genes.

|                                | **NF1**                                                                                                                                                                                        | **NF2**                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Incidence**                  | 1 in 4,000                                                                                                                                                                                     | 1 in 100,000                                                                                                          |
| **Gene & protein**             | Mutation in the NF1 gene on chromosome 17 → loss of neurofibromin, a negative regulator of RAS signalling                                                                                      | Mutation in the NF2 gene on chromosome 22 → loss of merlin, which controls Schwann cell growth and contact inhibition |
| **Primary cell type affected** | Affects neural crest–derived cells broadly (Schwann cells, melanocytes, fibroblasts)                                                                                                           | Predominantly affects Schwann cells                                                                                   |
| **Features**                   | Cafe-au-lait spots (>= 6, 15 mm in diameter)  <br>Axillary/groin freckles  <br>Peripheral neurofibromas  <br>Iris hamartomas (Lisch nodules) in > 90%  <br>Scoliosis  <br>[[Pheochromocytoma]] | Bilateral vestibular schwannomas  <br>Multiple intracranial schwannomas, menigiomas and ependymomas                   |
