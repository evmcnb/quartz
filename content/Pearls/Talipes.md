A fixed abnormal ankle position aka club foot. 

- Talipes equinovarus describes the ankle in plantar flexion and supination. (above)
- Talipes calcaneovalgus describes the ankle in dorsiflexion and pronation.

Most commonly idiopathic, but associated with: 
- [[Spina bifida]]
- [[Full/Cerebral Palsy|Cerebral Palsy]]
- [[Pearls/Identifying Chromosomal Disorders|Edwards syndrome]]

**Ponseti method**
- Manipulation and progressive casting which starts soon after birth. 
- Deformity is usually correct after 6-10 weeks
- Achilles tenotomy is required in 85% of cases (can be done under local)
- Night-time braces should be applied until age of 4
