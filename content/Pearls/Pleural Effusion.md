Transudate **tame** - fluids leak from intact vessels so **low protein**

Exudates **extreme** - from pathological capillaries so **high protein**

# Light's Criteria

How to determine what type of effusion it is:

|                         | Transudative                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Exudative                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mechanism               | Capillary hydrostatic pressure                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Capillary permeability                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Protein (pleural/serum) | <0.5                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | >0.5                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| LDH                     | <0.6 or  Pleural LDH < 2/3 upper limit of normal serum LDH                                                                                                                                                                           | >0.6 or  Pleural LDH > 2/3 upper limit of normal serum LDH                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Common Causes           | ***Leaky fluids from intact vessels***<br><br>- [[Full/Heart Failure\|Heart Failure]]<br>- [[Full/Liver Cirrhosis\|Liver Cirrhosis]] (hypoalbuminemia)<br>- [[Full/Nephrotic syndrome\|Nephrotic syndrome]]<br>- Peritoneal dialysis | ***Anything that causes cell death***<br><br>- [[Pearls/Pneumonia\|Pneumonia]]<br>- Malignancy<br>- [[Full/pulmonary embolism\|pulmonary embolism]]<br>- Infections ([[Full/Tuberculosis\|Tuberculosis]])<br>- Inflammatory diseases: [[Full/pancreatitis\|pancreatitis]], [[Pearls/Systemic Lupus Erythematosus\|Systemic Lupus Erythematosus]], [[Pearls/Rheumatoid Arthritis\|Rheumatoid Arthritis]]<br>- Post-cardiac injury syndrome |

## Transudate effusion

* Low capillary oncotic pressure/high hydrostatic pressure
* Low protein & lactate dehydrogenase

## Exudate effusion

* **Pathological capillaries leak too much** → high protein
