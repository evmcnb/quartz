## Introduction

- **Wash hands** (and don PPE if needed)
- **Introduce yourself** (name and role)
- Confirm patient's **name** and **DOB**
- Explain what the examination involves - "Need to carry out a vaginal exam, this involves one hand on the tummy and two fingers into your vagina. It shouldn't be painful but will feel a little uncomfortable"
- Explain the need for a **chaperone**
- Gain consent to continue
- Ask if patient in any **pain** or might be **pregnant before** continuing

**Provide the opportunity to pass urine before the exam**

---


> [!danger] A gynaecological exam starts with an abdominal exam
> Need to read the OSCE station/ask the examiner for time. Doesn't need to be exhaustive - feel the 9 quadrants well. 

#### Preparation

**Gather equipment**
- Gloves 
- Lubricant
- Paper towels

**Patient**
- Explain to the patient that they'll need to remove their trousers and underwear and lie on the couch
- Can do this in privacy then let us know when they're ready 
- They'll have a towel/sheet provided

## ## Vulval exam

#### Inspect the vulva

- Ulcers
- Abnormal vaginal discharge
- Scarring
- Leukoplakia
- Masses ([[Pearls/Bartholin's cyst|Bartholin's cyst]] or [[Pearls/Vulval Cancer|Vulval Cancer]])
- FGM

#### Palpate the inguinal lymph nodes

Identify any lymphadenopathy, can be associated with:
- acute infections
- malignancy

## Vaginal exam

If the patient **consents to continue** the exam:
1. Lubricate the index and middle fingers of dominant hand
2. Carefully separate the labia using non-dominant hand
3. Insert the 2 digits, stacked vertically
4. Once inserted, rotate laterally

5. Palpate the walls of the vagina for any irregularities or masses
6. Palpate the vaginal fornices for any masses
7. Palpate the cervix and try to move it in all 4 directions with the 2 digits

8. Using the non-dominant hand, place it on the abdomen
9. Push upwards with the internal fingers whilst palpating the abdomen (uterus)
10. To the same thing with the internal fingers in the fornixes and position abdo hand around the fallopian tubes 
11. Remove fingers and inspect gloves for blood or discharge
12. Cover the patient and explain that the exam is complete and they can have privacy to get dressed

#### Assessing the uterus

Need to assess the: 
- Position - may be anteverted or retroverted
- Size - should be approximately orange sized
- Shape - may be distorted by masses such as fibroids
- Surface characteristics - smooth or nodular
- Tenderness - suggests inflammation ([[Pearls/Pelvic inflammatory disease|Pelvic inflammatory disease]])


## To Complete the Exam

- **Explain** to the patient that the examination is now **finished**.
- **Thank the patient** for their time.
- **Dispose of PPE** appropriately and **wash your hands**.
- **Summarise** your findings.
