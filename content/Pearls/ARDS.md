Increased permeability of alveolar capillaries leading to fluid accumulation in the alveoli. So basically non-cardiogenic pulmonary oedema.

Causes  
- infection: [[Full/Sepsis|Sepsis]], [[Pearls/Pneumonia|Pneumonia]]
- massive [[Pearls/Blood transfusion|Blood transfusion]]
- trauma
- smoke inhalation
- acute [[Full/pancreatitis|pancreatitis]]
- [[Pearls/COVID 19|COVID 19]]
- cardio-pulmonary bypass


A chest x-ray and arterial blood gases are the key investigations.

Criteria (American-European Consensus Conference)  
- acute onset (within 1 week of a known risk factor)
- pulmonary oedema: bilateral infiltrates on chest x-ray ('**not fully explained by effusions, lobar/lung collapse or nodules'**)
- non-cardiogenic (pulmonary artery wedge pressure needed if doubt)
- pO2/FiO2 < 40kPa (300 mmHg)

Organ support as necessary. Prone positioning may help
