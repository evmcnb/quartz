Tissue at the **_proximal end_** of the **_urethra_** that obstructs urine flow. 

Only occurs in males

Back pressure into the bladder causes:
- Bladder distension
- [[Full/Vesicoureteral reflux|Vesicoureteral reflux]]
- Hydroureter (swelling of the ureters)
- Hydronephrosis (swelling of the renal pelvis and calyces in the kidney)
- Increased risk of [[Full/Urinary Tract Infection|Urinary Tract Infection]]


Severe cases can cause obstruction to urine outflow in the developing fetus, resulting in **_bilateral hydronephrosis_** and **_oligohydramnios_** (low amniotic fluid volume). **_Oligohydramnios_** can lead to underdeveloped fetal lungs (**_pulmonary hypoplasia_**).

### **Management**

Mild cases may be observed and monitored.

A **_urinary_** **_catheter_** can bypass the valve while awaiting definitive management.

Definitive management is **_ablation_** or **_removal_** of the extra tissue during **_cystoscopy._**
