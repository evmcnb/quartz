## Brief Overview

Grave's disease is an **autoimmune** disorder and the most common cause of [[Full/Hyperthyroidism]]. In this condition, the body's immune system mistakenly attacks the thyroid gland, leading to an **excessive production of thyroid hormones**. The overactive thyroid gland results in [[Pearls/Hyperthyroidism|Hyperthyroidism]] and a range of symptoms that affect various body systems.

**_Graves’ disease_** has **_specific_** features relating to the presence of **_TSH receptor antibodies_**:
- **_Diffuse goitre_** (without nodules)
- **_Graves’ eye disease_**, including **_exophthalmos_**
- **_Pretibial myxoedema_**
- **_Thyroid acropachy_** (**_hand swelling_** and **_finger clubbing_**)

## Grave's orbitopathy

- Lid lag
