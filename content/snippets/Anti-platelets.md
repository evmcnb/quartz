### Aspirin

Inactivates platelet cyclooxygenase reducing thromboxane A2.

* Irreversible effect 4-5 days

### P2Y\(\_{12}\) antagonist

Prevent platelet aggregation by blocking P2Y12 receptor

* clopidogrel, ticagrelor, prasugrel
