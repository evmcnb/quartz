Defined as the birth of a dead fetus after 24 weeks gestation. Before that it is a miscarriage. 

Stillbirth is the result of intrauterine fetal death. 
- Unexplained in 50% of cases
- [[Full/Pre-eclampsia]]
- [[Pearls/Placental abruption]]
- [[Pearls/Vasa praevia]]
- Cord prolapse or wrapped around the fetal neck 
- [[Pearls/Obstetric Cholestasis]]
- [[Pearls/Gestational Diabetes|Gestational Diabetes]]
- [[Pearls/Hypothyroid in pregnancy|Hypothyroid in pregnancy]]
- [[Pearls/Infections in Pregnancy|Infections in Pregnancy]]
- Genetic/congential issues

Sensitive **_breaking bad news_** and good **_emotional support_** is essential, provided in an appropriate place by appropriately trained and experienced staff. Counselling is offered to women, partners and family members. They are supported with their individual wishes, such as seeing the baby, naming the baby and keeping photographs (although not persuaded either way with what to do). They are also supported with wishes for funeral arrangements and services.
