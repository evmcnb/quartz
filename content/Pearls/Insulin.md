| **Drug Class**         | Peptide hormone                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mechanism**          | Lowers plasma glucose concentration:   * Glucose transport into fat and muscle cells * Stimulating glycogen synthesis * Inhibiting gluconeogenesis                      |
| **Typical Dose**       | (According to requirements)                                                                                                                                             |
| **Indications**        | * Diabetes type I (and 4th line for TII) * Ketoacidosis * Hyperglycaemic hyperosmotic non-ketotic coma * Emergency treatment of [[Pearls/Hyperkalaemia\|Hyperkalaemia]] |
| **Contra-indications** | Caution in renal impairment                                                                                                                                             |
| **Side-effects**       | * [[Full/Hypoglycaemia]] * Weight gain * Oedema                                                                                                                         |
| **Interactions**       | B-blockers - mask warning signs of [[Full/Hypoglycaemia]]                                                                                                               |
| **Elimination**        |                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Additional Notes**   |                                                                                                                                                                         |

The duration of acting is changed mainly by changing the solubility of the preparation. 

|                                    | **Onset** | **Peak**     | **Duration**   | Examples                           |
| ---------------------------------- | --------- | ------------ | -------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **Rapid-acting insulin analogues** | 5 mins    | 1 hour       | 3-5 hours      | insulin aspart, insulin lispro     |
| **Short-acting insulin**           | 30 mins   | 3 hours      | 6-8 hours      | Actrapid, Humulin S                |
| **Intermediate-acting insulin**    | 2 hours   | 5-8 hours    | 12-18 hours    | isophane insulin                   |
| **Long-acting insulin analogues**  | 1-2 hours | Flat profile | Up to 24 hours | insulin determir, insulin glargine |
