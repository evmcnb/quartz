## 2 Week Wait Referral/Bad news

Sometimes need to a patient why you are referring them urgently on a suspected cancer pathway _without_ definitively telling them they have cancer. **Be careful to dial up or down the SPIKES depending of the severity of things**

1. Introduces self & check patient details
2. (**SP**) Establishes** prior knowledge (ICE) and clarifies setting for the conversation
3. Asks patient if they'd like to know the details/result. Offer to calk in family members 
4. Warning shot - "I understand you had some tests and we were concerned that this might be something serious. I wanted to talk to you about the results and I'm afraid it wasn't the news we were hoping for."
5. Delivers news directly - call things what they are
6. Empathy/responding emotions - "I can see how hard that would be", "I can appreciate you've got a lot to balance"
7. Chunk and check
8. Explaining management/next steps - 2WW appointment received within next week for within 2 weeks $\rightarrow$ CT/imaging/biopsy $\rightarrow$ MDT referral $\rightarrow$ appointment with cancer specialist to start treatment
9. Safety net - if no appointment within a week get back in contact, shortness of breath/ other specific cancer red flags seek medical attention
10. Checks understanding of news 
11. Offers ongoing help & support from appropriate services 

## DNACPR Discussion

Either directly with the person or a relative. Next of kin should be involved.

- When the risks of CPR outweigh the benefits - medical decision 
- 20% survive to hospital discharge post-arrest across all age brackets, for elderly/frailer individuals, the rates are much lower. 
- Having the conversation does not mean we expect them to decline rapidly, just planning for the future
- An important exception to a DNACPR is a cardiac arrest caused by an **unanticipated and easily reversible cause** such as choking or anaphylaxis

#### Explanation

1. Open the conversation
2. Explore current understanding of health state and of DNACPR/resuscitation
3. Explain what cardiac arrest is and resuscitation - invasive procedure with a low success rate 
4. Explain what DNACPR is and why is appropriate - CPR works best on reversible causes, the heart and body need to be strong enough to recover afterwards. Its not giving up but rather allowing a dignified death. 
5. Reassure that it only applies to CPR not other forms of treatment unless already discussed
6. Answer any questions 
## Discharge planning

- ICE early
- Questions about preference - home, care home? 
- Whether needs package of care, how frequently, ? fast track 
- Who pays for carers? If needs are primarily health-based including terminal illness the NHS pays the full cost whether in a care home or at home
- Mobility assessment - able to manage stairs? Can walk around unaided? How far?
	- Physio review?
	- Mobility aids
- Occupational therapy assessment - where is the bathroom & bedroom? 
	- Stair lift
	- Meals on wheels? 
- Palliative care/GP follow up 
	- Community palliative care team
	- Gold standards framework at the GP 
- Advanced care planning - decided about resuscitation? RESPECT form? What ceiling of care is wanted? Is that easily available
- Anticipatory medications if appropriate


## Starting Antidepressants

- Optimise social situation - alcohol, drugs, job stresses, home life, uni/school
- Self help/lifestyle changes
- SSRIs take 2-4 weeks to begin to work - within the first week can make feelings of suicide worse. **Need to seek help if this is the case**
- Common side effects - GI upset. Need to remember to take them otherwise can get "discontinuation symptoms" - sweating, diarrhea, mood changes, paresthesia etc 
- Follow up appt in 2 weeks unless under 25 then 1 week
- Need to continue antidepressants for at least 6 months from the point at which they start feeling better 


## Driving Advice


> [!tldr] Doctors Responsibilities
> Doctors and other healthcare professionals should:
> - **Advise** the individual on the impact of their medical condition for safe driving ability
> - **Advise** the individual on their legal requirement to notify DVLA of any relevant condition
> - **Treat**, **manage** and **monitor** the individual’s condition with ongoing consideration of their fitness to drive
> - **Notify DVLA directly** of an individual’s medical condition or fitness to drive, where they cannot or will not notify DVLA themselves

Need to support them around work if HGV driver to transition to a different role whilst cannot drive. Can provide letters of support for that.
