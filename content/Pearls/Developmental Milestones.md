## Gross Motor

**Remember**: head to toe:

| Age       | Milestone                                                                       |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 months  | Good head movement (lifts head but can't roll over)                             |
| 4 months  | Good chest and trunk movement (rolls over but can't sit up)                     |
| 6 months  | Good hip movement (sits up but can't crawl)                                     |
| 9 months  | Good upper leg movement (crawls but doesn't walk)                               |
| 12 months | Good knee and feet movement (can walk but fine motor for feet still developing) |

**To expand a bit on the key areas:**
- Sits without support at 7-8 months (refer at 12)
- Walks unsupported at 13-15 months (refer at 18 months)
- The majority of children crawl but some bottom shuffle. This is a normal variant 

## Fine Motor

| **Age**   | **Milestone**                                                                                                                                     |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 3 months  | Reaches for object  <br>Holds rattle briefly if given to hand  <br>Visually alert, particularly human faces  <br>Fixes and follows to 180 degrees |
| 6 months  | Holds in palmar grasp. <br>Pass objects from one hand to another  <br>Visually insatiable, looking around in every direction                      |
| 9 months  | Points with finger  <br>Early pincer                                                                                                              |
| 12 months | Good pincer grip  <br>Bangs toys together                                                                                                         |
Hand preference before 12 months is abnormal and may indicate cerebral palsy

## Speech and hearing

Look at the number of letters for each sound 

| Age       | Milestone                                |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 2 months  | Co (cooing and making sounds)            |
| 4 months  | Haha (laughing)                          |
| 6 months  | Babble (double syllables)                |
| 9 months  | Mommy dada (understands no)              |
| 12 months | 1+ word (knows and responds to own name) |
|           | *pattern breaks down now*                |
| 2 years   | Combine two words                        |
| 2.5 years | Vocabulary of 200 words                  |
| 3 years   | Talks in short sentences                 |
| 4 years   | Asks why, when and how questions         |

## Social

**Look at the first letter**

| Age       | Milestone                                  |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| 2 months  | Twinkles (smiles, refer at 10 weeks)       |
| 4 months  | Focuses on sound                           |
| 6 months  | Stranger anxiety                           |
| 9 months  | Name (responds, tales everything in mouth) |
| 12 months | Told commands (responds, waves bye-bye)    |
**Play**

| Age       | Milestone                        |
| --------- | -------------------------------- |
| 9 months  | Plays 'peek-a-boo'               |
| 18 months | Plays contentedly alone          |
| 2 years   | Plays near others, not with them |
| 4 years   | Plays with other children        |

- Primary dentition at 6 months
- Same time to start weening

## Reflexes

| **Reflex**                                | **What It Looks Like**                                                                                                                              | **When It Typically Disappears** |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Rooting Reflex**                        | When the corner of the baby's mouth or cheek is stroked, they turn their head and open their mouth to "root" for a breast or bottle.                | **4 months** (becomes voluntary) |
| **Moro (Startle) Reflex**                 | In response to a sudden sound or movement, the baby throws back their head, extends their arms and legs, cries, and then pulls their limbs back in. | **4 to 6 months**                |
| **Palmar Grasp**                          | When an object is placed in the baby's palm, their fingers curl around it tightly.                                                                  | **5 to 6 months**                |
| **Asymmetric Tonic Neck (Fencer's Pose)** | When the baby's head is turned to one side, the arm on that side stretches out, and the opposite arm bends at the elbow.                            | **5 to 7 months**                |
| **Plantar Grasp**                         | When the sole of the foot is stroked just below the toes, the toes curl downward.                                                                   | **9 to 12 months**               |
| **Babinski Reflex**                       | When the bottom of the foot is stroked firmly from heel to toe, the big toe bends backward and the other toes fan out.                              | **1 to 2 years**                 |

## Red flags

There are certain red flags for things that would suggest there is a problem:
- Lost developmental milestones
- Persisting primitive reflexes
- Not holding an object by 6 months
- Not sitting unsupported by 9 months
- Not standing independently by 12–15 months
- Not walking by 15 months in girls or 18 months in boys ([[Full/Cerebral Palsy|Cerebral Palsy]], [[Pearls/Muscular dystrophy|Duchennes muscular dystrophy]], or could be normal with bottom shuffling)
- Not running by 2 years
- No words by 18 months
- No interest in others by 12 months
