Emergency cause of [[Pearls/Sore throat|Sore throat]] - life threatening.
Notifiable disease

Epiglottitis was generally considered a disease of childhood (2-7 years) but in the UK it is now more common in adults due to the immunisation programme
## Causes/Factors

- ***H. influenzae type b*** the most common cause
- Other causes include strep pneumoniae and Group A Strep

## Presentation

- ==Very acute onset==
- Fever
- Stridor
- Drooling
- Look unwell

Tripod position - finds it easier to breath leaning forward extending their neck in a seated position

## Diagnostic Tests

**Do not examine the throat of anyone with suspected epiglottitis**

Diagnosis is made by direction visualisation only be senior airway trained staff.

XRs may be done - particularly if there is a concern for a foreign body in children:
- A PA view will show subglottic narrowing - steeple sign
- A lateral view will show swelling of the epiglottis - thumb sign

## Management

- immediate senior involvement- anaesthesia and ENT
- A-E- secure airway - endotracheal intubation
- [[Pearls/Oxygen|Oxygen]]
- IV antibiotics (ceftriaxone)
- ? IV steroids
- ? nebulised adrenaline
