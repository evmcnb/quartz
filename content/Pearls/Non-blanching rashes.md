Caused by bleeding under the skin. Petechiae small (<3mm), purpura larger (3-10mm). Most concerning differential is [[Pearls/Meningococcal septicaemia|Meningococcal septicaemia]]

### Differential diagnosis

- [[Pearls/Meningococcal septicaemia|Meningococcal septicaemia]]
- [[Pearls/Henoch-Schonlein purpura]]
- [[Pearls/Idiopathic Thrombocytopenic Purpura|Idiopathic Thrombocytopenic Purpura]]
- Acute leukaemias ([[Pearls/Acute myeloid leukaemia|Acute myeloid leukaemia]]/[[Pearls/Acute lymphoblastic leukaemia|Acute lymphoblastic leukaemia]])
- [[Pearls/Haemolytic uraemic syndrome|Haemolytic uraemic syndrome]]
- Mechanical 
- Traumatic (Non-accidental injuries)
- Viral illness (flu)


Always require urgent referral and investigation
