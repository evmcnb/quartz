A severe primary skin infection by HSV1/2. More commonly seen in children with [[Full/Atopic dermatitis (eczema)|Atopic dermatitis (eczema)]] often presenting as a rapidly progressing painful rash

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260204105815.png|375]]

- Monomorphic punched out erosions
- Circular
- Depressed
- Ulcerated lesions
- 1-3mm in diameter 


**Potentially life threatening** - children should be admitted for IV aciclovir.
