HbA1c depends on average blood glucose concentration and red blood cell (RBC) lifespan. Sickle-cell anaemia and other haemoglobinopathies such as hereditary spherocytosis and G6PD deficiency **reduce RBC lifespan** and hence can **artificially lower HbA1c levels.**

Average glucose levels are likely to be more accurate as they are calculate from more data over longer period of time
