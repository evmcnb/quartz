Comprise both the conventional copper IUDs and levonorgestrel releasing IUS (e.g. mirena)

IUS is also used in the management of menorrhagia

Both are more than 99% effective

Risk of expulsion around 1 in 20 and most likely to occur within the first 3 months. 
### IUD

- Prevent fertilisation by causing decreased sperm motility and survival
- Can be relied upon immediately following insertion
- Effective for 5 years, if they have copper on the arms as well as the stem, can be effective for 10 years
- Make periods heavier, longer and more painful 
- Contraindicated in [[Pearls/Wilson's disease|Wilson's disease]]

### IUS

- Prevents endometrial proliferation and causes cervical mucous thickening
- Can be relied upon after **7 days**
- Most common IUS is effective for 8 years or until 55 years old
- Associated with inital frequent uterine bleeding and splotting

NSAIDs may be used to help with discomfort after the procedure. Women need to be seen 3 to 6 weeks after insertion to check the threads

Before the coil is removed, women need to abstain from sex or use condoms for 7 days, or there is a risk of pregnancy. The strings are located and slowly pulled to remove the device.
