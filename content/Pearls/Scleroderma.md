Autoimmune CTD characterised by the triad of **vasculopathy, autoimmunity, and fibrosis** (skin + internal organs). Female predominance, peak onset 30–50.

### Subtypes

**Limited cutaneous SSc (lcSSc)** 
- skin involvement distal to elbows/knees + face. 
- Raynaud's may be first sign
- Includes the **CREST** picture: **C**alcinosis, **R**aynaud's, o**E**sophageal dysmotility, **S**clerodactyly, **T**elangiectasia. 
- Slower course; pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) is the key late risk. Antibody: **anti-centromere**.

**Diffuse cutaneous SSc (dcSSc)** 
- proximal + truncal skin involvement, rapid progression. 
- Higher risk of ILD, cardiac disease, and scleroderma renal crisis. The most common cause of death is now respiratory involvement
	- patients with renal disease should be started on an ACE inhibitor - captopril is typically used due to its rapid onset and short half-life, allowing for dose titration
- Antibody: **anti-Scl-70 (topoisomerase I)**; **anti-RNA polymerase III** (renal crisis + malignancy risk).
- Poor prognosis

**Scleroderma** - without internal organ involvement
- tightening and fibrosis of skin 
### Key clinical features
- **Raynaud's phenomenon** - usually the earliest sign, often precedes other features by years; digital ulcers/pitting
- **Skin** - sclerodactyly, tightening, "salt-and-pepper" pigmentation, reduced oral aperture, beaked nose
- **GI** - oesophageal dysmotility/reflux (very common), gastric antral vascular ectasia (GAVE, "watermelon stomach"), bacterial overgrowth
- **Lung** - **ILD** (esp. dcSSc/Scl-70) and **PAH** (esp. lcSSc) — now the leading causes of death
- **Renal** - **scleroderma renal crisis**: acute hypertension + AKI; risk ↑ with dcSSc, anti-RNA pol III, and corticosteroids

### Investigations
Nailfold capillaroscopy (dilated loops, dropout), ANA (usually +ve) with SSc-specific antibodies, HRCT + PFTs (ILD), annual echo + BNP (PAH screening), BP/renal monitoring.

### Management
Organ-based, not a single therapy:
- **Raynaud's/digital ulcers** - CCBs, PDE-5 inhibitors, iloprost, bosentan
- **ILD** - mycophenolate, cyclophosphamide, nintedanib, rituximab
- **PAH** - endothelin antagonists, PDE-5 inhibitors, prostacyclins
- **Renal crisis** - **ACE inhibitors** (life-saving; do NOT stop even in AKI). MOST IMPORTANT TO KNOW FOR MLA
- **GI** - PPIs, prokinetics
- **Skin/diffuse disease** - methotrexate, MMF; **avoid high-dose steroids** (renal crisis risk)
