## Alpha Receptors:

* Generally found on smooth muscle cells.
* Subtypes: α1 and α2.
* α1 receptors primarily mediate smooth muscle contraction.
* α2 receptors mostly inhibit neurotransmitter release, leading to relaxation of smooth muscle.

## Beta Receptors:

* Predominantly located on cardiac muscle cells and some smooth muscle cells.
* Subtypes: β1, β2, and β3.
* β1 receptors primarily stimulate cardiac muscle contraction and increase heart rate.
* β2 receptors mainly induce smooth muscle relaxation, particularly in bronchi and blood vessels.
