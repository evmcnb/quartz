Non-diabetic euglycaemic form of ketoacidosis. Often alcoholics will not eat regularly and may vomit food that they do eat, leading to episodes of starvation. 

It typically presents with a pattern of:  
- Metabolic acidosis
- Elevated anion gap
- Elevated serum ketone levels
- Normal or low glucose concentration

The most appropriate treatment is an infusion of saline & thiamine. Thiamine is required to avoid Wernicke encephalopathy or Korsakoff psychosis.
