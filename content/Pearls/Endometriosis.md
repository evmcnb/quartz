* Ectopic endometrial tissue
* Bleeding into tissues $\rightarrow$ fibrosis
* Oesteogen dependent

* Laparoscopy is the gold standard treatment
* Surgical ablation of lesions is definitive treatment

**Features:**
- Chronic pelvic pain
- Secondary dysmenorrhoea 
- Deep dyspareunia
- Non-gynaecological: urinary symptoms, dysuria, urgency, haematuria, dyschezia
- IBS like symptoms

**Sites**
- Peritoneum
- Pouch of douglas
- Ovary/tubes
- Ligaments
- Bladder

Management depends on clinical features - poor correlation between laparoscopic findings and severity of symptoms. 
- NSAIDs $\pm$ paracetamol 
- If analgesia doesn't help, hormonal therapies such as [[Pearls/Combined oral contraceptive pill|Combined oral contraceptive pill]]
	- Dienogest - progestogen licensed for endometriosis
	- Mirena IUS

If analgesia & hormone treatment doesn't help or fertility is wanted, the patient should be referred to secondary care. Treatments include:
- GnRH analogues 
- Surgery - laparoscopic excision/ablation for those trying to conceive
