Type of microangiopathic haemolytic [[Pearls/Anaemia|Anaemia]], antibodies against ADAMTS13 enzyme. So vWF cannot be broken down leading to many clots

* aquired
* fragments of vWF slice up RBCs and vessels
* treat with plasma exchange to remove antibody and prevent thrombosis with [[Pearls/Aspirin|Aspirin]] and LMWH
