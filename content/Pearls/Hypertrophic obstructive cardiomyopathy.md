**Autosomal dominant** disorder of muscle tissue (beta-myosin heavy chain, MYH7 . Most common cause of sudden cardiac death in the young.
- Predominantly diastolic dysfunction - decreased compliance
- Myocytes try to build more and repair - fibrosis and disorganised

Features: 
- Often asymptomatic 
- Exertional dyspnea
- Syncope
- Sudden death
- Systolic murmurs - ejection systolic murmur (due to left ventricular outflow tract obstruction). Increases with valsalva manoeuvre and decreases on squatting. Mitral regurg

Associated with
- [[Pearls/Wolff-Parkinson White]] 

ECG: 
- Left ventricular hypertrophy 
- Non-specific ST segment and T-wave abnormalities
- Deep Q waves
