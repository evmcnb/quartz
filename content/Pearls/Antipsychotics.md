Used in the management of schizophrenia and other forms of psychosis, mania and agitation. Divided into typical (first generation) and atypical (second generation)

The atypical antipsychotics were developed due to the problematic [[Pearls/Extrapyramidal Side-effects]] which are associated with the first generation

|                         | Typical antipsychotics                                                                          | Atypical antipsychotics                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mechanism of action** | Dopamine D2 receptor antagonists, blocking dopaminergic transmission in the mesolimbic pathways | Act on a variety of receptors (D2, D3, D4, 5-HT)                                                                                        |
| **Adverse effects**     | Extrapyramidal side-effects and hyperprolactinaemia common. Prolonged QT                        | Extrapyramidal side-effects and hyperprolactinaemia less common  <br>Metabolic effects (weight gain, glucose intolerance, dyslipidemia) |
| **Examples**            | Haloperidol  <br>Chlorpromazine                                                                 | Clozapine  <br>Risperidone  <br>Olanzapine                                                                                              |

### Common side effects

- Anti-cholinergic - dry mouth, blurred vision, urine retention, constipation
- Sedation (anti-histamine)
- Elevated prolactin - amenorrhoea/galactorrhoea/sexual dysfunction/gynaecomastia/osteoporosis
- Metabolic side-effects
- Prolonged QTc

All can cause [[Pearls/Neuroleptic malignant Syndrome|Neuroleptic malignant Syndrome]]


In elderly patients:
- Increased risk of [[Full/Stroke|Stroke]]
- Increase risk of [[Full/Deep Vein Thrombosis|Deep Vein Thrombosis]]

### Selection

Anti-psychotics can be given orally or by depot injection. Start off orally and if concern of adherence can switch to depot injection. 2nd generation drugs can cause 1st generation side effects at high enough doses (looses selectivity).


| Drug           | Generation               | Advantage                                                                                                                             | Disadvantage                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Chlorpromazine | 1st (typical)            | Sedative properties, used for intractable hiccups                                                                                     | Anticholinergic effects                                                                                                                                   |
| Haloperidol    | 1st (typical)            | Effective suppressing hallucinations and delusions **without** sedation                                                               | High risk of [[Pearls/Extrapyramidal Side-effects]]. QT prolonged                                                                                         |
| Olanzapine     | 2nd (atypical)           | High efficacy (second only to clozapine)                                                                                              | Metabolic side effects                                                                                                                                    |
| Quetiapine     | 2nd (atypical)           | Very low risk of [[Pearls/Extrapyramidal Side-effects]] - can be used in those with [[Full/Parkinson's disease\|Parkinson's disease]] | Can be too sedating                                                                                                                                       |
| Risperidone    | 2nd (atypical)           | High efficacy for positive symptoms                                                                                                   | Hyperprolactinemia                                                                                                                                        |
| Clozapine      | 2nd (atypical)           | Most effective anti-psychotic                                                                                                         | Risk of [[Pearls/Agranulocytosis]] $\rightarrow$ death, lower seizure threshold, [[Pearls/Myocarditis\|Myocarditis]], constipation, sedation, weight gain |
| Aripiprazole   | 3rd (partial antagonist) | Fewer metabolic side effects - unlikely to gain weight                                                                                | Insomnia, anxiety, headache some anticholinergic side effects. Can cause akathisia                                                                        |

### Monitoring 

High risk of relapse if stopped before 6 months. After a first episode should be continued for a year. After 2+ episodes continue long term. This is on top of clinical follow-up that patients require.

| **Test**                                                                                                  | **Frequency**                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Full blood count (FBC), urea and electrolytes (U&E), liver function tests (LFT), thyroid function (TFT),, | - at the start of therapy<br>- annually<br>- clozapine requires much more frequent monitoring of FBC (initially weekly) |
| Lipids, weight                                                                                            | - at the start of therapy<br>- at 3 months<br>- annually                                                                |
| Fasting blood glucose, prolactin, HbA1c                                                                   | - at the start of therapy<br>- at 6 months<br>- annually                                                                |
| Blood pressure                                                                                            | - baseline<br>- frequently during dose titration                                                                        |
| Electrocardiogram                                                                                         | - baseline                                                                                                              |
| Cardiovascular risk assessment                                                                            | - annually                                                                                                              |

### Overdose 

- CNS depression 
- Hypotension
- Tachycardia
- QTc prolongation 

Supportive treatment with fluids and activated charcoal.
