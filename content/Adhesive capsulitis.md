Common cause of shoulder pain. Aetiology not understood. Inflammation and fibrosis of the glenohumeral joint. 

- middle aged females
- [[Full/Diabetes Mellitus|Diabetes Mellitus]] - 20% will have an episode


Features: 
- **External rotation** restriction
- On both active and passive movement 
- Typically have a painful freezing phase, an adhesive phase and a recovery phase. 
- Bilateral in 20%
- Episode typically lasts 6 months to 2 years

Clinical diagnosis. No intervention has been shown to improve outcome
- NSAIDs
- Physio
- Oral corticosteroids
- Intra-articular corticosteroids
