Nutrition via gastrointestinal tract - enteral feeding

* Mouth
* NG
* Percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy (PEG) - tube from the surface of abdo to stomach

Total parenteral nutrition (TPN) is supplying full nutritional requirements using an IV infusion. Solution of carbs, fats, proteins and minerals. **Very irritant to veins** and can cause thrombophlebitis so normally given through a **central line**

1kg of muscle = 200g of protein = 120g of glucose

Nitrogen loss ~ 60-70g/day but up to 300g

**Phase 1** - shock

**Phase 2** - hyper-catabolism, protein turnover

**Phase 3** - anabolic phase - 3-8 days after uncomplicated surgery
