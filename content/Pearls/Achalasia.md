Dysphagia, regurgitation, no weight loss → ?achalasia

Achalasia is caused by the degenerative loss of ganglia from Auerbach's plexus, resulting in failure of oesophageal peristalsis and impaired relaxation of the lower oesophageal sphincter. This leads to functional obstruction at the gastro-oesophageal junction with proximal dilatation of the oesophagus. The regurgitation of undigested food, sometimes hours after eating, is particularly characteristic of achalasia as food accumulates in the dilated oesophagus.
