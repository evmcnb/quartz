Abnormal flexion of the fingers caused by a thickening of the tendon or tightening of the sheath, preventing the tendon from moving smoothly through the pulley. 

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20261016132726.png]]
An issue on the flexor tendons preventing extension

Most commonly around the A1 pulley in the thumb, middle or ring finger. A nodule may be felt around this area

Management  

- steroid injection is successful in the majority of patients. A finger splint may be applied afterwards
- surgery should be reserved for patients who have not responded to steroid injections
