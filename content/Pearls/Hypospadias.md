Hypospadias is characterised by  
- a ventral urethral meatus
- a hooded prepuce
- chordee (ventral curvature of the penis) in more severe forms
- the urethral meatus may open more proximally in the more severe variants. However, 75% of the openings are distally located.

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20251216180337.png|425]]

Associated with [[Pearls/Cryptorchidism|Cryptorchidism]] in 10% of cases and [[Full/Hernias|inguinal hernia]]

Management:
- Refer to specialist - corrective surgery is performed when the child is ~12 months
- Essential child is not circumcised prior to the surgery
