Need to tailor to the specific age of the child and situation. In general: 
- Do not make assumptions about caregivers - clarify who is in the room
- Include the child as much as possible
- If the child is older **identify if the child wants the parent in the room**
## Introduction

- **Wash hands** (and don PPE if needed)
- **Introduce yourself** (name and role)
- Confirm patient's **name** and **DOB**
- Explain what history you'd like to take
- Gain consent to continue

## Presenting complaint
- Start with open questions: "How can I help today?"
- Allow patient to talk and ask 1/2 more follow up open questions

## History of complaint
Gather further details about the issue using SOCRATES

#### Site
#### Onset
#### Character
#### Radiation
#### Associated symptoms
#### Timeframe
#### Exacerbating factors
#### Severity

## Ideas concerns and expectations
You often get a mark in OSCE just for this. Useful insight into the patients mind and allow them to say anything that's bothering them

- **Ideas** - "Do you have any thoughts about what the problem is?"
- **Concerns** - "Is there anything that's worrying you?"
- **Expectations** - "Is there anything that you were hoping I could do for you today?"

## Systemic enquiry
Top to toe screen of other body systems that may or may not be related. Might also gain more useful information

### Paeds Specific

#### Feeding

- What time are meal times?
- Has there been any change in normal eating habits?
- *For infants* - are they breast or bottlefed? How often and how much do they feed? Are they weened?

#### Toileting

- Any change in the amount they are weeing?
- How many wet nappies does the child have a day? Has this changed from their normal
- How often do they open their bowels

### General points
- **Systemic** - weight loss, fever
- **Neurological** - headache, sensory disturbance
- **Respiratory** - [[Pearls/Sore throat|Sore throat]], cough, SOB
- **Cardiovascular** - chest pain, palpitations
- **Musculoskeletal** - trauma, limb weakness, joint pain
- **Gastrointestinal** - _eating and drinking_, vomiting or diarrhoea, bloating
- **Genitourinary** - _passing urine and stool_, and pain or discomfort
- Dermatology - [[Pearls/Rashes|Rashes]], itching


## Past medical history

Anything that might be relevant to the presenting complaint
- Conditions
- Surgeries
- Hospital admissions
- Repeat episodes 
- New symptoms to old diseases

#### Pre-natal history

- Were there any obstetric problems, abnormal scans or screening tests?
- Did the mother take any medications during pregnancy

#### Birth history 

- Were there any concerns during delivery?
- Was the child pre-mature? What was the gestation? 
- What was their birth weight 

#### Neonatal

- Did the child require admission to the special baby unit? If so for how long? 

#### [[Pearls/Developmental Milestones|Child Development]]

- Is the child or young person meeting their developmental milestones?
- Are there any concerns about their development? 

#### Growth history

- Is the child growing along an appropriate weight and height centile? 

#### [[Pearls/Vaccination Schedule|Immunisation history]]

- Is the child/young person up to date with their immunisations? If not why? 

Ideally should all be recorded in the *red book*

## Drug history

- **Allergies** - ask if the patient has any allergies
- Prescriptions - name, dose, frequency, form, route
- Over the counter medication
- Alternative therapies that might interact

## Family history

- Ask if family members or friends have similar symptoms. 
- Are there any conditions that run in the family? 
- Any history of disease in siblings/parents/grandparents
- Suspicious deaths/deaths at a young age

## Social history

### HEADSSS

The HEEADSSS framework is useful for performing a psychosocial assessment in young people

_“Anything we talk about today is confidential. That means I cannot tell others, including your parents, about it without your permission. The only exception would be if I thought you or someone else was at risk of serious harm. In that case, I would need to tell someone else, but I would discuss this with you first”_

- **H**ome - Who lives at home? Do you have your own room? Do you fight with anyone at home? Who do you turn to when upset?
- **E**ducation and employment - Do you go to school/college? What subjects do you enjoy? Do you have a job? What sort of hours do you work? Who are your friends at school? Does anyone bully you?
- **A**ctivities - What do you do in your spare time? How do you relax? What do you like todo with friends?
- **D**rugs and alcohol - Some people your age try smoking, alcohol and drugs, is that something you’ve experienced? How much/often? Why? Where do you get the money? Does it cause you any problems? Are you interested in cutting down?
- **S**exual activity, sexuality, and gender identity - Are you seeing anyone at the moment? Is that relationship with a boy or a girl? Some people your age start having sex, have you ever had sex? **How old is the partner?** How do you handle intimate relationships?  Do you feel pressure to go along with things that you’d rather not do?
- **S**uicide and self-harm - How is your mood at the moment? Do you ever feel sad or stressed? What do you do about that? What causes that? Have you ever thought about hurting yourself? Have you acted on those thoughts? Have you thought about ending your life?
- **S**afety - Do you ever feel unsafe? - Does anyone put pressure on you to do things you don’t want to do? - Is there anyone you can talk to about these things?

#### Social services involvement

Ask if the child or young person is currently under the care of **social services** or has previously had social services involvement:

- _“Do you have a social worker?”_
- _“Have you ever had input from social services?”_

#### Travel History

- Have you been out of the country recently?
- Did you get any travel vaccinations
## Closing the consultation

- **Summarize** the key points back to the patient
- **Ask** if they have any questions or concerns
- **Thank the patient** for their time.
- **Dispose of PPE** appropriately and **wash your hands**.
