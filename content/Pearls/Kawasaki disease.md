Kawasaki disease is a rare vasculitis mainly seen in children under 5-years-old. It is classified by a **fever** which is present for 5 days or more along with 4 of the following features:

- Oropharyngeal changes - strawberry tongue
- Changes in the peripheries such as oedema or **peeling**.
- Bilateral nonpurulent conjunctivitis
- Polymorphic rash.
- Cervical lymphadenopathy.
- Dry cracked lips


Treatment with high dose aspirin (cardio-protective) $\pm$ IV Ig

### Risk

Coronary artery aneurysms associated with Kawasaki disease are a major source of morbidity and mortality and should be screened for in all patients with a confirmed diagnosis

Treatment should be completed within 10 days for reducing risk
