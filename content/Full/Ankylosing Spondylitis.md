 Inflammatory condition affecting the axial skeleton (mainly spine and sacroiliac joint) causing stiffness and pain
## Causes/Factors

- Strong link with the HLA-B27 gene - necessary (90% of cases have the gene) but not sufficient (not all people with the gene get AK)
- Typical presentation of an 20 y/o male 

## Symptoms

- Pain and stiffness in the lower back
- Sacroiliac pain

The pain and stiffness are worse in the mornings & night with improvement on movement. Stiffness takes at least 30 mins


Additional symptoms and problems include:
- **_Chest pain_** related to the **_costovertebral_** and **_sternocostal_** joints
- **_Enthesitis_** (inflammation of the **_entheses_**, where tendons or ligaments insert into bone)
- **_Dactylitis_** (inflammation of the entire finger)
- **_Vertebral fractures_** (presenting with sudden-onset new neck or back pain)
- **_Shortness of breath_** relating to restricted chest wall movement)


## Associated Conditions

- [[Full/Uveitis|Uveitis]]
- [[Full/Aortic regurgitation|Aortic regurgitation]]
- Atrioventricular block
- Apical lung fibrosis
- [[Pearls/Anaemia|Anaemia]] of chronic disease

## Investigations

- CRP and ESR
- HLA-B27 gene testing
- XR spine and sacrum
- MRI spine (looking for bone marrow oedema in early disease)


##### Schober’s Test
- Basically make the patient touch their toes with hand on their sacral/lumbar spine and see if the fingers spread out
- Should extend by like 5 cm


##### XR Changes
"Bamboo spine" is the typical XR findings - fusion of the sacroiliac and spinal joints 

X-rays in ankylosing spondylitis can show:
- **_Squaring_** of the vertebral bodies
- **_Subchondral sclerosis_** and **_erosions_**
- **_Syndesmophytes_** (areas of bone growth where the ligaments insert into the bone)
- **_Ossification_** of the ligaments, discs and joints (these structures start turning into bone)
- **_Fusion_** of the **_facet_**, **_sacroiliac_** and **_costovertebral_** joints


## Management

- NSAIDs first line
- Anti-TNF second line
- Monoclonal antibodies against interleukin-17 third line 
- JAK inhibitors another third line option

**_Intra-articular steroid injections_** may be considered for specific joints.

Additional management:
- Physiotherapy
- Exercise and mobilisation
- Avoiding smoking
- Bisphosphonates for osteoporosis
- Surgery is occasionally required for severe joint deformity
