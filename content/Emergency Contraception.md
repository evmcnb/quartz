Two oral methods currently available in the UK:

**Levonorgestrel** (a progestone) - 1.5mg single dose
- Must be taken within 72 hours on unprotected sexual intercourse (UPSI)
	- May be offered after this period as long as patient is aware of reduced effectiveness and unlicensed indication
- Dose should be doubled for those with BMI >26 or weigh over 70kg
- If vomiting occurs within 3 hours then the dose should be repeated
- Can be used more than once in the menstrual cycle
- Hormonal contraception can be started immediately afterwards

**Ulipristal** (a progesterone receptor modulator) - 30mg single dose
- Must be taken no later than 120 hours after intercourse 
- May reduce the effectiveness of hormonal contraception - **contraception with the pill should be started/restarted 5 days afterwards**
- Caution in patients with severe asthma
- Can be used more than once in the same cycle
- Breastfeeding should be delayed for one week

##### Copper IUD
- Most effective method of emergency contraception and should be offered to all women if they meet the criteria
- Must be inserted within 5 days of UPSI or up to 5 days after the likely ovulation date
- Should be kept until start of next period
