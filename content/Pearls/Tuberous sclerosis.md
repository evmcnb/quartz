An autosomal dominant condition (TSC1/2 genes). Causes non-cancerous tumors to grow in the brain, skin, kidneys, heart and lungs. 

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260327140932.png|488]]

Similar to [[Pearls/Neurofibromatosis]], the majority of features seen are neurocutaneous:
- Depigmented 'ash-leaf' spots which fluoresce under UV 

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260327135909.png|459]]

- Roughened patches of skin over lumbar spine (Shagreen patches)

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260327140013.png|496]]

- Adenoma sebaceum (angiofibromas) - butterfly distribution over nose

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260327140134.png|446]]

- Periungual fibromas  

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20260327140221.png|381]]


### Features

- Developmental delay
- [[Pearls/Epilepsy|Epilepsy]], [[Pearls/West syndrome|Infantile spasms]]
- Retinal hamartomas (dense white areas on retina)
- Rhabdomyomas of the heart
- [[Full/Polycystic Kidney Disease|Polycystic Kidney Disease]]
- Lymphangioleiomyomatosis
