## Causes/Factors

- Increasing age
- HTN
- hyperlipidaemia
- [[Diabetes Mellitus]]
- high IOP

## Symptoms

- Sudden painless loss of vision or blurriness
- Haemorrhages and oedema
- RADP

## Signs

- Multiple haemorrhages on fundoscopy "pizza pie"

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20240416101952.png]]

## Investigations

- Fluorescein angiography
- OCT-images
- FBC, ESR, glucose, BP

## Management

- Intra-vitreal steroids to treat macular oedema 
- anti-VEGF to prevent/treat neovascularization
