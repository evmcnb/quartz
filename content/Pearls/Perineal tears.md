| Severity      | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Management                    |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| First degree  | Superficial damage with no muscle involvement                                                                                                                                                                            | Does not require any repair   |
| Second degree | Injury to the perineal muscle, not involving the anal sphincter                                                                                                                                                          | Requires suturing on the ward |
| Third degree  | Injury to the perineum involving the anal sphincter complex (external anal sphincter, EAS and internal anal sphincter, IAS)<br>- 3a: less than 50% of EAS thickness torn<br>- 3b: more than 50% of EAS<br>- 3c: IAS torn | Requires repair in theatre    |
| Fourth degree | Injury to the anal sphincter complex (EAS and IAS) and rectal mucosa                                                                                                                                                     | Requires repair in theatre    |

Risk factors:
- Primigravida
- Macrosomia
- Precipitant labour
- [[Pearls/Shoulder dystocia]]
- [[Pearls/Instrumental Delivery]]
