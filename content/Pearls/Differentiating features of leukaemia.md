* ***Acute lymphoblastic leukaemia*** is the most common leukaemia in children and associated with Down syndrome
* ***[[Pearls/Chronic lymphoblastic leukaemia|Chronic lymphoblastic leukaemia]]*** is associated with warm haemolytic [[Pearls/Anaemia|Anaemia]], Richter's transformation and smudge cells
* ***[[Pearls/Chronic myeloid leukaemia|Chronic myeloid leukaemia]]*** has 3 phases, including a long chronic phase and associated with the **Philadelphia chromosome**
* ***[[Pearls/Acute myeloid leukaemia|Acute myeloid leukaemia]]*** may result in a transformation from a **myeloproliferative disorder** and associated with **Auer rods**
