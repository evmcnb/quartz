Functional disorder - abnormal functioning of an otherwise normal bowel.
 
 Symptoms can significantly impact the patient’s life.

## Causes/Factors

- Women
- Younger adults

## Symptoms

**I**ntestinal discomfort
**B**owel habit abnormalities
**S**tool abnormalities 

- Abdo pain
- Diarrhoea
- Constipation
- Fluctuating bowel habit
- Bloating
- Worse after eating
- Improved after opening bowels
- Passing mucus

Can be worsened by:
- Anxiety & depression
- Stress & sleep disturbances
- Foods, caffeine, alcohol 

## DDx

It is important to ***exclude serious underlying pathology***. The top differentials to keep in mind are:
- [[Full/Bowel cancer|Bowel cancer]]
- [[Full/inflammatory bowel disease|inflammatory bowel disease]]
- [[Full/Coeliac Disease|Coeliac Disease]]
- [[Pearls/Ovarian Tumours|Ovarian Tumours]]
- [[Pearls/Pancreatic cancer|Pancreatic cancer]]

## Investigations

Thorough history and exam needed to exclude the red flag symptoms.
- FBC - [[Pearls/Anaemia|Anaemia]]
- Inflammatory markers (CRP, ESR)
- Coeliac serology - anti-TTG
- Faecal calprotectin - for IBD
- CA125 for ovarian cancer

> [!warning] NICE Diagnostic Criteria
> Differentials need to be excluded and the patient should have 6 months of abdominal pain/discomfort with 1+ of:
> - Pain or discomfort relieved by opening the bowels
> - Bowel habit abnormalities
> - Stool abnormalities
> 
> And at least 2+ of:
> - Straining 
> - Bloating
> - Worse after eating
> - Passing mucous
> 

## Management

**Lifestyle advice:**
- Drinking enough fluids
- Regular small meals
- Adjusting fibre intake according to symptoms (more fibre if predominantly constipated, less with diarrhoea/bloating) 
- Limit caffeine, alcohol and fatty foods
- Low **_FODMAP_** diet, guided by a dietician
- **_Probiotic_** supplements may be considered over-the-counter (discontinuing after 12 weeks if there is no benefit)
- Reduce stress where possible
- Regular exercise

**Medications:**
- Loperamide
- Ispaghula husk (avoid lactulose due to bloating)
- Antispasmodics for cramps - mebeverine, alverine, hyoscine butylbromide or peppermint oil

**All else fails**
- TCAs (e.g. amitriptyline)
- SSRIs
- CBT
