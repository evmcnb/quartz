Commonest cause of attendance at GU clinics. Caused by many varieties of HPV, especially types 6 &  11. Other types cause [[Full/Cervical Cancer|Cervical Cancer]]. 

Spread via skin to skin contact with friction 

Features: 
- Small (2-5) fleshy protuberances which are slightly pigmented
- May bleed or itch

Clinical diagnosis

**Management**

Offer STI screening

The majority clear without intervention within 1-2 years. But usually too long for more patients - combination of treatments usually used. 
- Topical podophyllum for multiple non-keratinised warts 
- Cryotherapy for solitary keratinised warts
- Imiquimod cream second line
