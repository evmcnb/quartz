Causes of an acute ***painful red eye***include:

* Acute angle-closure glaucoma
* Anterior uveitis
* Scleritis
* Corneal abrasions or ulceration
* Keratitis
* Foreign body
* Traumatic or chemical injury

Causes of an acute ***painless red eye***include:

* Conjunctivitis
* Episcleritis
* Subconjunctival haemorrhage
