Fat embolism can occur following the fracture of long bones. Fat globules are released into the circulation following a fracture (possibly from the bone marrow). These globules may become lodged in blood vessels. 

Trauma + petechial rash $\rightarrow$ fat embolus

Triad of symptoms:  
- Respiratory - low O2
- Neurological - confusion
- Petechial rash (tends to occur after the first 2 symptoms)
