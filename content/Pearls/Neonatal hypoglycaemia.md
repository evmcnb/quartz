Transient hypoglycaemia in the first hours after birth is common within the first 24 hrs of life. 

- Maternal labetalol use
- preterm birth (< 37 weeks)
- maternal diabetes mellitus
- IUGR
- hypothermia
- neonatal sepsis
- inborn errors of metabolism

Management depends on the severity of the hypoglycaemia and if the newborn is symptomatic  
- asymptomatic
    - encourage normal feeding (breast or bottle)
    - monitor blood glucose
- symptomatic or very low blood glucose (<1 mmol/L)
    - admit to the neonatal unit
    - intravenous infusion of 10% dextrose ~2ml/kg
