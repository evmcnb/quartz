title: Investigating  Metastatic Disease of Unknown Primary
date: 2023-09-27
date modified: 2025-04-23
---

NICE recommends the following investigations for all patients:  
- FBC, U&E, LFT, calcium, urinalysis, LDH
- Chest X-ray
- CT of chest, abdomen and pelvis
- AFP and hCG

Plus more specific investigations if some idea.
