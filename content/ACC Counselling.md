## NIV Counselling Station

- ICE early as might be distressed with everything
- Explain it helps push air into the lungs to reduce the work of breathing and clear carbon dioxide
- May help reduce the need for ventilation with a tube
- It will be very noisy and slightly uncomfortable to begin with, but should get used to the air
- It may feel quite claustrophobic, chance of blowing wind into the stomach
- Chunk and check


## [[Pearls/Pre-Operative Assessment and Care|Pre-Operative Assessment and Care]] 

- Previous anaesthetics - general? nerve block? 
	- Any problems with that anaesthetic? high temperature or sux apnoea. Head/neck trauma?
	- Did they say you had a difficult airway?
	- Did they require ICU afterwards due to the anaesthetic?
	- Any N&V afterwards?
- PMH - specifically cardio (angina, exercise tolerance, MI, ICDs, HF, hypertension), resp (asthma, COPD, recent infections), diabetes, epilepsy, TIA/stroke, renal/liver disease
- DH - **allergies**, what are they currently taking? What would they usually be taking and have now stopped? 
- SH - smoking, alcohol, other substances (cocaine)
- Fasting & reflux. Last time ate or drank? Do they get heart burn? 
	- 2 hours - clear fluids (not fizzy or with sediment)
	- 4 hours - breastmilk
	- 6 hours - solid food (simple)
	- Tablets - take with a small sip of water at any time
- Airway assessment - Mallampati grade (tongue position when mouth open), lose caps or crowns?, neck movement
- ASA grade 
- Indicate which pre-operative tests wanted - +/- FBC, U&E, ECG 

## Anaesthetic Explanation 


### General

- Brief anesthetics history - previous, high risk factors
- Explanation of GA/what happens: into room, cannula, drugs, off to sleep, tube in for breathing, waking up in recovery
- Common risk of sore throat, PONV, shivery, feeling a bit disorientated
- Rare but more serious of damage to teeth, allergic reaction, aspiration, nerve damage (if doing block)
- Awareness is 'a never event' and is extremely rare. We have monitors and measures to measure and prevent it (EEG monitors depending on the anaesthetic)
- Will prescribe anti-sickness medications throughout 
- Can use a guard for capped teeth
