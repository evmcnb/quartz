* Tinnitus, anxiety, diaphoresis, hyperventilation 
* Respiratory alkalosis followed by metabolic acidosis
* Give activated charcoal & intravenous sodium bicarbonate

Indications for haemodialysis in salicylate overdose  
- serum concentration > 700mg/L
- metabolic acidosis resistant to treatment
- acute renal failure
- pulmonary oedema
- seizures
- coma
