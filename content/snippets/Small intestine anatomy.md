## Duodenum 4 parts:

* Wraps around head of pancreas
* Supplied by celiac trunk, SMA, sup, mid & inferior pancreaticoduodenal artery

**1st part - superior**

* only intraperitoneal part
* hepatoduodenal ligament + greater omentum attachment

**2nd part - descending**

* contains major (+ minor) duodenal papilla
  + hepatopancreatic sphincter

**3rd part - transverse, 4th ascending**

* 4th part forms duodenojejunal flexure
* this flexure attachment to the posterior abdominal wall via ligament of Treitz

## Jejunum

* entirely intraperitoneal
* no clear line between jej. + ileum ~2/3 of continuem
* lots of branches of SMA
* wall of jejunum is thicker and lumen wider
* jejunum contains more prominent circular folds of Kerckring

## Ileum

* Longest part
* Form ileoceal function, laminar musculum of ileum protrudes to form fold
* SMA supply too
