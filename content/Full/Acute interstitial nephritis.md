A subset of tubulointerstitial nephritis

Type IV hypersensitivity reaction against tubules/interstitium

Causes: **N**o **PP** **R**iding **F**or **A**nyone  
- NSAIDs  
- Penicillin  
- PPI  
- Rifampicin  
- Furosemide  
- Allopurinol

- systemic disease: SLE, sarcoidosis, and Sjogren's syndrome
- infection: Hanta virus , staphylococci

Features  
- fever, **rash**, arthralgia
- eosinophilia
- mild renal impairment
- hypertension

Investigations
- sterile pyuria
- white cell casts

### Tubulointerstitial Nephritis with Uveitis

- Typically occurs in young females
- Fever, weight loss, painful, red eyes ([[Full/Uveitis|Uveitis]])
- Urinalysis is positive for leukocytes and protein
