Emergency cause of [[snippets/Sore throat|Sore throat]] - life threatening.
Notifiable disease

## Causes/Factors

- H. influenzae type b the most common cause
- Other causes include strep pneumoniae and Group A Strep

## Symptoms

- Very acute onset
- Fever
- Stridor

## Signs

- Drooling
- Muffled voice

## Diagnostic Tests

**Do not examine the throat of anyone with suspected epiglottitis**

## Management

- immediate senior involvement- anaesthesia and ENT
- A-E- secure airway
    - endotracheal intubation
- [[snippets/Oxygen|Oxygen]]
- nebulised adrenaline
- IV antibiotics
- IV steroids
- fluid resus
