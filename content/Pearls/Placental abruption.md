Separation of a normally sited placenta from the uterine wall resulting in internal maternal haemorrhage.

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20251229163237.png]]


Risk factors: 
- [[Full/Pre-eclampsia]]
- Cocaine use
- Multiparity
- Maternal trauma
- Increasing maternal age

Clinical features:
- shock out of keeping with visible loss
- constant pain
- tender tense uterus
- normal lie and presentation
- fetal heart: absent/distressed
- coagulation problems 

### Management: 

- Fetus alive and distressed: **immediate C-section**
- Fetus alive, not distressed and < 36 weeks: admit, observe, give steroids, threshold to deliver depends on gestation
- Fetus alive, not distressed and > 36 weeks: deliver vaginally 
- Fetus dead, induce vaginal delivery

#### Complications

Maternal  
- shock
- DIC
- renal failure
- PPH

  
Fetal  
- IUGR
- hypoxia
- death

  
Prognosis  
- associated with high perinatal mortality rate
- responsible for 15% of perinatal deaths
