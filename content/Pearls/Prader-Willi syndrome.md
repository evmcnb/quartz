Example of genetic imprinting, the phenotype depends on the deletion of the paternal or maternal gene:
- Prader-Willi syndrome if gene deleted from father
- [[Pearls/Angelman Syndrome]] if gene deleted from mother

This may be due to:  
- microdeletion of paternal 15q11-13 (70% of cases)
- maternal uniparental disomy of chromosome 15


Features  
- **hypotonia during infancy**
- dysmorphic features
- short stature
- hypogonadism and infertility
- learning difficulties
- **childhood obesity**
- behavioural problems in adolescence
- [[Pearls/Neonate sepsis|Neonate sepsis]]
