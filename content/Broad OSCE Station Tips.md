#### Hx Station

1. Don't forget ICE early - will often pull out information from actors
2. Red flags - always have a list
3. Social history early too as this often is where the twist is hidden
4. If getting lost take a moment to summarize - "if there's anything you feel like I've missed please let me know"

#### Explanation Station

**Condition**
1. History taking is not the aim, rule out red flags and move on
2. Check current understanding of diagnosis/condition/result
3. Chunk and check throughout
4. Sign post explanation - what we are going to talk about... what causes it, how we manage it and places of support
5. What is it - lay language to explain underlying pathophysiology
6. What causes it - split into modifiable and fixed
7. Management - split into lifestyle and medication
8. Places of support
9. Summarize at the end

**Medication**

Same beginning:
5. Condition summary – you are taking this for x, x is...
6. What the mediation is/how it help- the medication is called x, it is a tablet and it aims to improve/help/stop
7. How to take it- this includes things such as inhaler technique
8. How often/when- is it everyday, is it best had in the morning, on a full stomach, ect
9. How to know it’s working- reduction in symptoms or if something like BP we can see in clinic it is now controlled
10. Side effects- for each medicine have some side effects and what to do e.g. thrush for inhalers so much your teeth after
11. How long- lifelong, weeks, months, etc
12. Summarize at the end
