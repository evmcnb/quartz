Autosomal recessive condition affecting the cilia on the surface of cells.
- Mucus build up in the lungs & sinuses
- Cilia in fallopian tubes and sperm tails - reduced or absent fertility

Presenting features of: 
- Neonatal respiratory distress
- Recurrent chest infections
- Impaired growth 
- Bronchiectasis
- Chronic sinusitis
- Recurrent otitis media


Presents in a similar way to [[Full/Cystic Fibrosis|Cystic Fibrosis]] 

If the patient also has Situs inversus, they have [[Pearls/Kartagener's Syndrome|Kartagener's Syndrome]]. Basically the same thing just plus situs inversus. 

**Management**
- Regular chest physiotherapy
- Antibiotics - azithromycin
