Rare kidney disorder of the proximal tubule causing lots of stuff to get wasted into urine: 
- glucose
- amino acids
- uric acid
- phosphate
- bicarbonate

It leads to dehydration, muscle weakness, and bone pain (rickets in children, osteomalacia in adults).
