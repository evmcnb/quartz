Typically colonizes water tanks and hence questions may hint at air-conditioning systems or foreign holidays. No person to person transmission

Features  
- flu-like symptoms including fever (present in > 95% of patients)
- dry cough
- relative **bradycardia**
- confusion
- lymphopaenia
- **hyponatraemia**
- deranged liver function tests
- pleural effusion: seen in around 30% of patients

Ix: 
* If someone comes in with fever + cough urine is usually tested to check for legionella, easy to rule out
* CXR: mid-to-lower zone predominance of patchy consolidation

Treat with erythromycin/clarithromycin
