Affects [[Pearls/Premature Babies|Premature Babies]], particularly before 32 weeks.
Inadequate surfactant leads to high surface tension, reduced lung compliance and atelectasis. 

XR shows a ground glass appearance
![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20251218153705.png]]

**Management**
IM betamethasone given to mothers with suspected or confirmed preterm labour increases surfactant production and reduces the incidence and severity of RDS

Neonates may need:
- Endotracheal surfactant (artificial delivered into the lungs)
- Supplementary oxygen
- CPAP
- Intubation/ventilation 

Support with breathing is gradually stepped down as the baby develops and can maintain breathing on their own
