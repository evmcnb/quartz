A term used when urethritis occurs without identification of gonococcal bacteria on initial swab

In this case, the patient requires immediate treatment prior to waiting for the [[Pearls/Chlamydia]] test to come back 

No cause is found in half of causes.
- [[Pearls/Chlamydia]] - most common cause
- Mycoplasma genitalium - causes more symptoms

Possible patients are infected with both

#### Mycoplasma genitalium

NAAT testing to look for DNA/RNA
- Check every positive sample for macrolide resistance - perform "test of cure" after treatment in every positive patient
- Doxycycline 100mg BD for 7 days
- Azithromycin 1g STAT then 500mg OD for 2 days (unless known to be resistance to macrolides)
- Azithromycin alone used in pregnancy
