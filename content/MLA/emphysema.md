Defined histologically as enlarged air spaces distal to terminal bronchi with destruction of alveoli Over time, the inner walls of the air sacs weaken and rupture — creating larger air spaces instead of many small ones

- irreversible

Emphysema and chronic [[MLA/bronchitis]] are two conditions that make up chronic obstructive pulmonary disease ([[MLA/COPD]])

## Causes/Factors

- Tobacco/marijuana smoke
- Air pollution
- Chemical fumes & dust

## Symptoms

- Gradual onset dyspnea
- Productive cough
- Frequent chest infections
- Wheeze

## Signs

- Tachypnoea

## Diagnostic Tests

[[MLA/COPD#Diagnostic tests]]

## Management

Same as [[MLA/COPD#Management]]

- Stop smoking

## Complications/red Flags

- [[MLA/pneumothorax]]
- [[MLA/cor pulmonale]]
