Common disease caused by human herpes virus 6. Incubation period of 5-15 days, typically affects children 6 months - 2 years

Features:
- high fever lasting a few days followed by:
- maculopapular rash
- Febrile convulsions in 10-15% - most common complication
- Diarrhoea and cough
- Nagayama spots

School exclusion is not needed
