**Parietal peritoneum** - layers lining the surface walls

**Visceral peritoneum** - layer that covers a larger continuous layer like pleura

**Retroperitoneal** - situated outside the peritoneum in contact with body wall

**Intraperitoneal** - suspended from the abdo wall by a double layered fold of peritoneum

| Intraperitoneal organs | **Secondary retroperitoneal (lost a mesentery)** | Primary retroperitoneal (never had a mesentery) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| * Stomach * Liver * Gallbladder * Spleen * Duodenum 1st part * Tail of pancreas * Jejunum * Ileum * Appendix * Transverse colon * Sigmoid colon | * Duodenum 2nd + 3rd part * Head, neck & body of pancreas * Ascending colon * Descending colon * Upper rectum | * Kidneys * Adrenal glands * Ureters * Aorta * IVC * Lower rectum |
