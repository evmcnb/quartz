Benign inversion of columnar endocervical epithelium onto the ectocervix. Physiological finding and not associated with cervical malignancy. 

Driven strongly by high oestrogen - [[Pearls/Combined oral contraceptive pill|Combined oral contraceptive pill]] can be a cause.

Need to rule out cervical infection before diagnosis
- Ensure cervical screening is up to date
- Chlamydia screen, consider other [[Pearls/Sexually Transmitted Infection Treatments|Sexually Transmitted Infection Treatments]]
