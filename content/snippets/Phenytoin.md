| **Drug Class** | Anti-convulsant |
| --- | --- |
| **Mechanism** | Alters transmembrane movement of Na+/K+ by blocking voltage-gated Na+ channels - prevents spread of epileptic discharges |
| **Typical Dose** | 3-4mg/kg daily |
| **Indications** | * [[Snippets/Epilepsy|Epilepsy]] (all except absence seizure) * Trigeminal neuralgia |
| **Contra-indications** | Acute porphyrias |
| **Side-effects** | Common: dizziness, headache, confusion, insomnia, nausea, vomiting, hirsutism  Rarely: gum hypertrophy, rash, Stevens-Johnson syndrome, drug-induced SLE |
| **Interactions** | Induces hepatic drug-metabolising enzymes:   * [[Snippets/Amiodarone|Amiodarone]], [[Snippets/Aspirin|Aspirin]], cimetidine, diltazem, nifedipine * OCP - increased metabolism of OCP * Rifampicin - plasma concentration of phenytoin * [[Snippets/Warfarin|Warfarin]] - increased metabolism of [[Snippets/Warfarin|Warfarin]] |
