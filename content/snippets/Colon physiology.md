## Bacterial digestion

Fibre \(\rightarrow H\_2 + CH\_4 + \text{short chain fatty acids}\)

Urea + amino acids \(\rightarrow NH\_4\)

Bilirubin → urobilirogen

Cystine + methionine \(\rightarrow H\_2S\)

Bile acid fermentation \(\rightarrow N\_2 + H\_2 + CO\_2\)

## Na+/water absorption

* Stimulated by aldosterone
* Cl moves paracellularly (secreted in small intestine)
* Na+ 2nd active transport
* Creates osmotic gradient for water

* K+ can undergo secretion + absorption in the colon - **usually net secretion**
* Balance determined by plasma [K+], aldosterone + cAMP
* People with diarrhoea can become hypokalemic

## Motility

Slow contractions squeeze contents backwards and forwards as a segment - **haustration**

A few times a day becomes organised into peristatic wave - **mass movement**

Haustra disappear during mass movement then reform
