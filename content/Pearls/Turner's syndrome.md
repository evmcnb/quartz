It is caused by either the presence of only one sex chromosome (X) or a deletion of the short arm of one of the X chromosomes. Turner's syndrome is denoted as 45,XO or 45,X.

Features: 
- Short stature
- Primary amenorrhea 
- webbed neck
- widely-spaced nipples
- low posterior hairline 
- shield-shaped chest

- aortic dilatation and dissection - **greatest life-threatening complication**. Regular monitoring is key
- Coarctation of the aorta (systolic murmur) -  *The aorta does a sharp TURN in turners*
- Bicuspid aortic valve
- Horse-shoe kidneys 
- Increased risk of [[Pearls/The Hemophilias|The Hemophilias]] due to genetics
- high arched palate


**Somatotropin**, or recombinant human growth hormone can help with growth
