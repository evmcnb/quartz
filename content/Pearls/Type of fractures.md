**Compound -**when the skin is broken by the [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]]

**Stable** - when the seconds of [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] remain in alignment at the fracture

Terms used to describe in what way a [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] breaks

* **Transverse** - A fracture that crosses the [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] at a straight line.
* **Oblique** - A fracture that crosses the [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] at an angled line.
* **Spiral** - A fracture that spirals around the [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]].
* **Segmental** - When there are multiple fracture lines resulting in the [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] breaking into segments.
* **Comminuted** - When the [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] breaks into multiple fragments.
* **Compression fractures** - Specifically refer to fractures affecting the vertebrae in the spine.
* **Greenstick** - Typically occur in children rather than adults and involve a partial fracture where one side of the [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] bends *without* completely breaking.
* **Buckle (torus)** - Another fracture more common in children, where the [[Pearls/Bone|Bone]] bends into a buckled shape under compression.
* **Salter-Harris** - Refers only to fractures occurring through a child's growth plate, classified using the Salter-Harris system.
