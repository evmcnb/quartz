Defective bone mineralisation causes "soft" bones due to insufficient vitamin D. The same process causes rickets in children.

Vitamin D is created from cholesterol by the skin (UV light dependant). Diet has some vit D but not enough.

Risks for low vitamin D

* Malabsorption disorders - IBS
* Chronic kidney disease - kidneys add the second hydroxy group

![](https://nosos-static.s3.amazonaws.com/media/uploads/2024/03/04/image.png)

***Serum 25-hydroxyvitamin D:***

* Less than 25 nmol/L – ***vitamin D deficiency***
* 25 to 50 nmol/L – ***vitamin D insufficiency***

***Treatment***

*Symptomatic patients:*

* 50,000 IU once weekly for 6 weeks
* 4000 IU daily for 10 weeks

Maintenance dose of 800-2000 IU per day in continued

Check serum calcium within a month of loading regime. It may be:

1. Low in calcium deficiency =
2. High in primary hyperparathyroidism previously masked by the vitamin D deficiency
3. High in other conditions that cause hypercalcaemia, cancer, sarcoidosis, or TB
