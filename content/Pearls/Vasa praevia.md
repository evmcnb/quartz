A condition where the fetal vessels are within the fetal membranes and travel across the interna;l cervical os. 

![[z_attachments/Pasted image 20251229162706.png]]

Risk factors: 
- Low lying placenta
- IVF pregnancy
- Multiple pregnancy

May be diagnosed via:
- Ultrasound 
- Antepartum haemorrhage 
- Vaginal exam - pulsitile vessels 
- During labour - carries a very high fetal mortality even with emergency C-section

Presents with
- Painless vaginal bleeding
- Less fetal movements
- Fetal bradycardia 

Elective C-section at 34-36 weeks - prepare fetal lungs with corticosteroids at 32 weeks
