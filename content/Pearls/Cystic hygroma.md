Rare congenital benign tumour of the lymphatic system. Most common in the cervical-fascial region (posterior triangle of the neck)

Associated with:
- [[Pearls/Turner's syndrome|Turner's syndrome]]
- [[Pearls/Fetal alcohol syndrome]]
- [[Pearls/Infections in Pregnancy|Viral illness in Pregnancy]]

Features of the mass:
- More common on the left side
- Soft and compressible
- Not painful
- No movement on swallowing
- Transillumination
- May interfere with respiration or compromise the airway 

Need to consider and exclude other malignancy with USS CT or MRI

Surgical removal
